Meta says [Muse is now available in the UK](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/11/introducing-muse-in-the-uk-a-personal-ai-agent-you-control/). The Newsroom post is dated **November 23** and was updated **November 24**. It describes Muse as a personal AI agent "that you can task to get things done on your behalf," and it says the service is available "from today."

[The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1041084/metas-muse-agent-launches-in-the-uk) also reported the UK launch on November 24 at 3:46 a.m. CT.

This is a launch of **Meta's Muse in the UK**. It is not a confirmation that Walmart's Muse shopping integration is available there. We explain that distinction below.

## Where you can use it, and who it's for

According to Meta's post, Muse is available to **anyone aged 18 and over in the UK** on **iOS, Android, [muse.ai](https://muse.ai), and WhatsApp**. Meta says it is "coming to Meta's AI glasses in the months ahead."

Meta says Muse is "free to use for most of what people need, with subscription plans for those who want to do even more." The post does not list plan prices, and we are not adding any.

You can chat with Muse in the app or in WhatsApp, Meta says. The post also describes a voice for "natural, real-time conversations," with "hundreds" of voice options, "including a range of British accents," and a feature called **spaces** for custom trackers, planners, or boards inside the app.

## UK and global partners

Meta says Muse is launching with "popular global Connectors," including **Spotify** and **OpenTable**, and with UK connector partners that include:

Marks & Spencer, Debenhams Group, Currys, Ocado Retail, Trainline, Argos, Sainsbury's, ASOS, Jet2, LOOKFANTASTIC, MyProtein, Cult Beauty, JustPark, easyJet, and JD Sports.

Meta's wording is careful about what those connections do. "With some, you can connect your personal account so Muse can reorder your usual shop or pull up a booking. With others, Muse can find its way around their websites from day one." The post does **not** say which partners fall into which group, so we are not assigning them.

PayPal, Meta says, is "coming soon."

Shruti Dube, Meta's UK Country Director, is quoted as saying: "I'm thrilled that millions of Brits will now have access to a personal agent that they control and that gets things done on their behalf. Partnering with beloved British brands and retailers means Muse works seamlessly with services people here use every day, making everything from the weekly shop to sorting out their travel needs easier."

## What Meta says Muse can do, and the controls around it

Meta says Muse is powered by **Muse Spark**, which it calls "the first model from Meta Superintelligence Labs, built for real-world agentic work." With the user's oversight, the post says, Muse can send email, book travel, keep working after the app is closed, and ask for approval "before it sends an email or makes a purchase."

For payments, Meta says Muse can check out with **Onelink built by Stripe**, which "generates a unique, digital version of your card that's only accessible to agents using Onelink," and with **Shop Pay from Shopify** at supported stores.

On privacy and control, Meta says:

- You choose which Connectors Muse can use, and you can add or disconnect a service in settings.
- Muse "asks for your go-ahead before important actions, like sending an email or making a purchase."
- "Your conversations with Muse, and the data in your virtual machine, aren't shared with Meta's advertising systems."
- You can decide whether Meta may use Muse interactions to train and improve AI, and you can switch that off in Settings.
- "Muse has no visibility into people's passwords or payment methods."
- Every Muse "gets its own virtual computer in the cloud," and "no one else's Muse can access" that information.

Those are Meta's claims. We have not independently tested the UK product.

## What this is not: Walmart's U.S. Muse shopping beta

On **November 23**, [The Verge reported](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1039874/walmart-agent-gateway-meta-muse-black-friday) that Walmart was rolling out its **Agent Gateway** with Meta's Muse, so the agent can find and buy items through a Walmart account. Walmart later told The Verge the launch would proceed that same day, November 23, after an earlier delay notice. The Verge described Muse shopping through Walmart "Agent Profiles," with spending limits and checkout on Walmart.com using a card saved in the Walmart account.

**We found no official Walmart or Meta announcement that this Walmart shopping integration is available in the UK.** Walmart is not named among the UK connector partners in Meta's UK launch post. Until Walmart or Meta says otherwise, the UK story is Muse itself, with the British retailers and travel brands Meta listed, not Walmart shopping.

*Our take (opinion):* the two November 23 stories are easy to mash together, but they are not the same product moment. Muse arriving in the UK is on the record from Meta. Walmart letting Muse shop on Walmart.com is a separate, U.S.-centered beta.
