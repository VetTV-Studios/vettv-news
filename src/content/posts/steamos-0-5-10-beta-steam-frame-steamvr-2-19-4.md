Valve [posted SteamOS 0.5.10 Beta for Steam Frame](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4165890/view/694278269818634528) on November 19 at 1:12 p.m. CT, and [SteamVR Beta 2.19.4](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/250820/view/683019270749159762) fourteen minutes later, at 1:26 p.m. CT. Both updates are still in beta.

On Steam Frame, Valve says, "This update is for the Steam Frame Beta channel, and includes new features that are still being tested." You can opt in under Settings > System > System Update Channel. Anyone can opt into the SteamVR beta; Valve points to its [SteamVR beta instructions](https://help.steampowered.com/en/faqs/view/4F5E-AD22-7402-2EAD).

## SteamOS 0.5.10 on Steam Frame

The notes lead with image quality. Valve "Improved streaming decode performance, allowing for higher default streaming resolutions," and "Added global display sharpness control (defaulting to ON)." Users can tune it "in VR Settings > Display > Display Sharpness." The same list "Reduced 'green tint' on high frequency greyscale image content" and "Reduced head wobble when streaming remote desktops."

Under General, Valve "Fixed hang on Steam Frame logo at boot after updating the OS," "Improved Multi-Link Streaming robustness under adverse network conditions," and "Improved eye-tracking latency, particularly under lower framerates." It also "Added user preference to control haptics strength per game, in VR Settings > Controllers > Manage Controller Bindings," "Upgraded Iris video decoder driver," and "Renamed Foveated Sharpening to SGSR Sharpening."

For developers, the build "Added support for XR_KHR_convert_timespec_time to Android runtime."

## SteamVR 2.19.4 on the PC

The new Steam Link list says Valve "Improved streaming quality and reliability on both NVIDIA and AMD graphics cards," and "Improved robustness to transient network issues, including with Multi-Link Streaming." It also "Fixed skybox textures (such as VRChat's transition screens) sometimes looking wrong" and "Fixed occasional session running at a reduced bitrate."

The laptop line is specific: "On laptops with two GPUs, Steam Link now uses the high-performance GPU, unless a Windows graphics preference is set for SteamVR."

Steam Link for Meta Quest "now supports chroma key to allow Room View to be shown on VR content," with the settings "on the PC side in the Camera section." Under Linux, Valve "Fixed several crashes that affected all graphics drivers and headsets."

Elsewhere, the notes "Added user preference to turn off haptics per game, in VR Settings > Controllers > Manage Controller Bindings." "On Steam Frame over Steam Link, there is also a slider to control haptics strength."

These are betas. If you want the most stable build, stay on the default channels until Valve moves the changes out of testing.
