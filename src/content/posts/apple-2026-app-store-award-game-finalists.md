Apple [announced](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/meet-the-2026-app-store-award-finalists/) 45 finalists for this year's App Store Awards on November 18, 2026. The company's [newsroom post](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/meet-the-2026-app-store-award-finalists/) says the finalists span iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Vision Pro, Apple Watch, Apple TV, and Apple Arcade, as well as the Cultural Impact category. "Winners will be announced in the coming weeks."

"We're excited to celebrate this year's App Store Award finalists, whose creativity has brought extraordinary apps and games to life for users around the world," said Carson Oliver, Apple's vice president of App Store. "From games that transport players to imaginative new worlds and bring out the fun of playing together, to apps that empower users to learn new skills and make a difference in their communities, these developers are redefining how we connect, create, and experience the world."

The names and one-line descriptions below are copied from Apple's announcement. This piece focuses on the game categories. Apple also named app finalists for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Vision Pro, Apple Watch, Apple TV, and Cultural Impact.

## iPhone Game of the Year Finalists

- BALL x PIT, for breaking the mold of brick breakers.
- DAVE THE DIVER, for exploring the oceans deep.
- Gambonanza, for rewiring the rules of chess.

## iPad Game of the Year Finalists

- Aniimo, for forging fantasy quests with truly adorable creatures.
- Arknights: Endfield, for waging stunning battles on a faraway moon.
- Where Winds Meet, for creating cinematic martial arts moves.

## Mac Game of the Year Finalists

- Big Walk, for encouraging friends to puzzle together.
- Blue Prince, for delivering a mysterious mansion full of unexpected twists.
- Crimson Desert, for creating a sprawling war-torn fantasyland.

## Apple Arcade Game of the Year Finalists

- Perchang World, for stumping players with marble-filled physics puzzles.
- PowerWash Simulator, for making fighting grime so satisfying.
- Pusheen’s Place, for captivating users with really cute cats.

## Apple Vision Pro Game of the Year Finalists

- Cueva de las Manos, for designing one-of-a-kind puzzles that play with light and shadow.
- Ping Pong Club, for bringing smashing rounds of table tennis.
- Spatial Ops, for immersing players in first-person battles that go down right in their home.

## Cultural Impact Game Finalists

- and Roger, for telling a poignant and often challenging personal story.
- Consume Me, for grappling with the realities of eating disorders.
- PancitoMerge, for celebrating Mexico’s culinary culture.
- Sago Mini Jinja’s Garden, for giving families a way to play together.
- Spilled!, for making it fun to clean up polluted waterways.
- Tiny Bookshop, for bringing a cozy, inclusive community to life.
