NVIDIA's board has reinstated Jensen Huang as president, chief executive officer and a member of the board, [the company announced](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director) on Monday morning, November 23. The release says the change is "effective immediately" and that the board "voted unanimously" for it. The release gives a date but no time. NVIDIA's newsroom account [shared it on X](https://x.com/nvidianewsroom/status/2124865060397019480) at 7:58 a.m. CT, and [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1039937/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-reinstated) reported the news at 8:04 a.m. CT.

The return comes 11 days after NVIDIA [announced on November 12](/posts/nvidia-jensen-huang-resigns-colette-kress-interim-ceo/) that Huang had resigned and that CFO Colette Kress would serve as interim CEO.

## What the board said

According to the release, the board reinstated Huang "following a series of discussions and meetings with Mr. Huang, after which the Board was satisfied with respect to Mr. Huang's candor and ability to lead NVIDIA." It adds that the board "received input from senior levels of the United States government, and based on that input, the Board is confident that Mr. Huang's reinstatement poses no national security risk." The release does not say who in the government gave that input or what it was.

Harvey C. Jones, the board's chair, said: "The Board believes Jensen is the right person to lead NVIDIA, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers." Jones remains chair.

Huang said: "I am honored to return to NVIDIA and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a new industrial revolution." He thanked the board, Jones, Kress and the executive staff, and ended with: "I can't wait to get back to work."

## Kress continues as CFO

Jones thanked Colette Kress "for her outstanding service as interim CEO, in addition to her continuing responsibilities as NVIDIA's Chief Financial Officer." With Huang back as CEO, Kress continues in her CFO role. The release doesn't name any other change to her title.

## The investigation continues

The release says the board formed a committee of independent directors, which hired independent counsel, "in light of recent reporting that Mr. Huang has been the target of a foreign intelligence effort." The board "reiterated that the committee's investigation will proceed to conclusion," and says Huang "fully supports the investigation and is committed to cooperating with it."

NVIDIA also says:

- The board recently formed a **National Security and Export Control Committee**, made up only of independent directors, to oversee trade compliance and national security risks.
- It is starting a search for a new **Chief Compliance Officer**, who will report to the board's Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee.
- It "will continue to cooperate with ongoing government investigations and inquiries."

Our [November 12 report](/posts/nvidia-jensen-huang-resigns-colette-kress-interim-ceo/) has the background on the resignation, including Huang's November 8 staff email and how other outlets reported the reasons. The reinstatement release does not revisit those reports.

**Our take (opinion):** the board is betting that the investigation won't turn up anything that changes its mind, and it is saying so publicly before that investigation is done. What the committee finds, and who becomes Chief Compliance Officer, will be the next things to watch.
