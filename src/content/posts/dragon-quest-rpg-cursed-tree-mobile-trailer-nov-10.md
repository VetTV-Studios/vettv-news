Square Enix is teasing a new smartphone Dragon Quest title ahead of an announcement trailer. The English title used by Gematsu and related coverage is **Dragon Quest RPG: The Cursed Tree and the Chosen Isle**; Japanese coverage uses **ドラゴンクエストRPG　魔界樹と導かれし島**. Anime News Network transliterates it as *Dragon Quest RPG: Makai-Ju to Michibikareshi Shima*.

## Trailer timing

Anime News Network, citing Square Enix's YouTube channel, says the announcement trailer debuts **Tuesday at 8:00 p.m. JST** (**6:00 a.m. EST**). That is **5:00 a.m. CT**. Gematsu lists the same window as 3:00 a.m. PT / 6:00 a.m. ET / 20:00 JST on November 10.

Square Enix YouTube listings for the announcement trailer are the primary media reference for this story (Japanese premiere listing for *『ドラゴンクエストRPG　魔界樹と導かれし島』アナウンストレーラー*). The studio's Dragon Quest teaser domain was under maintenance when checked, so this draft relies on the YouTube premiere materials plus secondary confirmation of the time.

## Platforms—and what is not claimed

Coverage consistently identifies **iOS and Android** only. No PC, console, or simultaneous global-storefront launch is established by these sources. Price, monetization model, developer credit, combat systems, and a ship date are likewise unannounced in the materials reviewed.

This is a trailer tease, not a release announcement.
