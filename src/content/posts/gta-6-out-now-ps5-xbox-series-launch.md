Grand Theft Auto VI is out now in the United States on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Official [U.S. PlayStation Store](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/concept/10000730) and [Microsoft Store](https://www.xbox.com/en-us/games/store/grand-theft-auto-vi/9P3H4968GRSM/0017) listings timestamp the unlock to 2026-11-19T05:00:00Z, which is 11:00 p.m. CT on Wednesday, November 18, or 12:00 a.m. Eastern on Thursday, November 19.

That is a single nationwide U.S. store unlock, not a separate midnight in each U.S. time zone. [Rockstar Games](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak3ak31a49a221/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-set-to-launch-november-19-2026) set the launch for Thursday, November 19, 2026, on those two platforms. VetTV News laid out the confirmed times, prices and pre-load details in this morning's [launch-week preview](/posts/gta-6-launch-week-preview-countdown/).

## A rolling launch, already underway elsewhere

PlayStation Support's [U.S. page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/games/gta-vi-support/) still says the game "releases at midnight local time on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation®5 consoles." The [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/), in a November 10 post by Tim Turi of Sony Interactive Entertainment, described a "rolling midnight launch by country, beginning at 12:00AM NZDT in New Zealand and running across the globe as respective countries hit midnight on November 19."

The official store timestamps match that country-by-country reading. The [U.K. PlayStation Store](https://store.playstation.com/en-gb/product/EP1004-PPSA01547_00-GTAVISTANDARD001) listing timestamps to 2026-11-19T00:00:00Z, midnight Greenwich Mean Time, which is 6:00 p.m. CT on Wednesday. New Zealand's midnight, the first time PlayStation named, was 5:00 a.m. CT on Wednesday.

Xbox Cloud Gaming is a later, separate start. A [November 10 Xbox Wire post](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/) says the game "will be playable on day one through Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S, starting at 1am ET." That is 12:00 a.m. CT on Thursday, November 19, one hour after the U.S. store unlock. The post says cloud streams use Xbox Series S graphics settings, and that the game must be purchased separately. Xbox Wire's [November 13 "Next Week on Xbox" post](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/13/next-week-on-xbox-new-games-for-november-16-to-20/) repeats the 1:00 a.m. ET cloud start.

Xbox One is not a native platform. Native play is on Xbox Series X|S, per the [Microsoft Store product page](https://www.xbox.com/en-us/games/store/grand-theft-auto-vi/9P3H4968GRSM/0017) and Xbox Wire. The [PlayStation U.S. game page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/) says the game "will only be playable on PlayStation 5 consoles. It will not be available for PlayStation 4 consoles," and lists it as a "single-player experience." No official PC version or PC date appears on the Rockstar, PlayStation, Xbox, or Take-Two pages re-checked on November 18, 2026.

## Pre-load, storage and a required PS5 system update

Pre-loading has been open since November 10, according to [Rockstar's Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now) and [pre-load page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/pre-load-now). The [PlayStation U.S. game page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/) tells players to clear a minimum of 212 GB on a PS5. VetTV News previously reported [those official pre-load times and the 212 GB figure](/posts/gta-vi-pre-load-times-212gb-november-10/).

Physical copies contain a download code and no disc. PlayStation Support says: "Physical (Code-In-Box) versions will only contain a game download code inside the box to support pre-load. A disc will not be included in the box." Buyers who find a missing, damaged, or already-used code are told to contact the retailer, as [VetTV News reported](/posts/gta-6-boxed-download-code-missing-or-used/).

PlayStation players also need a current system update. On November 18, [PlayStation Support US](https://x.com/PS_Support_US/status/2122863683881619685) posted: "Sony Interactive has issued a PS5 system software update that is required before playing Grand Theft Auto VI. Players should download this update prior to midnight on November 19 so that they're ready to play on launch day, November 19." PlayStation's official [system software notes](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/hardware/ps5/system-software-info/) list the latest version as 26.07-14.23.00, with the note, "We've made some security fixes to the system software." PlayStation's [update guide](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/hardware/ps5/system-software/) has the install steps.

Rockstar had not posted official day-one patch notes for the game itself on the pages re-checked on November 18. Third-party reports of pre-launch game-update sizes are not treated as official here.

## Editions and U.S. prices

The Standard Edition is $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition is $99.99. Those are the prices in Take-Two's [June 24, 2026, press release](https://taketwointeractivesoftwareinc.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi/), and they were still on the official U.S. [PlayStation Store](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/concept/10000730) and [Microsoft Store](https://www.xbox.com/en-us/games/store/grand-theft-auto-vi/9P3H4968GRSM/0017) listings, including the [Ultimate Edition](https://www.xbox.com/en-us/games/store/grand-theft-auto-vi-ultimate-edition/9NNZSNHLR63L) at $99.99, when re-checked on the morning of November 18, 2026.

Pre-order bonus wording is not identical across official pages. Take-Two used "before November 20, 2026" for the Vintage Vice City Pack. Xbox Wire and the Microsoft Store product page say, "Purchase prior to 19 November 2026 23:59:59." Players should read the offer on their own store page.

## Reviews have started to appear

Reviews began appearing on Wednesday, November 18. [Luke Reilly's IGN review](https://www.ign.com/articles/grand-theft-auto-vi-review), published at 11:00 a.m. UTC (5:00 a.m. CT), scores Grand Theft Auto VI 10 out of 10. The review is subtitled "Unprecedented. Unbelievable. Unmissable," and Reilly writes, "Grand Theft Auto 6 is the single most impressive video game I have ever played."

[GameSpot's review](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/grand-theft-auto-6-review/) by Tamoor Hussain, posted at 3:00 a.m. Pacific the same morning, is accompanied by a GameSpot page titled "[Why We Gave Grand Theft Auto 6 A 10/10](https://www.gamespot.com/videos/why-we-gave-grand-theft-auto-6-a-10-10/)," which says the game "earned a 10/10 'Essential' from GameSpot."

Rockstar has not, on the official pages checked today, announced a review-embargo time. A fuller reviews roundup will follow from the published reviews themselves, using each outlet's own score and link. Aggregate scores are not included here.

## What to watch next

Xbox Cloud Gaming opens at 1:00 a.m. ET. Official game patch notes, if Rockstar posts them, will be covered separately, as will a reviews roundup and later performance news. Spectrum's temporary [gig-speed offer around the launch window](/posts/spectrum-gig-speed-lift-gta-vi-download-november-2026/) runs through November 30, according to that earlier report.

Players should confirm the unlock time and any remaining downloads on their own PlayStation or Microsoft Store account.
