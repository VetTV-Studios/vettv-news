Anthropic announced new workplace tools on **November 10, 2026**. In its product post, the company says **Claude Whiteboards** and **Claude Sheets** are launching in **beta**, and that **Claude Dashboards** is now **generally available** under the name **Claude Data**. It says those products are available on **all paid plans**, and that they work on web, desktop, and mobile. Claude Data needs a data connector such as a warehouse or CRM.

## Whiteboards, Sheets, and Data

Anthropic describes Whiteboards as a shared board where a team can brainstorm with Claude, and Sheets as a spreadsheet Claude can build and edit, including tabs, formulas, formatting, and drop-downs. For Sheets, the company says functionality is still simple, with Excel exports or Claude for Excel available when more is needed.

Claude Data, Anthropic says, can create dashboards, data stories, and narrated videos from connected company data. **Certified queries** and **live sharing** are still in **beta**. The company lists partners for Claude Data including Amplitude, Mixpanel, Omni, PostHog, and Sigma.

On Team plans, Anthropic says Whiteboards, Sheets, and Data are on by default. On Enterprise plans, Whiteboards and Sheets are off by default, and Claude Data turns on by default on **November 19, 2026**. Admins can change those settings in Organization settings > Artifacts.

## Interactive connectors

A companion Anthropic post says interactive connectors can now open as full apps beside a conversation or in full screen, on the web and in the Claude desktop app, on all plans, as accounts get the new Claude experience. Anthropic's examples of tools available in Claude today include **Adobe**, **Atlassian**, **Box**, **HubSpot**, and **Notion**. It also lists a starting group that includes Asana, Canva, Carta, Dropbox, monday.com, Smartsheet, Vercel, and Webflow.

## Claude Meetings

Anthropic's own overview says **Claude Meetings is coming soon**, and that it will let users add Claude to a call to take notes and update documents during the conversation. Anthropic's posts reviewed for this story stop at that coming-soon wording: they never say Meetings is available now, never name Google Meet, and never give a Team or Enterprise beta.

**TechCrunch**, in a November 10 report, wrote that Claude Meetings is launching in beta on Team and Enterprise plans and works with Google Meet at launch. That plan and platform detail comes from TechCrunch, not from Anthropic's announcements linked above.
