Elon Musk posted on X on **November 8, 2026** that Grok Bot fixes are in progress. The post, timed **13:17 UTC**, reads in full:

> Grok Bot fixes for improved token efficiency and smarter behavior are underway

That is the primary statement this article is built on. The post does **not** promise higher weekly limits, a price change, a free usage reset, or a delivery date.

## What led up to the post

Earlier the same day, Musk replied to a usage thread with, “I will dig into this today” ([reply](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2119415029812609301), 13:01 UTC). That reply followed a complaint from Derek Neighbors (@dneighbors):

> Problem is last week a $200 plan lasted 7 days full throttle. This week it lasts 14hrs under moderate load.

Neighbors' hours and plan description are **his own account** of usage and are not independently verified here. TeslaNorth summarized the same exchange as context; this article quotes the posts directly rather than treating that outlet as the primary source.

## What is not established

Musk's Nov 8 fixes post does not say what will change for subscribers, whether weekly allowances will move, or how improvement will be measured. User complaints about usage running out faster are reported as complaints—not as audited measurements of every plan tier.

No claim is made here that limits have already risen or that every subscriber will see a specific change.
