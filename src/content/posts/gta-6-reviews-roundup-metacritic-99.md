Grand Theft Auto VI has a Metascore of 99 on [Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi/), based on 147 critic reviews and labeled Universal Acclaim, when the official game page was checked at 8:12 a.m. CT (America/Chicago) on Wednesday, November 18, 2026. IGN, GameSpot and Game Informer have each scored the game 10 out of 10. It still launches Thursday, November 19, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, as laid out in VetTV News' [launch-week preview](/posts/gta-6-launch-week-preview-countdown/).

Aggregate scores change as more reviews are added. The figures below are what those pages showed at the time they were opened.

## Metacritic and OpenCritic

The lead Metascore on [Metacritic's Grand Theft Auto VI page](https://www.metacritic.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi/) was 99, with the page stating "Universal Acclaim Based on 147 Critic Reviews" and "100% Positive 147 Reviews, 0% Mixed, 0% Negative." The same page's All Platforms section also listed a 99 based on 153 critic reviews, and a 99 based on 14 critic reviews. The page's own description line used the 153-review figure. User reviews were still marked "tbd" and "not available yet" at that check.

Metacritic's related-games list on that page also showed [The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (1998)](https://www.metacritic.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi/) at 99. [Windows Central's review roundup](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/gta-vi-review-roundup-calling-it-the-highest-rated-game-ever-on-metacritic-still-doesnt-do-it-justice), by Sean Endicott, published November 18, 2026, described the 99 as "matching the highest-ever rating for a game (original Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time)." VetTV News has not independently verified a Metacritic all-time ranking that places Grand Theft Auto VI alone at No. 1, and is not treating that as a settled fact.

[OpenCritic's game page](https://opencritic.com/game/15398/grand-theft-auto-vi), opened the same morning shortly after the Metacritic check, listed a 99 Top Critic Average and "100% Critics Recommend." The page's description said Grand Theft Auto VI "is rated 'Mighty' after being reviewed by 75 critics, with an overall average score of 99" and "recommended by 100% of critics."

## Perfect and five-star scores

[Luke Reilly's IGN review](https://www.ign.com/articles/grand-theft-auto-vi-review), published at 11:00 a.m. UTC (5:00 a.m. CT) on November 18, 2026, scores Grand Theft Auto VI 10 out of 10. The review is subtitled "Unprecedented. Unbelievable. Unmissable," and Reilly writes, "Grand Theft Auto 6 is the single most impressive video game I have ever played."

[GameSpot's review](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/grand-theft-auto-6-review/) by Tamoor Hussain, posted at 3:00 a.m. Pacific the same morning (5:00 a.m. CT), is accompanied by a GameSpot page titled "[Why We Gave Grand Theft Auto 6 A 10/10](https://www.gamespot.com/videos/why-we-gave-grand-theft-auto-6-a-10-10/)," which says the game "earned a 10/10 'Essential' from GameSpot."

[Matt Miller's Game Informer review](https://gameinformer.com/review/grand-theft-auto-vi/outrageous-fortune), "Outrageous Fortune," is dated November 18, 2026, at 5:00 a.m. CT and scores the game 10 out of 10. It was reviewed on PlayStation 5. Miller calls it "a generational game that stands toe-to-toe with many of the great crime stories and satires across other media."

[Chris Tapsell's Eurogamer review](https://www.eurogamer.net/grand-theft-auto-6-review), published at 11:00 a.m. UTC (5:00 a.m. CT) on November 18, scores the game 5 out of 5. The headline is "Grand Theft Auto 6 review - the most believable and unbelievable video game world ever made." Tapsell, Eurogamer's editor-in-chief, notes that he played the game on PS5 Pro.

[Josh West's GamesRadar+ review](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-review/), published at 11:00 a.m. UTC the same day, also scores it 5 out of 5. The review is headlined, "Rockstar hasn't just set a new benchmark, it has delivered the highest-fidelity sandbox of the generation."

[Jordan Middler's VGC review](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/review/grand-theft-auto-6/), timestamped 11:00 a.m. UTC on November 18 (the page displays "18th Nov 2026 / 11:00 am"), scores the game 5 out of 5. The headline calls it "the game of the generation." The review was tested on PlayStation 5 and lists "occasional frame-rate issues in dense zones" among its notes, while still calling the game "a modern masterpiece."

[Jez Corden's Windows Central review](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/xbox/grand-theft-auto-6-xbox-review), published at 11:00 a.m. UTC on November 18, rates the game 5 out of 5. Corden, Windows Central's executive editor, writes in the verdict, "A game so good I asked our tech team if I could somehow give it 11/10." The review was conducted primarily on Xbox Series X.

## Reviews without a numeric score, and a PC Gamer impressions piece

Not every major outlet posted a number. [Giovanni Colantonio's Polygon review](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-review/), published November 18, 2026, at 6:00 a.m. Eastern (5:00 a.m. CT), does not display a numeric score. Colantonio writes that GTA 6 is "one of Rockstar's finest games to date." The game was reviewed on PlayStation 5 Pro.

[Andrew Webster's review for The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/games/1035801/grand-theft-auto-6-review), published November 18, 2026, at 11:00 a.m. UTC (5:00 a.m. CT), also has no numeric score on the page. Webster writes that "the game was actually worth the wait," and that Grand Theft Auto VI "does something impossible — it lives up to the hype."

[Christopher Livingston's PC Gamer piece](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/100-hours-with-gta-6-a-sprawling-adventure-that-will-be-so-much-better-on-pc/), published at 11:00 a.m. UTC on November 18, is not a scored review. It opens, "This isn't a review." Livingston writes, "Grand Theft Auto 6 is not on PC so I can't review it," and that he "sunk approximately 100 hours in it (on something called a PlayStation 5)." On a PC release date, the exact line is: "I wish we had some idea of when GTA 6 was coming to PC because after playing almost 100 hours on PS5, I'm desperate to get it on my desktop." There is still no official PC date on Rockstar's pages.

## Performance reports, not a VetTV test

[Notebookcheck reported](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GTA-6-review-suggests-base-PS5-suffers-frame-rate-drops-during-intense-driving-and-heavy-action-sequences.1453399.0.html), in a November 18 article by Abdul Haddi published at 1:16 p.m. Central European Time (6:16 a.m. CT), that a Digital Foundry review found frame-rate drops on the base PlayStation 5 during intense driving and prolonged explosive encounters. That should be read as a report, not as VetTV's own testing.

[Digital Foundry's own comparison](https://www.digitalfoundry.net/features/gta-6-ps5-vs-ps5-pro-xbox-series-x-and-series-s-which-console-plays-best), by Oliver Mackenzie, says all platforms target 30 frames per second, with no 40 or 60 fps mode. It says the base PS5 and Xbox Series X "often flicking between 30fps and the high 20s during traversal, combat and other more challenging scenes," with the worst drops "into the mid 20s" when destroying multiple vehicles, and "into the low 20s at the worst case." The PS5 Pro, in that testing, offered a more consistent 30 fps, with short drops to a minimum of 24 fps in similar destruction tests. Digital Foundry says PS5, PS5 Pro and Series X render at 1440p, with the Pro using PSSR upscaling toward 4K. A fuller performance piece will follow separately.

## The Game Awards

Official category pages on [thegameawards.com](https://thegameawards.com/nominees) list Grand Theft Auto VI in eight categories opened for this story: [Game of the Year](https://thegameawards.com/categories/game-of-the-year), [Best Action / Adventure](https://thegameawards.com/categories/best-action-adventure), [Best Game Direction](https://thegameawards.com/categories/best-game-direction), [Best Art Direction](https://thegameawards.com/categories/best-art-direction), [Best Narrative](https://thegameawards.com/categories/best-narrative), [Best Audio Design](https://thegameawards.com/categories/best-audio-design), [Best Score and Music](https://thegameawards.com/categories/best-score-and-music), and [Best Performance](https://thegameawards.com/categories/best-performance), where Manni L. Perez is nominated for Grand Theft Auto VI. The Players' Voice category page could not be loaded in this check, so this is the count from the official category pages that did open, not a claim that no other category exists.

[The Game Awards' own FAQ](https://thegameawards.com/faq) says the show "streams live on Thursday, December 10, 2026 from the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles," with the opening act starting at 7:30 p.m. ET / 4:30 p.m. PT (6:30 p.m. CT). An official [news post](https://thegameawards.com/news/tga-returns-december-10-2026) uses the same date. Fan voting, the FAQ says, closes on Wednesday, December 9 at 6 p.m. PT, which matches the nominees page's deadline of "December 9 at 9pm ET / 6p PT."

## What this is, and what comes next

*VetTV News opinion:* This is a reviews roundup, not a VetTV review, and we have not scored the game. The ratings above belong to those outlets. Early notices are unusually strong, but an aggregate can still move, and performance reports are already raising questions about frame rate on base hardware that we have not verified in our own tests.

The U.S. PlayStation Store and Microsoft Store listings still unlock at 11:00 p.m. CT tonight, as reported in the [launch-week preview](/posts/gta-6-launch-week-preview-countdown/). Official patch notes, if Rockstar posts them, and a longer look at performance will be covered separately.
