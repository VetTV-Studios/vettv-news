**Sword of the Sea** is **Steam Deck Verified**, developer **Giant Squid** said in a [Steam news post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2453160/view/1848664643342652) titled "Sword of the Sea is now Steam Deck Verified ✅." The post's Steam timestamp is **1793903782** (**2026-11-05 12:36:22 CT**). Steam app ID: **2453160**.

## What the studio says

> "Great news, Sword of the Sea is now ready for handheld play so you can surf in style wherever you go. We've tuned performance, optimized graphics, and eliminated bugs to give you the most portable surfing experience yet!"

The post links the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2453160/Sword_of_the_Sea/).

## Related note (same studio, earlier patch)

A separate **Patch 1.6** news item (Steam date **2026-10-29**) listed "Steam Deck Verified Status" among updates alongside DLSS support and other graphics/haptics work. Treat the **November 5** post as the dedicated Verified announcement; Patch 1.6 is prior studio notes.

The store API listing we checked shows Windows support (not native Linux/macOS); Deck play uses Steam's usual compatibility path under the Verified badge.

*Opinion:* Verified is the right badge for a controller-first stroll like this—Deck is a natural fit once the performance pass lands.
