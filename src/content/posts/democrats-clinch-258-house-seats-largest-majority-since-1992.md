*Updated Nov. 25, 2026: This story originally said Democrats had at least 258 seats, their largest majority since 1992, with four races open. On Tuesday, Nov. 24, [the AP called](https://apnews.com/article/victoria-broderick-tennessee-house-democrat-a9b4d403763a82ab536ea133cee59eb4) Tennessee's 4th District for Democrat Victoria Broderick over Republican Rep. Scott DesJarlais, and [the AP now reports](https://apnews.com/article/democrats-house-majority-election-2026-congress-07b3ccbe60db7c1d3d3f3087d8fca721) at least 259 Democratic seats, the most since the 1990 election, with three races still open. We updated the headline, the seat counts, the list of open races and the cover, and added a section on the Tennessee race.*

House Democrats will start the next Congress with at least 259 seats, more than in any election since 1990, [the Associated Press reported](https://apnews.com/article/democrats-house-majority-election-2026-congress-07b3ccbe60db7c1d3d3f3087d8fca721) on Tuesday, November 24.

The 259th seat came from Tennessee's 4th District, where Democrat Victoria Broderick narrowly defeated Republican Rep. Scott DesJarlais, according to [the AP](https://apnews.com/article/victoria-broderick-tennessee-house-democrat-a9b4d403763a82ab536ea133cee59eb4). That came two days after the AP first reported Democrats had reached [at least 258](https://apnews.com/article/democrats-house-majority-election-2026-congress-721096a4d9ba2cd68683692361798cdb), a total that matched their 1992 result.

The race that took them to 258 was North Carolina's 5th District. Democrat Chuck Hubbard defeated Republican Rep. Virginia Foxx, who chairs the House Rules Committee, after a recount failed to close a gap of a few hundred votes, according to [the AP](https://apnews.com/article/chuck-hubbard-virginia-foxx-north-carolina-23732afcd78a853e98acd9b72d0cbd14) and [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Politics/democrat-chuck-hubbard-wins-recount-north-carolina-us/story?id=138362634). Foxx conceded on Sunday.

Republicans will have at least 173 seats, [the AP reported](https://www.seattletimes.com/nation-world/nation/democrats-clinch-a-historic-house-majority-of-at-least-259-seats-the-largest-since-1990/). Three House races are still undecided: one in Minnesota and two in Louisiana that are headed to December runoffs.

## How big 259 is

Records kept by the [Office of the House Historian](https://history.house.gov/Institution/Party-Divisions/Party-Divisions/) put the number in context:

- **102nd Congress (1991-1993), elected in 1990:** 267 Democrats, 167 Republicans, 1 independent.
- **103rd Congress (1993-1995), elected in 1992:** 258 Democrats, 176 Republicans.
- **111th Congress (2009-2011), elected in 2008:** 257 Democrats, 178 Republicans.
- **119th Congress (2025-2027), the current House:** 220 Republicans, 215 Democrats at the start of the term.

At 259, the incoming majority is bigger than the Democrats' totals after the 1992 election (258) and the 2008 election (257), [the AP reported](https://www.seattletimes.com/nation-world/nation/democrats-clinch-a-historic-house-majority-of-at-least-259-seats-the-largest-since-1990/), which makes it the largest since the 1990 election. Even at 258, it was already the most House seats either party had held this century, as [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/weekend-rundown-november-22-rcna610436) noted. It is still well short of the Franklin D. Roosevelt-era Democratic majorities, which went past 300, [the AP](https://www.seattletimes.com/nation-world/nation/democrats-clinch-a-historic-house-majority-of-at-least-259-seats-the-largest-since-1990/) noted.

According to [the AP](https://www.seattletimes.com/nation-world/nation/democrats-clinch-a-historic-house-majority-of-at-least-259-seats-the-largest-since-1990/), Democrats have gained 45 House seats so far this cycle, five more than the 40 they picked up in 2018, the midterm of President Donald Trump's first term. On the Republican side, the AP noted that the party's 173-seat floor is far below the 230 seats it won in 1994 and the 247 it won in 2014.

Democrats will also control the Senate, where they won a 56-seat majority after [Mary Peltola's win in Alaska](/posts/peltola-unseats-sullivan-alaska-senate-democrats-56-seats/). With a Republican president in the White House, that means divided government starting in January.

## The race that reached 258

Unofficial results posted by the [North Carolina State Board of Elections](https://er.ncsbe.gov/?election_dt=11/03/2026&county_id=0&office=FED&contest=0), as of Monday morning, show:

- **Chuck Hubbard (D):** 139,769 votes (48.74%)
- **Virginia Foxx (R):** 139,439 votes (48.62%)
- **Robert B. Luffman (Libertarian):** 7,578 votes (2.64%)

That is a 330-vote margin out of 286,786 votes. Foxx had asked for a recount, and [The News & Observer](https://www.newsobserver.com/news/politics-government/article318203408.html) reported that the recount moved the margin by only one vote. The results are not final until the state certifies them.

Foxx, first elected in 2004, [said in a statement](https://www.wxii12.com/article/virginia-foxx-concedes-recount-north-carolina-5th-congressional-district/74501474) posted Sunday: "After a hard-fought and thorough election process, the voters of the Fifth Congressional District exercised their Constitutional rights and made their voices heard." She added that she had called Hubbard "to congratulate" him and "sent him my best wishes as he represents this wonderful district in the 120th Congress."

Hubbard, a former journalist who also ran against Foxx in 2024, [wrote](https://www.wxii12.com/article/virginia-foxx-concedes-recount-north-carolina-5th-congressional-district/74501474): "Let me be the first to thank Congresswoman Foxx for her decades of service and countless sacrifices. I deeply appreciate her commitment to a smooth transition of the office and constituent casework."

Trump carried the district by almost 18 points in 2024, according to an NBC News Decision Desk analysis [cited by NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/weekend-rundown-november-22-rcna610436). With Hubbard's win, Democrats will hold 11 of North Carolina's 14 House seats, up from four, [WRAL reported](https://www.wral.com/news/nccapitol/virginia-foxx-concedes-november-2026/).

## The race that reached 259

[The AP](https://apnews.com/article/victoria-broderick-tennessee-house-democrat-a9b4d403763a82ab536ea133cee59eb4) called Tennessee's 4th District for Broderick on Tuesday, Nov. 24, at 11:12 a.m. Central time, [FOX 17 Nashville reported](https://fox17.com/news/election/victoria-broderick-officially-wins-tennessees-4th-congressional-district-per-ap). Broderick was ahead by 361 votes when the AP made its call, three weeks after Election Day, according to [Roll Call](https://rollcall.com/2026/11/24/victoria-broderick-democrat-scott-desjarlais-tennessee).

The state's unofficial election-night totals, still posted by the [Tennessee Secretary of State](https://www.elections.tn.gov/county-breakdown/United%20States%20House%20of%20Representatives%20District%204), show a closer race:

- **Victoria Broderick (D):** 95,585 votes
- **Scott DesJarlais (R):** 95,305 votes
- **Jacob Kristopher Anders (I):** 2,833 votes
- **Clay Faircloth (I):** 1,853 votes

That is a 280-vote gap. Provisional ballots counted in Davidson County after Election Day widened Broderick's lead, [Tennessee Lookout reported](https://tennesseelookout.com/2026/11/16/desjarlais-concedes-tennessee-4th-congressional-district-to-democrat-victoria-broderick/). The results are not official until the state certifies them, which [the Secretary of State's office expected](https://www.theepochtimes.com/us/democrat-leads-gop-incumbent-in-tennessee-congressional-district-by-280-votes-6118484) to happen on Dec. 1.

DesJarlais, an eight-term Republican first elected in 2010, had already conceded on Nov. 16. ["It appears that we have a result showing my campaign has come just a few hundred votes short out of just under 200,000 votes cast,"](https://desjarlais.house.gov/2026/11/desjarlais-announces-concession) he said in a statement, adding that he had called Broderick to congratulate her.

Broderick, who lost to DesJarlais by more than 40 points in 2024 under the old map, won a district that Republican lawmakers redrew this year, [Roll Call reported](https://rollcall.com/2026/11/24/victoria-broderick-democrat-scott-desjarlais-tennessee). The new 4th District reaches into Nashville's Davidson County and would have backed Trump by 24 points in 2024, according to Inside Elections calculations cited by Roll Call. "Whether you voted for me or not, I want you to know that I will be your representative," Broderick [said in a statement](https://fox17.com/news/election/victoria-broderick-officially-wins-tennessees-4th-congressional-district-per-ap).

## What is still open

- **Minnesota's 1st District:** Democrat Jake Johnson leads Republican Rep. Brad Finstad by 324 votes in results certified by the State Canvassing Board on November 19, [FOX 9 reported](https://www.fox9.com/news/mn-1st-congressional-district-race-results-certified-recount-lingers-nov-19-2026). Finstad requested a state-funded recount that day, and counties will recount ballots between Nov. 30 and Dec. 3, with the board set to certify the recount on Dec. 8, [MPR News reported](https://www.mprnews.org/story/2026/11/19/brad-finstad-requests-recount-in-1st-congressional-district-after-loss-to-jake-johnson).
- **Louisiana:** two races go to December runoffs, [according to the AP](https://www.seattletimes.com/nation-world/nation/democrats-clinch-a-historic-house-majority-of-at-least-259-seats-the-largest-since-1990/).

## What comes next

The 120th Congress convenes on January 3, 2027. House Democrats have [nominated Hakeem Jeffries for speaker](/posts/house-democrats-nominate-jeffries-speaker-251-11/). If the full House elects him, he will be the first Black speaker in U.S. history.

[The AP reported](https://www.alaskasnewssource.com/2026/11/22/democrats-clinch-historic-house-majority-least-258-seats-largest-since-1992/) that Democrats are planning early votes to reverse Republican Medicaid cuts and restore the Affordable Care Act tax credits that expired, and that they intend to use their subpoena power for investigations of the Trump administration, including how Trump and his family have grown wealthier since he returned to office. The AP also reported that Jeffries has not ruled out trying to impeach Trump a third time. The White House has warned that some of the planned probes may amount to "blatant criminality," the AP reported, and Trump has since [ordered federal contractors](/posts/trump-order-federal-contractors-notify-white-house-congress-requests/) to alert the White House before answering congressional requests.

There may also be areas of agreement. Trump said Saturday that he would work with the incoming Democratic Congress to cap credit card interest rates, [the AP reported](https://apnews.com/article/trump-credit-cards-interest-rates-warren-banks-098a69b520e520e535eebe595cd0c64a). "I would do it. These credit card companies have made a lot," he told Fox News Channel's "Saturday in America."

Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez of New York called the results "a mandate for change," [according to the AP](https://www.alaskasnewssource.com/2026/11/22/democrats-clinch-historic-house-majority-least-258-seats-largest-since-1992/). Rep. Jim Jordan of Ohio, whom House Republicans have chosen as their leader in the minority, said: "Over the next two years, House Republicans will fight for American families."
