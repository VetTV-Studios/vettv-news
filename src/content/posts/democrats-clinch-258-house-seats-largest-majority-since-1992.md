House Democrats will start the next Congress with at least 258 seats, their largest majority since the 1992 election, [the Associated Press reported](https://apnews.com/article/democrats-house-majority-election-2026-congress-721096a4d9ba2cd68683692361798cdb) on Sunday, November 22.

The race that put them over the line was North Carolina's 5th District. Democrat Chuck Hubbard defeated Republican Rep. Virginia Foxx, who chairs the House Rules Committee, after a recount failed to close a gap of a few hundred votes, according to [the AP](https://apnews.com/article/chuck-hubbard-virginia-foxx-north-carolina-23732afcd78a853e98acd9b72d0cbd14) and [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Politics/democrat-chuck-hubbard-wins-recount-north-carolina-us/story?id=138362634). Foxx conceded on Sunday.

Republicans will have at least 173 seats, [the AP reported](https://www.alaskasnewssource.com/2026/11/22/democrats-clinch-historic-house-majority-least-258-seats-largest-since-1992/). Four House races are still undecided: one in Minnesota, one in Tennessee and two in Louisiana that are headed to December runoffs.

## How big 258 is

Records kept by the [Office of the House Historian](https://history.house.gov/Institution/Party-Divisions/Party-Divisions/) put the number in context:

- **103rd Congress (1993-1995), elected in 1992:** 258 Democrats, 176 Republicans.
- **111th Congress (2009-2011), elected in 2008:** 257 Democrats, 178 Republicans.
- **119th Congress (2025-2027), the current House:** 220 Republicans, 215 Democrats at the start of the term.

That makes 258 the most House seats either party has held so far this century, as [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/weekend-rundown-november-22-rcna610436) noted, citing the House Historian. It is still well short of the Franklin D. Roosevelt-era Democratic majorities, which went past 300, [the AP](https://www.alaskasnewssource.com/2026/11/22/democrats-clinch-historic-house-majority-least-258-seats-largest-since-1992/) noted.

According to [the AP](https://www.alaskasnewssource.com/2026/11/22/democrats-clinch-historic-house-majority-least-258-seats-largest-since-1992/), Democrats have gained 44 House seats so far this cycle, four more than the 40 they picked up in 2018, the midterm of President Donald Trump's first term. On the Republican side, the AP noted that the party's 173-seat floor is far below the 230 seats it won in 1994 and the 247 it won in 2014.

Democrats will also control the Senate, where they won a 56-seat majority after [Mary Peltola's win in Alaska](/posts/peltola-unseats-sullivan-alaska-senate-democrats-56-seats/). With a Republican president in the White House, that means divided government starting in January.

## The race that settled it

Unofficial results posted by the [North Carolina State Board of Elections](https://er.ncsbe.gov/?election_dt=11/03/2026&county_id=0&office=FED&contest=0), as of Monday morning, show:

- **Chuck Hubbard (D):** 139,769 votes (48.74%)
- **Virginia Foxx (R):** 139,439 votes (48.62%)
- **Robert B. Luffman (Libertarian):** 7,578 votes (2.64%)

That is a 330-vote margin out of 286,786 votes. Foxx had asked for a recount, and [The News & Observer](https://www.newsobserver.com/news/politics-government/article318203408.html) reported that the recount moved the margin by only one vote. The results are not final until the state certifies them.

Foxx, first elected in 2004, [said in a statement](https://www.wxii12.com/article/virginia-foxx-concedes-recount-north-carolina-5th-congressional-district/74501474) posted Sunday: "After a hard-fought and thorough election process, the voters of the Fifth Congressional District exercised their Constitutional rights and made their voices heard." She added that she had called Hubbard "to congratulate" him and "sent him my best wishes as he represents this wonderful district in the 120th Congress."

Hubbard, a former journalist who also ran against Foxx in 2024, [wrote](https://www.wxii12.com/article/virginia-foxx-concedes-recount-north-carolina-5th-congressional-district/74501474): "Let me be the first to thank Congresswoman Foxx for her decades of service and countless sacrifices. I deeply appreciate her commitment to a smooth transition of the office and constituent casework."

Trump carried the district by almost 18 points in 2024, according to an NBC News Decision Desk analysis [cited by NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/weekend-rundown-november-22-rcna610436). With Hubbard's win, Democrats will hold 11 of North Carolina's 14 House seats, up from four, [WRAL reported](https://www.wral.com/news/nccapitol/virginia-foxx-concedes-november-2026/).

## What is still open

- **Minnesota's 1st District:** Democrat Jake Johnson leads Republican Rep. Brad Finstad by 324 votes in results certified by the State Canvassing Board on November 19. The margin is close enough that Finstad can request a recount paid for by the state, [FOX 9 reported](https://www.fox9.com/news/mn-1st-congressional-district-race-results-certified-recount-lingers-nov-19-2026).
- **Tennessee:** one race has not been called by the AP.
- **Louisiana:** two races go to December runoffs.

## What comes next

The 120th Congress convenes on January 3, 2027. House Democrats have [nominated Hakeem Jeffries for speaker](/posts/house-democrats-nominate-jeffries-speaker-251-11/). If the full House elects him, he will be the first Black speaker in U.S. history.

[The AP reported](https://www.alaskasnewssource.com/2026/11/22/democrats-clinch-historic-house-majority-least-258-seats-largest-since-1992/) that Democrats are planning early votes to reverse Republican Medicaid cuts and restore the Affordable Care Act tax credits that expired, and that they intend to use their subpoena power for investigations of the Trump administration, including how Trump and his family have grown wealthier since he returned to office. The AP also reported that Jeffries has not ruled out trying to impeach Trump a third time. The White House has warned that some of the planned probes may amount to "blatant criminality," the AP reported, and Trump has since [ordered federal contractors](/posts/trump-order-federal-contractors-notify-white-house-congress-requests/) to alert the White House before answering congressional requests.

There may also be areas of agreement. Trump said Saturday that he would work with the incoming Democratic Congress to cap credit card interest rates, [the AP reported](https://apnews.com/article/trump-credit-cards-interest-rates-warren-banks-098a69b520e520e535eebe595cd0c64a). "I would do it. These credit card companies have made a lot," he told Fox News Channel's "Saturday in America."

Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez of New York called the results "a mandate for change," [according to the AP](https://www.alaskasnewssource.com/2026/11/22/democrats-clinch-historic-house-majority-least-258-seats-largest-since-1992/). Rep. Jim Jordan of Ohio, whom House Republicans have chosen as their leader in the minority, said: "Over the next two years, House Republicans will fight for American families."
