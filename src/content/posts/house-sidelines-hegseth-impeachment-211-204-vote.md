The House voted 211 to 204 on Tuesday, November 10, to refer the resolution impeaching Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to committee, effectively ending the effort for now. The roll call (No. 317 on H. Res. 1655) closed at 6:02 p.m. ET, with 18 members not voting, per the [Office of the Clerk](https://clerk.house.gov/votes/2026317). The [Republican Cloakroom](https://repcloakroom.house.gov/vote_sheet/vote-series-i-tuesday-november-10th-2026/) lists it as a Republican motion to refer.

It was not a vote on whether to impeach Hegseth. Referring the resolution short-circuits any floor vote on the articles and virtually guarantees they won't be taken up before the current Congress ends, [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/10/nx-s1-6036813/house-votes-to-effectively-end-the-impeachment-effort-against-pete-hegseth) reported. Had the effort advanced, final votes would have come Wednesday, per [The Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/national-security/2026/11/10/hegseth-defends-his-record-iran-war-impeachment-vote-looms/).

## How members voted

Republicans voted 210 to 1 to refer the resolution, Democrats voted 203 to 0 against, and the House's one independent, Rep. Kevin Kiley of California, voted to refer, according to the [Clerk's tally](https://clerk.house.gov/votes/2026317). Seven Republicans and 11 Democrats did not vote.

The lone Republican against the motion was Rep. Thomas Massie (R-Ky.), the resolution's author. "Somebody's got to put their foot down and say, 'Are we going to follow the laws that are on the books? Are we going to follow the Constitution?'" Massie said, per [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/republican-led-house-rejects-hegseth-impeachment-effort-rcna608534). Because he filed it as a privileged resolution, GOP leaders had to hold a vote within two legislative days, NBC reported. Massie reintroduced it on Monday after his first attempt expired when Speaker Mike Johnson called an early recess before the midterms, per [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/10/nx-s1-6036813/house-votes-to-effectively-end-the-impeachment-effort-against-pete-hegseth).

## What the articles charged

Massie's eight articles accuse Hegseth of waging the Iran war without authorization and continuing it after Congress directed U.S. forces be removed, ignoring laws meant to limit civilian casualties, directing "extrajudicial killings" in strikes on suspected drug boats, seizing Venezuela's Nicolás Maduro without legal authority, retaliating against Sen. Mark Kelly (D-Ariz.), and carrying out an unlawful war in Yemen, per [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/republican-led-house-rejects-hegseth-impeachment-effort-rcna608534) and Massie's [press release](https://massie.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=395853). These are allegations in a resolution, not findings.

## What Hegseth and Trump said

Hegseth opened the day with a rare Pentagon news conference. "It's a farce, and it's a stunt and everybody knows it," he said, per [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/republican-led-house-rejects-hegseth-impeachment-effort-rcna608534), arguing every operation was lawful. "I stand behind every one of these operations," he added, according to [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/10/nx-s1-6036813/house-votes-to-effectively-end-the-impeachment-effort-against-pete-hegseth). "I was proud to execute them, and I would do it again tomorrow."

After the vote, President Donald Trump called the result an "EXONERATION of a Great American Patriot" on Truth Social, [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/republican-led-house-rejects-hegseth-impeachment-effort-rcna608534) reported.

## Why some Hegseth critics voted no

Outgoing Rep. Don Bacon (R-Neb.), who has clashed with Hegseth, told reporters, "I don't really align with the impeachment itself," per [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/republican-led-house-rejects-hegseth-impeachment-effort-rcna608534). Rep. Darrell Issa (R-Calif.) said the measure would "die in the Senate," where 67 votes are needed to convict, NBC reported.

## What happens next

Democrats take the House majority in January and have promised investigations into Hegseth's Pentagon, [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/10/nx-s1-6036813/house-votes-to-effectively-end-the-impeachment-effort-against-pete-hegseth) reported. House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries (D-N.Y.), expected to become speaker, has said Democrats will "run Pete Hegseth out of town," per [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/republican-led-house-rejects-hegseth-impeachment-effort-rcna608534). Whether they will bring a new impeachment resolution is unclear, NPR said.

**Not confirmed as of 9 a.m. CT Wednesday:** which committee received the resolution (the Clerk's roll-call page does not name it), and whether Democrats will refile articles of impeachment in the new Congress.
