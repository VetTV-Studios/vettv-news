Apple posted revised developer betas of iOS 27.3 and iPadOS 27.3 on November 19, 2026. The company's [iOS releases entry](https://developer.apple.com/news/releases/?id=11192026a) lists **iOS 27.3 beta 2 v2 (24C5049f)**, and the [iPadOS entry](https://developer.apple.com/news/releases/?id=11192026b) lists **iPadOS 27.3 beta 2 v2 (24C5049f)**, both dated November 19. We checked [Apple's releases index](https://developer.apple.com/news/releases/) on the morning of November 20.

Those November 19 posts are the official confirmation we have from Apple: a second developer beta 2 build for iPhone and iPad. The same index still dated the 27.3 beta 2 builds of macOS (26C5047e), tvOS (24K5298d), and watchOS (24S5296d) to November 16.

[MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/19/ios-27-3-public-beta-2/) reported on November 19 at 1:43 p.m. PST (3:43 p.m. CT) that Apple also seeded the second public betas of iOS 27.3, iPadOS 27.3, macOS Golden Gate 27.3, tvOS 27.3, and watchOS 27.3 that day, and that revised iOS and iPadOS 27.3 betas were available for developers. Apple's brief releases stubs do not announce the public betas, so that part of the story is attributed to MacRumors.

## Features, according to MacRumors

Apple's November 19 releases posts do not describe what is in the builds. The highlights below come from MacRumors, unless noted.

According to MacRumors, iOS 27.3 adds a Vertical Tabs layout option and Adaptive Dark Mode for Safari. In the second developer beta, MacRumors says, Apple introduced a facial unlock feature for iPhone Duo that uses the front camera, along with an option to lock the iPhone Duo when it is closed. MacRumors also says a redesigned Health app is available with custom Insights and Longevity information, and that Apple has started testing Apple Intelligence and Siri AI usage limits, with extra access available through iCloud+ plans.

On the Mac side, MacRumors says macOS Golden Gate 27.3 has the same Safari features and "brings Live Activities to Macs without a Dynamic Island."

We previously covered Apple's own wording on those Siri AI limits, and on extra access through some iCloud+ plans, in [Apple Starts Testing Siri AI Daily Limits, With Extra Access on Some iCloud+ Plans](/posts/ios-27-3-siri-ai-daily-limits-icloud-plus/).

MacRumors also mentions Reachability on iPhone Duo, a Selfie Sing feature in Apple Music, StandBy changes on non-Duo iPhones, and Audio Intelligence features on Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4. Those items are likewise from MacRumors' report, not from Apple's releases posts.
