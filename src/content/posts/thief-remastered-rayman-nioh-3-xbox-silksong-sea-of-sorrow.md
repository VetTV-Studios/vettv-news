Four gaming items from Thanksgiving week, each checked against the publisher's or developer's own post.

## Thief and Rayman come back December 3

Xbox Wire's [Next Week on XBOX](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/27/next-week-on-xbox-new-games-for-november-30-to-december-4/) list, posted November 27 by Will Fulton, includes three re-releases on **December 3**:

- **Thief: The Dark Project Remastered**: "Nightdive Studios brings back the pioneering 3D stealth game from Looking Glass Studios." The Xbox listing shows Smart Delivery and Xbox Play Anywhere, with a **$29.99** pre-order. On [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4200960/), it's listed for Windows at $29.99 for December 3, developed by Nightdive Studios and Looking Glass Studios and published by Atari.
- **Rayman Legends Retold** (Ubisoft): billed as "a bold reimagining of the acclaimed platformer, filled with new content and narrative twists." Pre-orders get the Hoodlum Havoc Pack, which includes two costumes for Rayman and Globox.
- **Rayman Origins: Enhanced Edition** (Ubisoft).

The list doesn't give PC or other platform details for the two Rayman games, so we haven't listed any.

Also on the list: **Hela: of Mice & Magic** (December 1) and **FreeStyle Football 2** (December 3), both marked Game Pass and Play Anywhere, and Square Enix's **Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World** (December 3, $59.99 pre-order, Play Anywhere).

## Nioh 3 comes to Xbox on January 28, 2027

On [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/24/nioh-3-xbox-series-debut/) on November 24, Koei Tecmo America's John Hardin wrote that "Nioh 3 and Nioh 3: Complete Edition will launch for the first time on XBOX Series X|S beginning January 28, 2027." It's the series' first release on Xbox. The Complete Edition includes the base game plus the **Hell Rising** and **Bloody Insurrection** expansions. The post names Xbox Series X|S only and doesn't mention Game Pass or Play Anywhere.

## Silksong: Sea of Sorrow arrives December 11

Team Cherry [posted on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1030300/view/687523504123283104) at **9:12 a.m. CT on November 27** that **Hollow Knight: Silksong - Sea of Sorrow** is "free for all players December 11." Team Cherry writes that players "will voyage above and beneath Pharloom's forgotten ocean, across islands, through forts and into deep caverns beneath the waves," with new music by Christopher Larkin. Silksong is a native Linux game on Steam.

## Free Play Days through Sunday

Xbox Wire's [Free Play Days](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/26/free-play-days-11-26-2026/) post makes **Warhammer 40,000: Mechanicus** and **Little Rocket Lab** free to play "from Thursday, November 26 until Sunday, November 29" for Game Pass Ultimate, Premium and Essential members. Mechanicus is 90% off ($3.99) until December 7, and Little Rocket Lab is 30% off ($13.99).
