Valve published [Proton 11.0-3](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/Proton/releases/tag/proton-11.0-3) on GitHub on **November 24 at 12:22 a.m. CT** (06:22 UTC). The release is marked as the latest stable tag. [GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2026/11/proton-11-0-3-is-out-now-bringing-lots-of-fixes-for-windows-games-on-steamos-linux/), writing at 1:36 a.m. CT, says it arrives in Steam as an update to Proton 11, and that a lot of the changes were already in Proton Experimental.

## Newly playable

The official notes list **15** newly playable titles:

- Hero Plus
- City Car Driving
- Soul Land: Awakening World
- Bloody Zombies
- Welcome to Elderfield Demo
- The Heroic Legend of America: Second Revolutionary War
- Blair Witch VR
- Cool People Club
- The Eden of Grisaia
- The Rabbit's Scroll
- Elisa: The Innkeeper - Prequel
- Korea. IL-2 Series
- One Shot: Fading Memory
- Impulse Response
- 8Bit Fiesta - The Drinking Game

## Device-specific and other notable fixes

Among the device-specific items, Valve lists **HDR in Far Cry 5 on OLED Steam Decks**, a fix for the **Final Fantasy XIV launcher not working on the Steam Frame**, Ragnarok: The New World playable on devices other than Steam Decks, Dragon Quest Builders audio issues after suspend, Arma 3 input issues after changing focus between the game and the launcher on gamescope, and Far Cry no longer becoming uninteractable after switching away and back on SteamOS.

Other notable fixes in the same notes include:

- Crysis and Crysis Wars playable on some newer AMD CPUs that support CPUID faulting
- Avatar: Frontiers of Pandora and Star Wars Outlaws detecting game controllers
- Controllers working again in Dragon Age: Inquisition
- Steam authentication in Nightingale, and Borderlands 4 no longer showing a command prompt on first launch
- Japanese text rendering in Killer7
- A "SuspendThread loop" crash in VRChat when fsync is used
- Better Steam Input controller support in Far Cry New Dawn, Binding of Isaac: Rebirth, and Split/Second
- Several Xalia launcher fixes, including Deus Ex: Mankind Divided, Final Fantasy XI, Fallout: New Vegas, and Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2 (2009)

The notes also add **early support for 32-bit OpenXR**, which "requires `PROTON_USE_WOW64=1` on x86."

## Proton 11 regressions and components

Valve lists **19** Proton 11 regression fixes, including Deus Ex: Mankind Divided on AMD GPUs, Lords of the Fallen, Command & Conquer: Generals menu glitches, Grand Theft Auto III intro videos, Guild Wars 2 and improved focus-loss behavior on KDE for games including Overwatch and Garry's Mod, a Forza Horizon 4 memory leak on first start on KDE Wayland, and Command & Conquer: Red Alert Counterstrike and The Aftermath.

Component updates listed in the release: **vkd3d 2.1**, **Wine Mono 11.3.0**, **Xalia 0.5.0.1**, **FEX 2610**, and **dxvk-nvapi v0.9.2-94**. DXVK and vkd3d-proton are updated to the latest state of their Proton 11 support branches.

**Our take (opinion):** this is a sizable stable drop, and the Deck OLED, Steam Frame, and regression fixes are the ones most people will feel first. If a game was already fine on Proton 11, you can wait and update with the rest of Steam.
