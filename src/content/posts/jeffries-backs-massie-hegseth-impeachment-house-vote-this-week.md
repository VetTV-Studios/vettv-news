House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries (D-N.Y.) said Sunday, November 8, that he will vote to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth. "I'm a hard yes on the Hegseth resolution," Jeffries said on ABC's "This Week," according to the [ABC News transcript](https://abcnews.com/Politics/week-transcript-11-8-26-house-democratic-leader/story?id=137944448). "The so-called secretary of defense has abused his power, undermined our national security, and engineered this reckless and costly war of choice in Iran, which has skyrocketed prices on the American people and made the country less safe. Pete Hegseth has got to go."

The resolution belongs to Rep. Thomas Massie (R-Ky.), who told ABC News he intends to reintroduce it as a privileged resolution as early as Monday, November 9. Once a member raises it as a question of privilege on the floor, House leaders must schedule a vote within two legislative days, [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Politics/im-hard-hakeem-jeffries-announces-support-hegseth-impeachment/story?id=137952235) reported. [Roll Call](https://rollcall.com/2026/11/09/this-week-lame-duck-kicks-off-with-hegesth-impeachment-effort/) reported Monday that the House is expected to consider it this week as the post-election lame-duck session opens.

## What the resolution charges

Massie first filed the resolution on September 15, giving notice on the House floor that he would raise it as a question of privilege, per the [Congressional Record](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CREC-2026-09-15/html/CREC-2026-09-15-pt1-PgH5637-3.htm). His office's [press release](https://massie.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=395853) summarizes eight articles:

- **Articles I to III:** waging the Iran war in violation of three sections of the War Powers Resolution of 1973, including continuing it after both chambers agreed to H. Con. Res. 86 directing U.S. forces be removed.
- **Article IV:** ignoring laws meant to minimize civilian casualties.
- **Article V:** "extrajudicial killings" in lethal military strikes on suspected drug traffickers, which the resolution says caused at least 221 deaths.
- **Article VI:** using Defense Department authority to retaliate against Sen. Mark Kelly (D-Ariz.) for his speech.
- **Article VII:** directing the operation that seized Venezuela's Nicolás Maduro and Cilia Flores without legal authority.
- **Article VIII:** starting an unauthorized war in Yemen.

These are allegations in a resolution, not findings. The [full text](https://massie.house.gov/uploadedfiles/hegseth_articles_of_impeachment.pdf) is posted on Massie's House website. CNN described it as a 34-page resolution that Massie spent hours reading aloud on the floor in September, per [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/15/politics/thomas-massie-impeachment-pete-hegseth-trump).

## Why it didn't get a vote in September

The New York Times, in a report republished by [The Philadelphia Inquirer](https://www.inquirer.com/politics/impeach-hegseth-jeffries-hard-yes-massie-trump-iran-war-democrats-republicans-20261108.html), said Massie's September move prompted Speaker Mike Johnson to call an early recess to avoid a vote. Because that notice expired, Massie has to renew it, which restarts the two-day clock. [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/house-minority-leader-hakeem-jeffries-hard-yes-impeaching-hegseth-rcna608137) likewise reported that Republican leaders canceled planned votes and the House left early.

Massie lost his primary this spring to a Trump-backed challenger and is leaving Congress at the end of his term, per [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/15/politics/thomas-massie-impeachment-pete-hegseth-trump) and the [Times](https://www.inquirer.com/politics/impeach-hegseth-jeffries-hard-yes-massie-trump-iran-war-democrats-republicans-20261108.html).

## How the administration responded

President Donald Trump defended Hegseth on CBS News' "60 Minutes" Sunday. "They want to impeach him. Very unfair," Trump said, according to the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cj4g1nwx39wno). "We had two wars, we won both. We won Venezuela, we won Iran." The Pentagon told the BBC it had followed the law and that Hegseth would not be deterred by "phony investigations."

In a video released last week, Hegseth called the effort "sham articles" and said, "It's all about the politics of personal destruction. Didn't work against President Trump and it won't work with me," the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cj4g1nwx39wno) reported.

The Times reported that Trump wrote Saturday on social media that the impeachment calls amounted to "extortion at the highest level," and that Attorney General Todd Blanche said on CNN's "State of the Union" that the Justice Department "represents members of the executive," per [the Inquirer](https://www.inquirer.com/politics/impeach-hegseth-jeffries-hard-yes-massie-trump-iran-war-democrats-republicans-20261108.html).

## Where Republicans stand

Some Republicans have signaled openness without endorsing impeachment. Rep. French Hill (R-Ark.) said on CBS' "Face the Nation," "I want to hear the debate on it to see if it merits impeachment," while cautioning that poor performance is not by itself an impeachable offense, the [Times](https://www.inquirer.com/politics/impeach-hegseth-jeffries-hard-yes-massie-trump-iran-war-democrats-republicans-20261108.html) reported. Rep. James Comer (R-Ky.) called it "very possible" enough lawmakers would vote to impeach, the Times said.

## What happens next

Jeffries told ABC that House Democrats would "have that conversation on Tuesday morning" when they reconvene, adding, "I believe there will be incredibly strong support for that resolution," per the [transcript](https://abcnews.com/Politics/week-transcript-11-8-26-house-democratic-leader/story?id=137944448).

This vote would happen in the current Congress, where Republicans still hold the House majority until January, per the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cj4g1nwx39wno). Even if the House impeaches Hegseth, removal requires a two-thirds vote in the Senate, and only two Cabinet officials have ever been impeached, neither removed, the [Times](https://www.inquirer.com/politics/impeach-hegseth-jeffries-hard-yes-massie-trump-iran-war-democrats-republicans-20261108.html) noted.

**Unconfirmed as of 9 a.m. CT Monday:** whether Massie has formally renewed his notice, and the exact date of any floor vote. House leaders could also move to table the resolution, as the House did with a separate Trump impeachment resolution in September, per [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/15/politics/thomas-massie-impeachment-pete-hegseth-trump).
