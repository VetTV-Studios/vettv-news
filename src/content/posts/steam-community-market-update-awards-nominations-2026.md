Valve posted two Steam news items on **November 23**: a [Community Market update](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/593110/view/678515036155545443) that is live for everyone, and the opening of [2026 Steam Awards nominations](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/593110/view/685271070563893997).

## The market update

[More Steam Community Market Updates](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/593110/view/678515036155545443) went up at **4:26 p.m. CT**. Valve says this follows an earlier market beta this year with new item pages, listings, and search, and that today's changes are "available to everyone starting today." [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/software/platforms/steam-revamps-its-marketplace-for-the-2nd-time-this-year/), writing November 24, described it as the second marketplace revamp this year.

Valve's notes highlight:

- A rebuilt **Market Home** that, Valve says, loads faster and replaces the old Popular Items section with **Trending Items** drawn from the games you play, plus personalized game links.
- **My Watchlist**, which can save a specific listing, an item type (including filters), or a search (including filters and text). Entries can be renamed and reordered.
- A section for Steam Community items (trading cards, emotes, and profile backgrounds) from games you play, including badges in progress.
- The ability to **reprice a live listing** without taking it down and relisting it.
- A **list view** for search results and buy/sell listings, alongside the card view.
- **Inline Steam Guard confirmations** in the Steam Mobile App when that device is also the authenticator, so you don't have to leave the Market for the Confirmations page.
- Counter-Strike 2 containers that show and link to their possible contents.

For developers, Valve says you can now "configure a custom fee percentage for Market purchases, along an optional fee maximum for each sale."

## Awards nominations

[Nominations are now open for the 2026 Steam Awards](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/593110/view/685271070563893997), posted at **12:13 p.m. CT**. Valve says this year is the **10th anniversary**. Nominations run "from now through November 30th at 10 a.m. Pacific" (**12:00 p.m. CT**).

Valve added throwback categories inspired by earlier years: **Just 5 More Minutes**, **Defies Description**, and **This is My Job Now**. When voting opens during the Steam Winter Sale, there will also be a **Hall of Fame** category "that lets you decide among past winners of Steam's Game of the Year."

Valve says it will reveal the nominees when voting opens "alongside the start of the Steam Winter Sale on December 17th."
