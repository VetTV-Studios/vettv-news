EA has released 25 classic PC games, which its [announcement](https://www.ea.com/news/ea-classics-launch) on November 5 says are "now available on Steam for the first time, alongside GoG, and the EA App."

## Seven restored Anniversary Editions

EA says it has restored seven of the games "with robust quality-of-life updates and higher resolution Full-Motion Videos (FMV) from the Master tapes":

- Road Rash: 30th Anniversary Edition
- Wing Commander: 35th Anniversary Edition
- Wing Commander II: Vengeance of the Kilrathi: 35th Anniversary Edition
- Wing Commander III: Heart of the Tiger: 30th Anniversary Edition
- Wing Commander IV: The Price of Freedom: 30th Anniversary Edition
- Wing Commander Prophecy: 30th Anniversary Edition
- Privateer 2: The Darkening: 30th Anniversary Edition

EA says Road Rash is "the first ever digital release of the 1996 PC version." The Kilrathi Saga versions of Wing Commander I–III are integrated into the installs, which EA says is their first digital release.

## 18 faithful ports

EA calls the rest of the lineup "faithful ports":

- Wing Commander: Privateer
- Wing Commander: Academy
- Wing Commander: Armada
- Strike Commander
- Theme Hospital
- Crusader: No Remorse
- Crusader: No Regret
- Ultima I: The First Age of Darkness
- Ultima II: The Revenge of the Enchantress
- Ultima III: Exodus
- Ultima IV: Quest of the Avatar
- Ultima V: Warriors of Destiny
- Ultima VI: The False Prophet
- Ultima VII: The Complete Edition
- Ultima VIII Gold Edition
- Ultima IX: Ascension
- Ultima Underworld: The Stygian Abyss
- Ultima Underworld II: Labyrinth of Worlds

## Pricing

EA says all 25 games have "special pricing on Steam and GOG from November 5–19." When we checked Steam, Wing Commander Prophecy: 30th Anniversary Edition was listed at $9.99, discounted to $4.99, and Ultima IV and Theme Hospital were listed at $4.99, discounted to $2.49. The Verge (secondary) reports that every Anniversary Edition costs $9.99 and the other games cost $4.99. GG.deals (secondary) lists an Ultima bundle and a Wing Commander bundle on Steam.

**Already own them?** EA says anyone who "previously owned a digital copy of the Wing Commander games on GoG or the EA App will receive the Anniversary Edition upgrade at no cost on their respective digital platform."

## The vault

EA says the project began in March 2022, when Senior Producer Jim Vessella found a physical media vault at EA's Redwood Shores campus holding "almost 20,000 archival assets," including master tapes for Wing Commander and Road Rash. The team digitized an estimated 2,500 hours of footage.

*Opinion:* the Anniversary Editions are the real story here, because restored FMV from the master tapes is the kind of preservation work publishers rarely pay for.
