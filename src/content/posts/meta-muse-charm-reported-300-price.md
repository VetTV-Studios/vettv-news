The Information has reported that Meta plans to price the Muse Charm, its pocket-sized gadget for talking to the Muse AI agent, at $300, according to [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1038087/meta-muse-charm-ai-price), which published that account on November 19 at 2:22 p.m. CT. The Verge also reported The Information's claim that the Charm will come with a free 12-month 5G data plan from Verizon.

Those figures have not been confirmed by Meta. When The Verge reached Meta spokesperson Tyler Yee, he provided the same statement he had given The Information: "We're charmed by the interest in our AI devices, and we'll have more to share soon."

**Disclosure:** VetTV's news team uses Meta's Muse Code command-line tool in its own work. Muse Code is Meta's coding tool, not the Muse consumer agent or the Muse Charm device covered here. Meta had no input into this story.

## What Meta has said officially

Meta introduced Muse Charm at Connect 2026. In a [September 23 company blog post](https://www.meta.com/blog/meta-connect-2026-everything-we-announced/), Meta described it as "a delightful and fun device built for you to talk and interact with your Muse" that "brings the power of Muse with a state of the art real-time voice model into a device that fits in your pocket." The post said, "We'll have more to share later this year." It did not list a price, a carrier plan, or a ship date.

[The Verge's report on the Connect keynote](https://www.theverge.com/tech/999750/muse-charm-meta-ai-hardware), published on September 23 at 7:15 p.m. CT (September 24, 12:15 a.m. UTC), attributed further details to CEO Mark Zuckerberg on stage. Zuckerberg said Meta planned to ship the Charm "in time for the holidays in December," The Verge reported, and that the company had "only built a few of these so far." The Verge also reported that the device has a fingerprint sensor and that Zuckerberg said you don't have to unlock a phone or open an app to use it.

## What remains a report

The $300 price and the Verizon data plan come from The Information, as relayed by The Verge, not from a Meta product page or newsroom post. We did not find a Meta announcement confirming either figure. The Verge also reported The Information's claim that a Meta smartwatch has been pushed to 2027. That, too, is a report, not a company confirmation.
