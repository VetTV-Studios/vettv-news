**Bloomberg reports** that Jon McCormack, Apple's head of camera and photo software, has left the company.

According to Bloomberg's Mark Gurman, writing in accounts carried under his byline by [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/cons-products/electronics/apple-loses-the-man-behind-the-iphone-camera-jon-mccormack-photo-app/articleshow/136011815.cms), [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/apple-camera-software-chief-jon-mccormack-exits-after-eight-years-126112000193_1.html), and [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/11/20/apples-executive-in-charge-of-the-iphones-camera-app-has-left), McCormack "retired at the end of October after more than eight years at the company, according to people with knowledge of the matter." The same report says he "oversaw the Camera and Photos apps and the underlying technology that determines how users capture, view and edit their pictures and video."

We are citing Bloomberg from those full, Bloomberg-bylined copies, checked on the morning of November 20. Bloomberg's website blocked our attempts to open the article, including with a likely slug taken from the headline on [Gurman's Bloomberg author page](https://www.bloomberg.com/authors/AS7Hj1mBMGM/mark-gurman), so we could not capture a working bloomberg.com URL for the 2026 story. [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1037846/apple-jon-mccormack-retired-iphone-camera) and [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/19/apple-head-of-camera-software-retires/) also attributed the retirement to Bloomberg on November 19.

## What the Bloomberg report says about the handover

The report says Apple "hasn't named a new vice president to take over McCormack's exact role." Instead, "his former deputies are assuming his responsibilities under another executive," according to the same people, who asked not to be identified while discussing internal moves. "An Apple spokesperson declined to comment."

On the reporting line, Bloomberg says the camera and photos software teams "are now reporting to Sebastien Marineau-Mes, who oversaw the teams earlier in his career but is otherwise in charge of integrating AI features across the company's operating systems."

## Background we could verify separately

A [Bloomberg article on April 6, 2018](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2018-04-06/apple-hires-former-amazon-devices-cto-for-software-role), also by Mark Gurman, said, "Apple Inc. said it has hired technology executive Jon McCormack as a vice president working on software." That official hire, about eight and a half years before a late-October 2026 retirement, is consistent with Bloomberg's later wording that he had been at Apple for more than eight years.
