SteamDeckHQ's Nov 2–8 highlights list new Steam Deck Verified and Playable titles. Where possible, this article checked the current Steam store compatibility fields rather than taking the roundup alone as proof.

## Confirmed Verified (Steam resolved category 3)

- **DreamWorks Shrek 2 Remastered** (app 2523740)
- **Faraway** (app 2831000)
- **Disney•Pixar Toy Story 3 Complete Edition** (app 4049180)
- **Crimson Moon** (app 4317690)

SteamDeckHQ also notes Shrek 2 Remastered as pre-purchase ahead of a November 10 release. Treat storefront pricing and sale windows in the roundup as time-sensitive secondary details.

## Confirmed Playable (Steam resolved category 2)

- **Rip It! Card Collector** (app 5088440)
- **Cozy Game Restoration** (app 3581230)
- **Divine Frequency** (app 2548880)
- **DreadOut 2** (app 945710)
- **Hull Rupture** (app 4358660)
- **Phantom Blade Zero** (app 4115450)

SteamDeckHQ lists Playable caveats for several of these—community controller layouts, incorrect glyphs, small text, or manual keyboard use. Those caveat texts come from the roundup's summary of Steam's compatibility notes.

## Phantom Blade Zero performance (SteamDeckHQ)

SteamDeckHQ rates Phantom Blade Zero Playable because graphical settings may need manual changes for optimal performance. Separately, the site reports that the game "isn't the greatest experience on the Steam Deck and can't hold 30 FPS on the lowest settings from the beginning of the game to the end." That framerate claim is **SteamDeckHQ's reported experience**, not an independent VetTV News benchmark.

Verified/Playable badges describe Steam's compatibility categories. They are not promises of a specific framerate, battery life, or bug-free play across an entire campaign.
