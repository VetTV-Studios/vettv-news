xAI's [Introducing Grok Bot 1.0](https://x.ai/news/grok-bot-1-0) news post is dated **November 2, 2026** on the page. The visible date and the page's published-date metadata both say November 2. The post opens, "Today we're launching Grok Bot 1.0, a major update to our AI teammates."

The official [Grok Bot changelog](https://x.ai/changelog/bot) lists its latest entry as **v1.0.1, dated November 11, 2026**. That November 11 heading is titled "Meetings, Microsoft Teams, and shared connectors" and is marked **"Also includes 1.0.0."** The 1.0 launch date on the news page and the changelog's November 11 grouping of 1.0.0 with 1.0.1 are both taken from those primary pages.

## What xAI says 1.0 adds

These are xAI's own claims in the November 2 post. Since introducing Grok Bot in beta, the company says it made three major improvements: **multiplayer teammates** (share Bots and bring them into Slack, Microsoft Teams, and video calls), **proactive teammates** (recognize work and offer to take it on), and **more capable teammates** (faster, more reliable browser use and complex work, including documents and presentations, plus lower resource use). Capability language in that post is xAI's.

Under "Get started with Grok Bot 1.0," xAI writes that Grok Bot 1.0 "is available starting today," and it sets the plan limits as follows:

- **Team Bots** are available on **Team and Enterprise** plans.
- **Meetings** are available on **Team, Enterprise, and Ultra** plans.
- **Enterprise admins** can turn on Meetings in settings.

## Changelog v1.0.1 (November 11)

The November 11 changelog entry says that on **Team, Enterprise, and Ultra** plans, "a Bot can join a Google Meet, Zoom, or Microsoft Teams call from a link, answer in the meeting chat, and send a summary afterward."

Other items in that same v1.0.1 / 1.0.0 block, as written by xAI, include: Team Bots joining Microsoft Teams after a team admin turns it on, an owner or Manager connects the Bot, and a Teams admin approves it; owners and Managers sharing their own app login as a shared connector once a team admin turns on Allow Shared Logins; teammate teachings waiting under Review learnings until the owner or a Manager approves them; team admins limiting who shares Bots; a Voice menu Speed slider from 0.5x to 2x; and a Linux AppImage taskbar icon fix after installing.

Claims about what the Bot can do in meetings and across apps are xAI's product descriptions, not independently tested here.

*Opinion:* the useful confirmation here is the plan split — Team Bots on Team and Enterprise, Meetings on Team, Enterprise, and Ultra — not the marketing language about teammates.
