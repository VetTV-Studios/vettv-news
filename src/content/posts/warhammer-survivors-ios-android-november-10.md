Auroch Digital says **Warhammer Survivors** is out now on mobile (**iOS and Android**), as well as on PC (Steam and GOG), Nintendo Switch 1 and 2, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S. The studio's official FAQ states that the game **launched on 10th November 2026** across all platforms. Auroch describes it as an officially licensed bullet-heaven game developed with **poncle**, the studio behind Vampire Survivors, and in partnership with Warhammer.

Auroch's game page lists a price of **£6.39 | $7.99 | €7.49**, "with a 10% launch discount available on select platforms." The US App Store listing, from seller Auroch Digital Ltd, showed **$7.19** when checked, which matches a 10% reduction from that $7.99 figure. The FAQ notes that prices may vary slightly by platform and region.

The App Store description is the source for the roster counts in this story. It says the game has **over 40** iconic playable characters from Warhammer 40,000 and Warhammer Age of Sigmar, including Malum Caedo from *Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun*, and **75+** weapons. Auroch's own marketing page describes a roster of champions and an arsenal of familiar weapons, but does not itself state those two figures.
