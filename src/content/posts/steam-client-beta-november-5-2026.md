Valve posted a **Steam Client Beta** update titled **"Steam Client Beta - November 5th"** ([announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/groups/SteamClientBeta/announcements/detail/713417405767876797)). The group RSS lists the item's pubDate as **2026-11-06 00:21:59 UTC** (about **6:21 PM CST on November 5**), authored by **lostgoat**.

## Changes (full notes)

**General**

- Fixed an issue where the **friends list could get stuck** when failing to login at launch and required toggling offline to load the friends list.
- Fixed a regression that caused the **patch notes preview** to show the previous patch notes instead of the current ones.

**Big Picture Mode**

- Added **timezone selection entries** for locations with changes to daylight savings time, e.g. British Columbia, Alberta, Manitoba.

**Linux**

- Fixed a crash when **creating a lobby** in games that use the **ISteamNetworking** interface, e.g. **Sons of the Forest**.

These notes apply to the **Steam Client Beta** channel, not a SteamOS Stable/Beta/Preview or Proton release.

*Opinion:* the Linux ISteamNetworking lobby crash is the one that actually unblocks play sessions—the friends-list and patch-notes fixes are quality-of-life.
