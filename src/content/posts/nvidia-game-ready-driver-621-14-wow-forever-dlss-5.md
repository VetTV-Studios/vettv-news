NVIDIA released GeForce Game Ready Driver **621.14 WHQL** on November 4. It's a Release 620 driver for Windows 11 and Windows 10, and it covers GeForce RTX cards and NVIDIA's RTX Spark systems.

## What it's ready for

- **World of Warcraft: Forever.** The driver is tuned for Blizzard's new take on Azeroth, which [goes live today at 5:00 p.m. CT](/posts/world-of-warcraft-forever-launch-time-november-4/).
- **DOOM: The Dark Ages | Revelations.** Its new endless Slaughter Mode arrives later this month. You fight escalating demon waves across several arenas, chasing a personal best round score and earning cosmetics.
- **Hogwarts Legacy.** The game is getting DLSS 5 with Neural Rendering. NVIDIA says the option sharpens environment and character textures and improves shading, lighting, shadows and reflections. Avalanche Software says it tuned the feature with NVIDIA to keep the game's original look.
- **War Thunder.** DLSS 5 with 3D-Guided Neural Rendering comes in the Eagle's Shadow update, which NVIDIA says goes live "in just a few weeks."

## Fixes

The release notes list two fixed issues:

- *Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4* could show incorrect colors.
- On some GeForce RTX 50 Series GPUs and RTX Spark systems, video playback could freeze or go to a black screen after updating to driver 621.01.

NVIDIA lists no open issues for this version. You can get the driver from the NVIDIA app or NVIDIA's driver download page.
