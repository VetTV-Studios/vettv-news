Braden Peters, the "looksmaxxing" influencer known online as Clavicular, has been indicted in Massachusetts and is charged with two counts of rape and one count of providing alcohol to a person under 21, according to court records reported by [WCVB](https://www.wcvb.com/article/clavicular-indictment-rape-looksmaxxing/74464654) and the [Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/11/18/metro/clavicular-indicted-on-rape-charges-braden-e-peters/). [News 12 New Jersey](https://newjersey.news12.com/looksmaxxing-influencer-hoboken-native-clavicular-indicted-on-rape-charges) describes him as a Hoboken native.

**An indictment is an accusation, not a finding of guilt. Peters is presumed innocent unless and until he is proven guilty in court.** He has denied the allegations through his attorney.

## The charges

- **What he's charged with:** two counts of rape and one count of providing alcohol to an underage person, according to WCVB, the Boston Globe and [Boston 25 News](https://www.boston25news.com/news/local/social-media-influencer-clavicular-indicted-rape-charges-stemming-2025-cape-cod-incident/L5PPILU3TBDNDBJKERRK5UHCS4/). WCVB describes the rape counts as felonies and the alcohol count as a misdemeanor.
- **When:** WCVB reported that court records show the indictment was returned on November 13, 2026. The [Cape Cod Times](https://www.capecodtimes.com/story/news/courts/2026/11/18/looksmaxxing-influencer-clavicular-indicted-rape-charges-cape-cod/92845876007/) also reported that the Superior Court charges were filed November 13, according to court documents.
- **Where:** the case moves from Orleans District Court to Barnstable Superior Court on Cape Cod, the Globe reported. It is prosecuted by the office of Cape and Islands District Attorney Robert Galibois.
- **What changed:** Peters was first charged in Orleans District Court with rape, drugging a person for sexual intercourse and furnishing alcohol to a person under 21, according to the Cape Cod Times. The Boston Globe and Boston 25 News report that prosecutors dropped the drugging charge and replaced it with a second rape count.
- **The allegations:** prosecutors allege the offenses happened on May 23, 2025, at his family's home in Chatham, Massachusetts, according to court documents cited by [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/us/looksmaxxing-influencer-clavicular-allegedly-told-teen-lie-about-age-call-vodka-water-before-assault) and a police report cited by the Boston Globe. The outlets and Peters' attorney all say the complainant was 17 at the time. According to the district-court criminal complaint, as reported by WCVB, she told police Peters gave her alcohol at the home and that she woke up the next morning to him sexually assaulting her. These are allegations that have not been tested at trial.

## Plea, bail and the next court date

- **Plea:** Peters pleaded not guilty at his October 22 arraignment in Orleans District Court, the Boston Globe, WCVB and Fox News reported. He has not yet been arraigned on the indictment.
- **Bail:** he was released on $10,000 bail, surrendered his passport, and was ordered to stay away from the complainant and not to post online about the case, WCVB reported.
- **Next court date:** none set as of Friday evening in the reports we reviewed. WCVB said Superior Court records reviewed Wednesday showed no hearings scheduled, and the Globe and Boston 25 News said no Superior Court arraignment date had been set. Fox News reported that the Orleans District Court clerk's office confirmed the indictment on Wednesday and said a hearing previously scheduled for Tuesday will no longer take place.

## What the defense says

Peters' attorney, David E. Meier, said his client "categorically denies these allegations," according to the [Cape Cod Times](https://www.capecodtimes.com/story/news/courts/2026/11/18/looksmaxxing-influencer-clavicular-indicted-rape-charges-cape-cod/92845876007/) and [Oxygen](https://www.oxygen.com/crime-news/looksmaxxing-influencer-braden-clavicular-peters-accused-of-raping-teen).

In a statement reported by the Boston Globe, Meier said: "As set forth in our memorandum to the Court, in May 2025, Mr. Peters was 19 years old, the complainant was 17 years old, and they were involved in a professional and personal relationship that continued for another year." Meier also questioned the timing of the complaint, linking it to a civil lawsuit the complainant filed against Peters, the Globe reported.

A representative for Peters, Mitchell P. Jackson, told Fox News that Peters denies all charges and allegations against him and had no further comment.

## What prosecutors say

We did not find a public statement from the Cape and Islands District Attorney's Office about the indictment in the reports we reviewed through Friday evening. The charges are described here as they appear in court records reported by the outlets linked above.

## Who is Clavicular?

Peters is a livestreamer and creator in the online "looksmaxxing" scene. The Globe [describes looksmaxxing](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/11/19/metro/who-is-clavicular/) as a pursuit of looking more attractive and raising one's perceived social status, sometimes through extreme measures. News 12 calls it "a trend focused on maximizing physical attractiveness."

His [Kick channel](https://kick.com/clavicular) showed about 463,000 followers when we checked at 6:57 p.m. CT on November 20, 2026. The Boston Globe reported that he has 1 million followers on Instagram and 1.2 million on TikTok.

## Conflicting details in the reports

- **Name:** News 12 spells his first name "Branden." The Boston Globe, WCVB, Boston 25 News, the Cape Cod Times and Fox News spell it "Braden," and the Globe gives his full name as Braden E. Peters. We use Braden.
- **Age:** most outlets, including the Globe, WCVB, News 12 and Fox News, report that he is 20. The Cape Cod Times lists him as 21. His attorney's statement says he was 19 in May 2025.
