Valve's Proton wiki changelog now opens with items **available in Proton Experimental as of 2026-11-10**. This is Valve's official Experimental notes, not a stable Proton 11.0 release.

The changelog is cumulative and still includes all changes from **Proton 11.0-2**, plus earlier Experimental fixes. Compared with the previous "as of 2026-10-30" heading, the November 10 update adds a new "now playable" title and a fresh set of fixes at the top of the list.

## New in the November 10 notes

- **Now playable:** Danganronpa V3: Killing Harmony (added to the existing playable list).
- Fixed **Aliens: Fireteam Elite 2** hanging on the intro cutscene when the scene is allowed to play fully instead of being skipped.
- Fixed **AION 2** crashing when clicking the audio icon in the party info.
- Fixed video playback in **Phantom Blade Zero** at high resolutions.
- Fixed multiplayer in **Catharage**.
- Fixed text to speech not working in **Overwatch**.
- Fixed **Age of Empires: Definitive Edition** crashing when pressing arrow keys in text input fields.
- Fixed **DARK SOULS II: Scholar of the First Sin** not being playable on the **Steam Frame**.
- Fixed bad controller mappings in **ONE PIECE PIRATE WARRIORS 3** with Steam Input enabled.
- Fixed **The Forest** crashing when swapping through multiplayer options.
- Fixed some HID-using peripherals crashing Proton's input stack and making controllers unusable.
- Fixed an Experimental regression that caused **Wild Assault** to crash on launch.
- Fixed the microphone not working in **Among Us 3D** on devices other than Steam Deck.
- Added support for **Nvidia Streamline OTA updates**.
- Updated **Xalia** to **0.5.0.1**, which Valve says should help with idle CPU usage.

These are Valve's changelog claims for the Experimental branch. They are not independent compatibility tests, and Experimental builds can change again.
