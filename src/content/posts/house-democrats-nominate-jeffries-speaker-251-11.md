House Democrats on Wednesday, November 18, nominated Rep. Hakeem Jeffries of New York to be the next speaker of the House. If the full House elects him when the new Congress meets in January, he will be the first Black speaker in U.S. history, as the [AP](https://apnews.com/article/hakeem-jeffries-house-leadership-speaker-democrats-260433358f776473f29151992569f81d), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/5f374b32b07b/world/us/urgent-democrats-nominate-hakeem-jeffries-next-us-house-speaker-2026-11-18/) and [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/democrats-formally-nominate-rep-hakeem-jeffries-paving-way-first-black-rcna609619) all reported.

The House Democratic Caucus [announced the result on X](https://x.com/HouseDemocrats/status/2123076107721187567) at 9:29 AM CT: "House Democrats nominate @RepJeffries for Speaker of the House for the 120th Congress." Jeffries [posted](https://x.com/RepJeffries/status/2123088562900054224) about 50 minutes later: "Thank you House Democrats. Together, we will deliver bold change."

## The vote

The caucus voted behind closed doors by secret ballot. Jeffries won 251-11, according to [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/democrats-formally-nominate-rep-hakeem-jeffries-paving-way-first-black-rcna609619), which cited two sources in the room, and [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/5f374b32b07b/world/us/urgent-democrats-nominate-hakeem-jeffries-next-us-house-speaker-2026-11-18/), [Roll Call](https://rollcall.com/2026/11/18/democrats-nominate-jeffries-to-be-house-speaker-a-historic-first/), [Politico](https://www.politico.com/live-updates/2026/11/18/congress/hakeem-jeffries-speaker-nomination-01172291) and [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/18/nx-s1-6043183/hakeem-jeffries-house-democrats-leadership-votes). No one ran against him.

[Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/19/hakeem-jeffries-speaker-vote-left-progressives) reported that 264 Democratic House members and non-voting delegates were eligible: 251 voted for Jeffries, 11 voted against and two did not vote.

Some of Jeffries' allies had wanted to nominate him by acclamation, which produces no tally. [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/democrats-formally-nominate-rep-hakeem-jeffries-paving-way-first-black-rcna609619) reported that Jeffries asked for a recorded vote instead, so members could voice support or opposition, as the caucus did when it returned Nancy Pelosi to the speakership after the 2018 midterms.

## What Jeffries said

At a press conference after the vote, Jeffries said, according to [his office's transcript](https://democraticleader.house.gov/media/press-releases/speaker-designate-jeffries-were-committed-delivering-change-american-people): "It's a high honor and a distinct privilege to stand before the American people today as the Democratic nominee to be the next Speaker of the United States House of Representatives."

He said House Democrats would focus on the cost of living and on oversight. "We're going to fight to lower the high cost of groceries and gas and housing," he said, and added that "House Democrats will hold the crooks accountable, not for partisan reasons, but for patriotic ones."

Asked about the 11 no votes, Jeffries said, as quoted by [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/democrats-formally-nominate-rep-hakeem-jeffries-paving-way-first-black-rcna609619) and [Roll Call](https://rollcall.com/2026/11/18/democrats-nominate-jeffries-to-be-house-speaker-a-historic-first/): "Our job is to fight MAGA extremism, not each other."

Pelosi, who [Roll Call](https://rollcall.com/2026/11/18/democrats-nominate-jeffries-to-be-house-speaker-a-historic-first/) notes made history as the first female speaker, [posted on X](https://x.com/SpeakerPelosi/status/2123076634555367556): "Congratulations, Speaker-designate Hakeem Jeffries!" Rep. Jim Clyburn of South Carolina, a civil rights movement veteran and one of Jeffries' mentors, told reporters before the vote, "This is a very surreal moment for me," [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/democrats-formally-nominate-rep-hakeem-jeffries-paving-way-first-black-rcna609619) reported.

## The rest of the leadership team

The caucus filled out its leadership for the 120th Congress the same morning, according to posts from the House Democrats account:

- **Majority Leader:** Rep. Katherine Clark of Massachusetts ([House Democrats](https://x.com/HouseDemocrats/status/2123083732215337119)). The [AP](https://apnews.com/article/hakeem-jeffries-house-leadership-speaker-democrats-260433358f776473f29151992569f81d) and [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/democrats-formally-nominate-rep-hakeem-jeffries-paving-way-first-black-rcna609619) say she will be the first woman to hold the job.
- **Majority Whip:** Rep. Pete Aguilar of California ([House Democrats](https://x.com/HouseDemocrats/status/2123089940992995821)). The [AP](https://apnews.com/article/hakeem-jeffries-house-leadership-speaker-democrats-260433358f776473f29151992569f81d) and [Roll Call](https://rollcall.com/2026/11/18/democrats-nominate-jeffries-to-be-house-speaker-a-historic-first/) say he will be the first Latino majority whip.
- **Caucus Chair:** Rep. Ted Lieu of California ([House Democrats](https://x.com/HouseDemocrats/status/2123064402316300421)). [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/democrats-formally-nominate-rep-hakeem-jeffries-paving-way-first-black-rcna609619) says he will be the first Asian American in the role.
- **Assistant Speaker:** Rep. Joe Neguse of Colorado ([House Democrats](https://x.com/HouseDemocrats/status/2123095312302739834)).
- **Caucus Vice Chair:** Rep. Lauren Underwood of Illinois ([House Democrats](https://x.com/HouseDemocrats/status/2123128836527456552)), who beat Rep. Sara Jacobs of California in the only significant contested race, per [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/democrats-formally-nominate-rep-hakeem-jeffries-paving-way-first-black-rcna609619).
- **Democratic Policy and Communications Committee Chair:** Rep. Debbie Dingell of Michigan, elected Thursday, November 19 ([House Democrats](https://x.com/HouseDemocrats/status/2123433278736851221)).

## The no votes

Seven incoming members signed a joint statement saying they voted no: Reps.-elect Darializa Avila Chevalier and Claire Valdez of New York, Adam Hamawy of New Jersey, Frederick Haynes of Texas, Melat Kiros of Colorado, Donavan McKinney of Michigan and Chris Rabb of Pennsylvania, according to [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/democrats-formally-nominate-rep-hakeem-jeffries-paving-way-first-black-rcna609619), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/5f374b32b07b/world/us/urgent-democrats-nominate-hakeem-jeffries-next-us-house-speaker-2026-11-18/) and [Roll Call](https://rollcall.com/2026/11/18/democrats-nominate-jeffries-to-be-house-speaker-a-historic-first/).

"Whether on Medicare for All, stopping ICE terror, getting money out of politics, or ending the genocide in Gaza, our constituents sent us to Congress with a mandate on these issues," the statement said, as quoted by [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/democrats-formally-nominate-rep-hakeem-jeffries-paving-way-first-black-rcna609619). "We have worked to secure commitments from Leader Jeffries in good-faith conversations. While we have seen meaningful progress, there is still work to be done to ensure that the direction our party takes this coming Congress reflects what our constituents are demanding of leadership. Those conversations are ongoing. As such, our vote in caucus today was no."

[Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/19/hakeem-jeffries-speaker-vote-left-progressives) added that Haynes signed the statement but, according to a spokesperson, "narrowly missed the vote" and his "intended vote was no." Axios also reported that Reps.-elect Don Leonard of Ohio and Angela Gonzales-Torres of California said they voted no, and that three no votes were still unaccounted for as of Wednesday night. Because the ballot was secret, VetTV News could not independently confirm how individual members voted.

The Congressional Progressive Caucus leadership, Chair Greg Casar, Deputy Chair Ilhan Omar and Whip Chuy García, [congratulated Jeffries](https://progressives.house.gov/2026/11/cpc-leadership-statement-on-jeffries-nomination) and said the caucus "will work with him to pass the first year of Medicare For All, an end to for-profit immigrant detention centers, and so much more." The statement added: "Between now and January, all CPC members will continue conversations with Leader Jeffries."

## What happens next

The nomination is not the final step. Under the [20th Amendment](https://www.law.cornell.edu/constitution/amendmentxx), terms of House members begin at noon on January 3, and the full House elects its speaker when the new Congress convenes. The [House historian's office](https://history.house.gov/People/Office/Speakers/) says the speaker is elected by the House from candidates chosen by each party's caucus.

[NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/democrats-formally-nominate-rep-hakeem-jeffries-paving-way-first-black-rcna609619) and [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/5f374b32b07b/world/us/urgent-democrats-nominate-hakeem-jeffries-next-us-house-speaker-2026-11-18/) report that Jeffries will need 218 votes on the floor January 3. Reuters says Democrats will hold at least 257 seats, with some races still uncalled, and NBC projects 259. That leaves room for dozens of Democratic defections. As [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/5f374b32b07b/world/us/urgent-democrats-nominate-hakeem-jeffries-next-us-house-speaker-2026-11-18/) notes, the speaker is second in line to the presidency.

On the Republican side, House Republicans last week chose Judiciary Committee Chairman Jim Jordan of Ohio over Majority Leader Steve Scalise to lead them in the minority, after Speaker Mike Johnson said he would not seek to lead the party in the minority, [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/5f374b32b07b/world/us/urgent-democrats-nominate-hakeem-jeffries-next-us-house-speaker-2026-11-18/) reported.

Jeffries, 56, is from Brooklyn. He has served in the House since 2013 and has been the Democratic leader since 2023, according to [Politico](https://www.politico.com/live-updates/2026/11/18/congress/hakeem-jeffries-speaker-nomination-01172291) and [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/democrats-formally-nominate-rep-hakeem-jeffries-paving-way-first-black-rcna609619).
