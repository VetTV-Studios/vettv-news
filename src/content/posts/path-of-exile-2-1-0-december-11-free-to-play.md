Grinding Gear Games says **Path of Exile 2** leaves Early Access for its **1.0 full release on December 11 (PST)** and launches as a **fully free-to-play** action RPG. The studio's August forum announcement, posted by Natalia_GGG, is the primary confirmation of that date and free-to-play shift.

## Classes and campaign

GGG's official YouTube trailers flesh out the launch content package:

- **Acts 5 and 6**, covered in the *A Return to Oriath | Act 5 & 6 Trailer*.
- **The Duelist**, first revealed with the December 11 date and revisited in a Duelist deep-dive forum post that again ties the class to the 1.0 release.
- **The Shadow**, detailed in GGG's *Path of Exile 2: The Shadow Trailer* as a dagger-and-trap class with Stealth and Assassin / Infiltrator ascendancies at launch.

GamingOnLinux summarizes official class notes that match those trailers: Stealth that keeps monsters from seeing you until you are close, traps that can be prepared in Stealth and then triggered in throw order, Duelist stances that empower sword skills, and Ascendancies including Assassin, Infiltrator, Knight, and Master-at-Arms. Treat those mechanical details as studio marketing claims, not hands-on review findings.

GamingOnLinux also cites Acts 5 and 6 adding **24** new areas, **18** unique bosses, and more than **20** Champion Rare encounters, plus item additions, class tweaks, and a passive-skill-tree rework. Those counts come from the report's summary of the update package; they are not independently verified here.

## Linux note

GamingOnLinux reports Path of Exile 2 has long run well on Linux via **Proton / Wine**. That is a Linux-compatibility observation for the Windows client, not a native Linux build announcement from Grinding Gear Games.

Path of Exile 2 remains available through GGG's listed channels, including Steam, the studio's standalone client, PlayStation, Xbox, and the Epic Games Store, per the studio's release communications.
