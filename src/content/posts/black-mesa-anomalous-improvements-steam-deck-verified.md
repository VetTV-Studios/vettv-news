Crowbar Collective [posted "Anomalous Improvements Update"](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/362890/view/1849458702690709) for [Black Mesa](https://store.steampowered.com/app/362890/Black_Mesa/) on November 16 at 12:03 p.m. CT. "Today, we are proud to release a major update featuring visual upgrades, engine optimizations, and a complete interface overhaul," the studio writes.

## Steam Deck Verified

Under the heading "Steam Deck Verified for Field Operations," the post says: "Black Mesa is now officially Steam Deck verified!" The announcement does not reprint Valve's individual compatibility-report checks.

## UI, controllers, visuals, and performance

The studio describes "a streamlined, clutter-free UI built from the ground up for speed and responsiveness, and plug & play controller support," with "full native layout and glyph support for Xbox, PlayStation, and Steam Controllers, alongside refined mouse and keyboard navigation." Full UI modding support is "now live via the Black Mesa Steam Workshop," including a Classic Menu option.

On visuals, the post describes parallax-corrected cubemaps for "local, room-accurate reflections" in the Black Mesa Research Facility and Xen, and "an extensive overhaul to the Source Engine's lighting system," including "Enhanced bounce and indirect lighting," "Outdoor image-based lighting," "Improved vertex and lightmap fidelity on static props," and "Physically accurate light transmission through translucent and glass surfaces."

On performance, Crowbar Collective says it "fine-tuned the engine to deliver significantly better performance across the entire game," with "higher frame rates, smoother gameplay, and solid stability across every chapter, including a fully optimized Xen."

## Mods

The studio says it overhauled how the game uses mods, including fixes for crashes when launching from the menu, campaign and chapter select from the Add-Ons menu, an improved Subscribed tab, and workshop delivery for total-conversion mods "with no installation or moving of files." It adds: "Please note that some existing mods may need updates after our patch." The same post says H.E.C.U. Collective released a Black Mesa: Blue Shift update for compatibility with the latest version, simpler installation, and improved lighting with parallax-corrected cubemaps.
