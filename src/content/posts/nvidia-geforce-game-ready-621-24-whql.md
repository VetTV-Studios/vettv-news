NVIDIA has released [GeForce Game Ready Driver 621.24 WHQL](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/drivers/details/285321/), dated November 12, 2026, in its [Windows 11 / Windows 10 release notes](https://us.download.nvidia.com/Windows/621.24/621.24-geforce-win11-x64-release-notes.pdf). The notes say this edition "lists changes made to the driver since driver version 621.14."

## Supported games

Under "What's New in Version 621.24 WHQL," NVIDIA writes: "This new Game Ready Driver provides the best gaming experience for Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War IV, and Assetto Corsa Rally, and the addition of DLSS 5 with 3D-Guided Neural Rendering to Where Winds Meet."

A [Game Ready Driver article](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/news/warhammer-40000-dawn-of-war-4-geforce-rtx-spark-game-ready-driver/) by Andrew Burnes, dated November 11 on NVIDIA's site, uses slightly more specific wording. It says the new "GeForce RTX and RTX Spark Game Ready Driver includes optimizations and enhancements for Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War IV, supports the addition of DLSS 5 with 3D-Guided Neural Rendering to Where Winds Meet, and supports the addition of DLSS 4.5 Dynamic Multi Frame Generation to Assetto Corsa Rally." The DLSS 4.5 Dynamic Multi Frame Generation detail for Assetto Corsa Rally comes from that article. The release notes do not repeat the DLSS 4.5 phrase.

The article's date is one day earlier than the November 12 release date printed on the driver notes. This story uses November 12 as the driver's release date, as stated in the official notes.

## Fixes

In "Fixed Issues in Version 621.24 WHQL," the notes leave the "Fixed Gaming Bugs" heading without any titles underneath it. Under "Fixed General Bugs," they list:

- "Chaos Vantage: NVIDIA OptiX Denoiser may fail at high resolutions. [6953474]"
- "Windows Photos/Media Player: Incorrect colors with DJI D-Log H.265 video playback."

The notes describe 621.24 as part of the Release 620 driver family for Windows 10 and Windows 11. The details page lists the operating systems as Windows 10 64-bit and Windows 11.
