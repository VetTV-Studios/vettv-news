id Software [posted Update 5 release notes](https://slayersclub.bethesda.net/en-US/news/doom-the-dark-ages-revelations-slaughter-mode-update-5-release-notes) for DOOM: The Dark Ages | Revelations on November 16. The notes are published on Bethesda's Slayers Club site and headed "DOOM: The Dark Ages | Revelations Slaughter Mode Update 5 Release Notes."

## Slaughter Mode

"Update 5 for DOOM: The Dark Ages | Revelations introduces Slaughter Mode; an endless horde survival experience that challenges players to battle through escalating demon encounters across multiple arenas," the notes say. Players "Complete Rounds, earn Skulls and push deeper into each run as you fight to set a new Personal Best."

"The new mode is available at no additional cost to anyone who owns Revelations, the Premium Edition, or the Premium Upgrade of DOOM: The Dark Ages." The notes do not describe Slaughter Mode as included with the base game alone.

The mode is round-based. "Each round features either a randomized or curated encounter." Skulls earned in a run advance a Profile Level and unlock cosmetic rewards and Badge Ranks. Reaching a Round Checkpoint lets players "Secure" current Skulls against death and unlock that round as a later starting point. At a checkpoint, "Risk It" adds a Skull multiplier; the notes say that if you die before the next checkpoint, you "will lose all AT RISK Skulls."

## PC: AMD FSR 4 and Intel frame generation

Under "Game Settings Updates – PC Only," the Video Settings list says the game "Now supports AMD FSR 4 on compatible GPUs, featuring FSR Upscaling 4.1 and Frame Generation 4." It also says "FSR frame generation can now be enabled on Intel GPUs that meet the required hardware and driver capabilities." The notes do not name the specific AMD or Intel GPUs that qualify.

The rest of Update 5.0 includes HUD options for screen damage effects and low-health vignette intensity, a set of weapon and gameplay adjustments, and a list of bug fixes. This article does not reprint that full balance and bug-fix list.
