The official Grok Bot account, [@bot](https://x.com/bot/status/2120946968403095906), posted on November 12 at 12:28 p.m. CT (6:28 p.m. UTC): "Grok Bot on Mac can now use Messages, Mail, Notes, Reminders, Calendar, Location, and Contacts."

[9to5Mac reported](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/12/grok-bot-for-mac-adds-integration-with-apple-mail-messages-notes-and-more/) the same list later that morning, in a story dated 10:40 a.m. PT (12:40 p.m. CT). The article quotes the @bot post and describes the additions as integrations with six Apple apps, plus Location.

The official post does not spell out setup steps, permission prompts, or which macOS versions are required. A public changelog page did not include those details in the static text available for this report, so those points are left out here.
