Spectrum is giving eligible residential internet customers a temporary free speed upgrade around the launch of Grand Theft Auto VI, Charter Communications said in a [November 4 press release](https://corporate.charter.com/newsroom/spectrum-giving-free-gig-speed-lift-thanksgiving-grand-theft-auto).

The offer, called Gig Speed Lift, runs **November 9 to November 30**. Customers with qualifying plans and compatible equipment can activate it in the My Spectrum App. Speeds return to the original tier after November 30, with no change to the bill, Charter said. Restrictions apply, and the offer is subject to change; customers must not have an outstanding obligation to Spectrum.

## What Charter says you get

- Most eligible customers are boosted to Spectrum Internet 1 Gig.
- In select areas, customers who already have 1 Gig move up to 2 Gig.
- Charter says customers with Spectrum Internet Gig can "start playing Grand Theft Auto VI in less than 30 minutes," so game day is "for cruising, not downloading."
- The lift also covers Thanksgiving through Cyber Monday, when households stream, video-call and shop at once.

Charter's timeline puts the lift on **November 9**, says Grand Theft Auto VI pre-load opens **November 10**, the game launches **November 19**, and the lift ends **November 30**.

## Rockstar's dates (slightly different)

Rockstar's [customer support page](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions) says digital pre-orders can begin pre-loading at **local midnight on November 12, 2026**, and that physical code-in-box copies are available from November 12 to support that pre-load. Rockstar's [newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak3ak31a49a221/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-set-to-launch-november-19-2026) sets the launch for **November 19, 2026**. Spectrum's "November 10" pre-load line is Charter's wording; Rockstar's published pre-load date is November 12.

## The 212GB claim

[Dexerto](https://x.com/Dexerto/status/2118425730979709355) (secondary) reported that Spectrum "claims its 1 Gig speeds can download the 212GB game in under 30 minutes." Charter's press release markets the "less than 30 minutes" figure but **does not state a 212GB file size** in the copy we reviewed. Rockstar's public support and store pages we checked also **do not list a download size**. Treat 212GB as a secondary report, not a Rockstar-confirmed figure. Actual download time still depends on Wi-Fi vs. wired, console network limits and store servers.

## Who this is for

This is Spectrum's promotion alone. Rockstar and Take-Two are not part of the offer, and nothing about the game itself changes for customers who take the lift. Dexerto is a secondary outlet summarizing the Charter announcement.

*Opinion:* a free, temporary gig bump is a useful marketing push for a huge console download week — just confirm eligibility in the My Spectrum App before you count on it.
