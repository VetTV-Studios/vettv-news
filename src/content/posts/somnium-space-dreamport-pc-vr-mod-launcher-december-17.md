Somnium Space announced **Dreamport** on November 28 at Somnium Connect 2026, the company's yearly event in Prague. Dreamport runs games you already own inside the Somnium Space social VR platform, with mods layered on top, including PC VR ports of flat games. The company's newsroom, Somnium Times, [posted the announcement](https://somniumtimes.com/2026/11/28/somnium-space-announces-dreamport-bring-any-game-into-your-social-vr-world/) at 7:44 a.m. CT. It says: "Dreamport launches on December 17, 2026."

Dreamport has been announced but isn't out yet. Everything below comes from Somnium's own description and a press presentation. No one has tested it publicly.

## What Dreamport does

Somnium describes three layers: the Somnium platform "wraps around the game," the original game "fully playable rather than recreated," and on top of that "VR ports, add ons, wearables, and other modifications." Your avatar, friends list, camera, tablet and calling tools come with you into the game. Somnium says even single-player games become "multiplayer by default when wrapped in Somnium."

Somnium also makes these claims about how it works:

- **You must own the game.** "Somnium does not host any of the original game files on its servers. Players must own the game and have a valid licence to play it."
- **Ports are small.** A Dreamport is "a set of instructions telling Somnium how to wrap around an existing game," and "these ports are all under 3 MB."
- **Performance.** Somnium claims "no additional performance overhead" and says some games "load faster inside Somnium than they do when launched natively through Steam." That claim hasn't been tested independently.
- **Modding.** Creators get "full C# access" through Somnium's open SDK, the same one used for building worlds. Each Dreamport has a profile, ratings and karma, and Somnium says it will run internal quality checks.

## Free community ports, paid publisher ports

Somnium lists two routes. **Community ports** are free: anyone who owns a game can build a Dreamport, publish it and invite friends. **Published games** come from studios, who can make them free or paid, and "Somnium will take a 10% platform fee from paid content." Either way, you still need your own license for the underlying game.

Flat2VR Studios, which makes official flat-to-VR ports, is the launch partner. Somnium's post says Flat2VR's first titles are **Trombone Champ: Unflattened** and **I Am Your Beast VR**. [Road to VR](https://www.roadtovr.com/somnium-space-pc-vr-mod-launcher/) gives a different first lineup. It says Flat2VR is bringing five paid integrations, Roboquest VR and I Am Your Beast VR in December, then FlatOut 4 VR, WRATH: Aeon of Ruin VR and V-Racer Hoverbike in Q1 2027, with five more later in 2027. Only I Am Your Beast VR appears in both reports, and neither gives prices. We'll update this post when Somnium or Flat2VR confirms the lineup.

In its presentation, Somnium showed mods already running in Dreamport, Road to VR reports. The games include Age of Mythology, Assetto Corsa, Black Ops 2, Fallout 2, GTA Vice City, Halo, Metroid Prime, Phasmophobia, Quake 3, Rec Room, Skyrim and World of Warcraft. Somnium's own post shows Euro Truck Simulator 2, World of Warcraft, Assetto Corsa and Skyrim. Neither source says whether those game publishers approved the mods, and showing a mod in a demo doesn't mean it will be in the launch library.

## Steam Deck and Steam Frame

Somnium says Dreamport will work on "PC VR," "Handheld Steam Deck," "Standalone Steam Frame" and in "2D mode." It hasn't said how a Windows game wrapped in Somnium will run on SteamOS, whether through Proton or some other way. It also hasn't said whether Steam Frame support means running on the headset itself or streaming from a PC. Expect that to vary by game until there are real tests.

## Who can use it

Somnium Space is free to play, but it's adults-only: users have to "complete age verification and be at least 18 years old." Road to VR notes that the platform is coming back to Steam after Valve delisted it in 2021 during its crypto-games purge, and that it has since played down its crypto features. Somnium's own boilerplate still calls it "a blockchain-based virtual reality platform." The [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4891730/) lists a December 2026 release and Windows only. In a Steam post on November 6, Somnium gave December 7 as the date. Dreamport sign-ups are open at [somniumspace.com/dreamport](https://somniumspace.com/dreamport).
