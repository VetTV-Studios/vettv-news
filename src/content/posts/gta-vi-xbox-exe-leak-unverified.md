**This report is unverified.** On November 23, [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/cyberleek-releases-executable-for-gta-6-xbox-build-leaked-last-week-the-torch-passes-to-the-open-source-recomp-and-emulation-projects/) reported that the leaker known as Cyberleek had released the executable for a *Grand Theft Auto VI* Xbox build that had leaked the week before. PC Gamer has **not verified** the file. As of the morning of November 24, we found **no official comment** from Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive on this reported executable.

This article **does not link** to any leaked files, torrents, or download hosts, and it does not describe how to obtain them.

## What PC Gamer reported

[Andy Chalk, writing for PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/cyberleek-releases-executable-for-gta-6-xbox-build-leaked-last-week-the-torch-passes-to-the-open-source-recomp-and-emulation-projects/) on November 23, said Cyberleek "appears to have followed through on their threat to release the executable of the Xbox build of GTA 6 they put into the wild last week." PC Gamer quoted Cyberleek calling the drop "an early Christmas gift" and writing, "The torch passes to the open source recomp and emulation projects."

According to that report, Cyberleek had held back the main executable after leaking a near-complete Xbox build the previous week, tying a later release to either a cryptocurrency market-cap target or November 23, and then released the executable on November 23 after the market-cap target was missed. Those details are PC Gamer's account of Cyberleek's claims. We have not independently confirmed the files, their contents, or their authenticity.

PC Gamer also quoted a lead on an unofficial "Leonida" recompilation project, identified as Shayder, as saying "it will take at least a week" for the project to reach a point where the game would be playable on PC. That is a claim about an unofficial project. It is **not** a confirmation that a playable PC version exists, and *Grand Theft Auto VI* has [not been released for PC](/posts/gta-6-out-now-ps5-xbox-series-launch/) by Rockstar.

## What has not been confirmed

PC Gamer's writer was direct: "I haven't verified whether the released exe is legit, but the previously-released material appears to be and the general consensus is that this is too." The first half of that sentence is the part that matters for this story: **the executable itself has not been verified** by PC Gamer.

As of the morning of November 24, a check of public statements did not turn up a Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive comment on this specific executable release. We are not treating earlier company statements about other leaks as a response to this report.

PC Gamer also reminded readers that software piracy is illegal, and that unofficial files from strangers can contain malware. We agree, and we will not link to the material.

**Our take (opinion):** this is a piracy report, not a verified product story. Until Rockstar or Take-Two speaks, or a reputable outlet verifies the file, the responsible way to read it is as an unverified claim.
