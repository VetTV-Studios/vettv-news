**NBA 2K27 Arcade Edition** is available **today (November 5, 2026)** exclusively on **Apple Arcade** for **iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple TV**, 2K said in its [Newsroom post](https://newsroom.2k.com/news/nbar-2k27-arcade-edition-now-available-on-apple-arcade). Arcade access means no ads and no in-app purchases as part of Apple's subscription.

Developed by **Visual Concepts** and published by **2K** (Take-Two). ESRB rating: **E for Everyone**.

## New on this Arcade Edition

Per 2K:

- **WNBA debut:** all **15 WNBA teams** and **180 active WNBA players** in Quick Match and Blacktop (5v5 team play; 1v1 / 3v3 / 5v5 street).
- **Faster MyCAREER:** MyPLAYER rookies start at **80 OVR**; season story beats run through **12 strategic matchups** toward the Playoffs.
- **Expanded Neighborhood:** Center Terminal for events/quests/seasonal content; Gym for temporary weekly buffs (speed, acceleration, vertical, strength, stamina); Barbershop and Tattoo Studio for customization.
- **Other modes:** The Association (GM/coach), NBA Eras (iconic dynasties from '90s Bulls to today's Warriors), multiplayer, NBA Quick Match, and Blacktop. Game Center supports play with friends.

## Controllers

2K lists Xbox, PlayStation DualShock, DualSense, and MFi-compatible controllers as supported enhancements on Arcade devices.

*Opinion:* shipping the full WNBA set on day one of an Arcade Edition is the real headline—MyCAREER's 80 OVR start just shortens the grind on a subscription tier.
