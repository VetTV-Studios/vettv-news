Meta's **November 6, 2026** research post announces specialized versions of existing models trained using **Muse Wonder**, rolling out to people using **Muse and Meta AI**. It does not say that every user is receiving the full Muse Wonder model series.

## Active Memory

Meta says Active Memory helps its assistants retain important information and recognize when past context matters. The company describes training models to distinguish temporary from permanent information, reason across time, and separate relevant context from stale details.

On its **Active Memory evaluation**, Meta reports **90%** for the current Muse model versus **76%** previously, measured as tasks correctly using critical context. It separately describes a **2.4x reduction in forgetting critical information**. These are Meta's evaluation results and wording, not independently reproduced measurements by VetTV News.

## Active Focus

Meta describes Active Focus as helping Muse keep working toward a goal when unexpected problems arise: trying harder, recovering, or finding another route. It reports a **67% improvement** in completing everyday tasks with unexpected issues.

The company also says the specialized models handle multitasking, mid-task steering, and clarification better, balancing independent work with live collaboration. For Meta AI, it describes stronger problem breakdown, follow-up questions, and conversational course changes.

## What the numbers do—and do not—show

The post's **Personal Superintelligence Index** chart lists **47%** for the current Muse model and **38%** for the previous one. That is a nine-percentage-point difference, not evidence that an assistant succeeds on 47% of every user's real-world tasks.

All performance comparisons here are **vendor-reported**. The short announcement does not provide exact specialized model identifiers, a complete country/device rollout schedule, or enough detail to independently reproduce the claimed gains. No universal memory guarantee or automatic completion of every task is implied.
