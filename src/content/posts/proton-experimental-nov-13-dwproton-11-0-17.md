Two Proton-family updates landed on November 13: an official Proton Experimental changelog refresh, and a separate community build called DWProton. They are not the same project.

## Proton Experimental, as of November 13

The [Proton changelog on Valve's GitHub wiki](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/Proton/wiki/Changelog), in a section headed "Available in Proton Experimental (as of 2026-11-13)," includes ArcheAge Chronicles among titles marked "now playable." The same section lists "Fixed Warhammer 40,000: Darktide not working in windowed mode," "Fixed Deep Rock Galactic hitching when clicking mouse," and "Fixed VRChat news videos having wavy colors during playback."

That Experimental list is cumulative. It also restates older items already described in earlier changelogs, including a Phantom Blade Zero video fix covered previously. This article is only highlighting the November 13 heading and the newly listed items above, not reprinting the full Experimental log. There was no new Proton stable release in this window.

## DWProton 11.0-17, a community build

Separately, the community project [DWProton released 11.0-17](https://dawn.wine/dawn-winery/dwproton/releases/tag/dwproton-11.0-17) on November 13 at 9:12 a.m. CT (15:12 UTC). DWProton is a community Proton build, not an official Valve release.

The project's notes say it "Updated Proton and components to `cachyos-11.0-20261107-proton-dev`," "Forced `SteamOS=1` only for Wuthering Waves' launcher, should help after the 3.8 update," and "Fixed white in-game browser pages in Wuthering Waves after the 3.8 update," pointing to [issue 86](https://dawn.wine/dawn-winery/dwproton/issues/86).

The same notes add that there have been reports `SteamOS=1` should also fix the browser-page issue, "but as of right now it also breaks FSR3 compatibility." That FSR3 warning is the project's own. This article does not independently confirm the Wuthering Waves fixes or the FSR3 tradeoff.
