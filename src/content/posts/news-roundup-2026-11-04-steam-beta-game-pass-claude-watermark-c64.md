## Steam Client Beta (November 3)

Valve's November 3 Steam Client update is beta-only. It's on the Preview/Beta channel and not out to everyone yet. Changes:

- Fixes for Steam windows briefly flashing black.
- Achievement Sets:
  - Completed sets now show when sorting the library by achievement completion.
  - Achievement totals count all unlocked achievements again.
  - The rarity threshold is fixed.
  - The play bar's Achievements button opens the achievements panel.
- Lower thumbstick touch sensitivity in the Steam Controller firmware, to make early touch detection less likely.

Microsoft released Xbox PC app version 2612.1001.21.0 to PC Gaming Preview members at 4:00 p.m. PT on November 3. The release notes list only "various performance and stability fixes."

## Claude text watermark expands

Anthropic's November 3 platform release notes say text generated by Claude Mythos 5, Claude Opus 4.7 and Claude Sonnet 4.6 now carries Anthropic's text watermark. One caveat: on Sonnet 4.6, text from a request that sets `temperature`, `top_p` or `top_k` to a non-default value might not carry it. Anthropic's help article says watermark detection is in private preview for eligible organizations.

## Grok Bot business calls (reported)

*This is based on coverage. We found no official x.ai announcement.* Teslarati reports that Grok Bot users can now have the bot call US businesses on their behalf, citing creator Peter Yang and posts from Elon Musk. Teslarati also says calling works only with US numbers for now. What we could confirm: SpaceXSI's Grok Bot Terms have a Phone Calls section. It says calls identify themselves as made by a virtual assistant on the user's behalf, may be recorded, and can't be used for marketing, mass calls or impersonation.

## Retro

- **C64 Ultimate goes retail:** Commodore says it has signed an exclusive retail deal with distributor Colby to bring the C64 Ultimate to stores across Europe and parts of Africa and Asia. The company adds, "We're working on the USA too!" A US release isn't confirmed. (Via Time Extension.)
- **PS5SX2 2.30:** a new version of this PCSX2-based homebrew PS2 emulator for PS5, from developer Sword. According to the developer's post, frame generation now reaches 120 fps on original PS5 consoles below firmware 9.00, up from a 40/80 fps limit, with lower latency and faster emulation. The emulator requires a modified console. VetTV News doesn't provide setup instructions. (Via VideoCardz.)

## Mobile

**The Farmer Was Replaced** is out on iOS and Android. It's a programming game where you write Python-like code to automate a farm. Pocket Gamer reports a free trial, then a one-time $8.99 purchase that unlocks everything, including The Miner Was Replaced DLC.
