Elon Musk posted a Grok 4.8 status update on **November 10** at **2026-11-10 09:35:59 UTC** (**3:35 a.m. CT**). There is **no accompanying xAI blog post or published benchmark suite** for this update. Every claim below is what Musk said.

Replying to a question about when to expect Grok 4.8, Musk wrote:

> We have dumped JAX/SGLang and rewritten the entire RL and inference stack from scratch in C/C++, which took some extra time.
>
> Training (aka pre-training) is done. Lot of cleanup was needed on the SFT data to get that mostly right. Now, 4.8 is in RL and the evals are starting to climb. We will do a soft rollout to Bot users later this week and see how it goes.
>
> Grok 4.8 will bear no resemblance to 4.7 (thankfully). It is not yet incredible, but, this time, the foundations are built right, so I expect it to evolve to be something special.

In a follow-up at **2026-11-10 10:22:36 UTC** (**4:22 a.m. CT**), asked how Grok 4.8 would compare to Opus 5.5, Musk wrote:

> Not as good. Opus 5.5 is a masterpiece.
>
> But Grok 5 should exceed Opus 5.5 and maybe that happens in 2 or 3 months, with continued acceleration thereafter.

Those timing and quality comparisons are Musk's projections, not independently tested results.
