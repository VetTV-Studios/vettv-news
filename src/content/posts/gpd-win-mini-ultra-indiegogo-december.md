GPD's next pocket-sized clamshell PC has a launch window. In an [X post on November 18](https://x.com/softwincn/status/2122993646429732911) at 4:01 a.m. CT, GPD shared a preview of the **WIN Mini Ultra** on Indiegogo and said, "Crowdfunding starts in December. Shipping begins promptly after it wraps up."

GPD hasn't announced a price or an exact start date for the campaign. [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GPD-confirms-global-launch-details-for-new-ultra-compact-PC-with-Arc-B390-graphics-and-64-GB-RAM.1453391.0.html) reported the December window on November 18 and noted that the launch runs through Indiegogo rather than the GPD Store.

## What GPD has listed so far

The [Indiegogo campaign preview](https://www.indiegogo.com/en/projects/gpdhk/gpd-win-mini-ultra-clamshell-handheld), posted by GPD HK, is labeled "a campaign preview," so details could still change. Its summary lists:

- **Processor:** Intel Arc G3 Extreme
- **Screen:** 8-inch, 1920x1200 at 120Hz, with VRR support
- **Memory and storage:** 32GB of LPDDR5X at 8533 MT/s and a 1TB SSD
- **Ports:** two USB4 v2.0 ports, with support for GPD's G1 and G2 external GPUs
- **Controls:** capacitive thumbsticks and linear Hall-effect triggers

GPD also has a [product page](https://www.gpd.hk/gpdwinminiultra) up for the device.

## What comes from reports, not GPD

Outlets reported several more details first, and GPD's own product page now lists them. Notebookcheck says the WIN Mini Ultra will come with **32GB or 64GB** of RAM and a **56Wh** battery, and refers to the chip's integrated graphics as **Arc B390**. The Indiegogo preview we saw shows only the 32GB configuration. In an [earlier report](https://www.notebookcheck.net/New-GPD-Win-Mini-Ultra-previewed-before-release-with-VRR-display-and-Intel-Arc-B390-graphics.1448794.0.html) on November 12, Notebookcheck cited a leak putting the device at 182.5 x 133 x 30 mm and 845 g. GPD's product page now lists 32GB or 64GB of LPDDR5x, Arc B390 graphics, a 56Wh battery, 182.5 x 133 x 30 mm and 845 g, matching those reports. The Indiegogo preview still shows only the 32GB model with the Arc G3 Extreme name.

## Before you back it

Crowdfunding isn't the same as buying from a store. GPD's post promises prompt shipping after the campaign, but it doesn't give a ship date, and Notebookcheck expects few units to ship before early 2027 because campaigns usually run about a month. Read Indiegogo's terms and GPD's reward details once the campaign opens.

## Opinion: one to watch, not one to rush

*This section is opinion.* An 8-inch 120Hz screen and Intel's newest integrated graphics in a clamshell this small is an interesting combination, and the dual USB4 v2.0 ports make it more flexible than most handhelds. Without a price, though, there's no way to judge it. Notebookcheck lists the current WIN Mini 2025 at $1,922 on Amazon, so price will matter. It's also a very different pitch from the cheaper handheld Lenovo is [rumored to be working on](/posts/lenovo-legion-go-beam-leak-rumor/). We'll come back to it when GPD posts pricing and the campaign goes live.
