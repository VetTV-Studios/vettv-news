**Update, November 20, 2026:** GPD has posted Indiegogo pricing. In an [X post on November 19](https://x.com/softwincn/status/2123343717386113524) at 3:12 a.m. CT, the company's @softwincn account said, "The final IGG price for the WIN Mini Ultra is $1699, 32GB+1TB edition, shipping included. US buyers also get tax covered."

A "Price list" graphic attached to that post, which we opened on the morning of November 20, lists IGG prices of **$1699** for "GPD WIN Mini Ultra" with the specification "G3E+32GB+1TB," and **$2149** for "GPD WIN Mini Ultra + GPD G2" with the specification "G3E+32GB+1TB + GPD G2."

On memory costs, GPD wrote: "You all know how crazy memory prices have been. DDR5 prices went up another 44% in November compared to August." The same post adds, "the way things are going, there’s no telling how wild prices will get, but all we can do is seize the moment." GPD also said, "The campaign starts in December, shipping begins promptly after it wraps up, no need to waiting."

The [Indiegogo campaign page](https://www.indiegogo.com/en/projects/gpdhk/gpd-win-mini-ultra-clamshell-handheld) was still labeled "a campaign preview" when we checked on November 20, and the text we could read there did not list the $1,699 or $2,149 figures. Those prices come from GPD's November 19 post and the graphic attached to it. GPD still has not given an exact campaign start date.

GPD's next pocket-sized clamshell PC first got a launch window in an [X post on November 18](https://x.com/softwincn/status/2122993646429732911) at 4:01 a.m. CT, when GPD shared a preview of the **WIN Mini Ultra** on Indiegogo and said, "Crowdfunding starts in December. Shipping begins promptly after it wraps up." [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GPD-confirms-global-launch-details-for-new-ultra-compact-PC-with-Arc-B390-graphics-and-64-GB-RAM.1453391.0.html) reported the December window on November 18 and noted that the launch runs through Indiegogo rather than the GPD Store.

## What GPD has listed so far

The [Indiegogo campaign preview](https://www.indiegogo.com/en/projects/gpdhk/gpd-win-mini-ultra-clamshell-handheld), posted by GPD HK, is labeled "a campaign preview," so details could still change. Its summary lists:

- **Processor:** Intel Arc G3 Extreme
- **Screen:** 8-inch, 1920x1200 at 120Hz, with VRR support
- **Memory and storage:** 32GB of LPDDR5X at 8533 MT/s and a 1TB SSD
- **Ports:** two USB4 v2.0 ports, with support for GPD's G1 and G2 external GPUs
- **Controls:** capacitive thumbsticks and linear Hall-effect triggers

GPD also has a [product page](https://www.gpd.hk/gpdwinminiultra) up for the device. The November 19 price-list graphic also shows a specs line that includes "32GB / 64GB RAM."

## What outlets reported first

Outlets reported several more details first, and GPD's own product page now lists them. Notebookcheck says the WIN Mini Ultra will come with **32GB or 64GB** of RAM and a **56Wh** battery, and refers to the chip's integrated graphics as **Arc B390**. The Indiegogo preview we saw shows only the 32GB configuration in its summary. In an [earlier report](https://www.notebookcheck.net/New-GPD-Win-Mini-Ultra-previewed-before-release-with-VRR-display-and-Intel-Arc-B390-graphics.1448794.0.html) on November 12, Notebookcheck cited a leak putting the device at 182.5 x 133 x 30 mm and 845 g. GPD's product page now lists 32GB or 64GB of LPDDR5x, Arc B390 graphics, a 56Wh battery, 182.5 x 133 x 30 mm and 845 g, matching those reports. The Indiegogo preview still shows only the 32GB model with the Arc G3 Extreme name.

## Before you back it

Crowdfunding isn't the same as buying from a store. GPD's posts promise prompt shipping after the campaign, but they don't give a ship date, and Notebookcheck has said campaigns usually run about a month. Read Indiegogo's terms and GPD's reward details once the campaign opens.

## Opinion: a price, still a wait

*This section is opinion.* We now have GPD's own Indiegogo prices, which is the missing piece from our first report. $1,699 for 32GB and 1TB, with shipping included, is the figure to judge against other clamshell handhelds, including the WIN Mini 2025 price Notebookcheck previously listed. The G2 bundle at $2,149 is a different proposition, and the campaign still has no exact start date. It's also a very different pitch from the cheaper handheld Lenovo is [rumored to be working on](/posts/lenovo-legion-go-beam-leak-rumor/). We'll come back to it when the campaign actually opens.
