**The short version:** [VideoCardz](https://videocardz.com/newz/rtx-5090-scalper-lists-140000-worth-of-gpus-just-as-prices-start-to-fall) reported that **some GeForce RTX 5090 listings have dropped by as much as 21% in just over two weeks**, as a large Ontario Facebook Marketplace stash of the cards was listed for about **CA$9,500 to CA$10,000** each (roughly **$6,600 to $7,000**). Retail stock has also returned. On **November 15**, [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/gpus/nvidia-rtx-5090-stock-returns-to-newegg-with-21-models-but-scarcity-pricing-keeps-the-cheapest-air-cooled-cards-at-least-three-times-msrp) counted **21 RTX 5090 models** at Newegg, with the cheapest air-cooled cards still at least three times NVIDIA's **$1,999** starting price. We have not independently tracked those listings, and we are not saying Facebook Marketplace prices as a whole fell 21%.

## The Ontario Marketplace listing

On November 23, VideoCardz, [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/gpus/canadian-scalper-places-40-nvidia-rtx-5090s-on-facebook-marketplace-for-over-usd6-600-apiece-ironically-still-cheaper-than-memory-expresss-10-000-cad-retail-price-purportedly-selling-every-unit-quite-quickly), [PCMag](https://www.pcmag.com/news/reseller-lists-over-40-rtx-5090-graphics-cards-for-6600-plus-each), and [EtekNix](https://www.eteknix.com/scalpers-list-140k-in-rtx-5090s-as-retailers-cut-prices/) reported a Facebook Marketplace listing in Ontario offering a large stockpile of RTX 5090 cards, including models from ASUS, Gigabyte, MSI, and Zotac.

The outlets did not agree on the count:

- VideoCardz said the listing showed **more than 20** boxes, with asking prices from **CA$9,500 to CA$10,000** (about **$6,620 to $6,967**), plus an extra **CA$500** (about **$348**) for a sealed unit. At those prices, VideoCardz estimated the collection could be worth around **$140,000**. [NDTV Profit](https://www.ndtvprofit.com/world/is-nvidia-rtx-5090-scalping-bubble-bursting-a-140-000-gpu-stash-tests-the-market-12371959), citing that reporting, also described a listing of more than 20 cards for as much as $7,000 each.
- Tom's Hardware, in a November 23 article by Bruno Ferreira, described an Ontario listing of **more than 40** RTX 5090s, with the cheapest priced at **CA$9,500** (over **$6,600**) and an extra **CA$500** (about **$350**) for a sealed box. PCMag, citing Tom's Hardware, also reported "over 40" cards at $9,500 CAD each (around $6,600).

We have not independently viewed the listing. The 20-versus-40 count is not settled. We are not naming the seller, and we have not confirmed why the cards were listed.

## Retail stock is back, at still-high prices

NVIDIA's [GeForce RTX 5090 product page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/graphics-cards/50-series/rtx-5090/) lists a starting price of **$1,999**. That matches the launch price NVIDIA [announced](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-blackwell-geforce-rtx-50-series-opens-new-world-of-ai-computer-graphics) in January 2025, when it said the card would be available on January 30 at $1,999. NVIDIA has not announced a change to that U.S. starting price in the material we reviewed.

On November 15, Tom's Hardware's Mark Tyson reported that Newegg had **21 RTX 5090 SKUs** available, **11** of them sold and shipped by Newegg, with a one-per-customer order limit. The lowest prices he found were **$6,599**, but those applied to a water-cooled Aorus model and a Gigabyte AI BOX eGPU. For a standard air-cooled card, the cheapest he listed was an Aorus RTX 5090 Master at **$6,659**. That is about **3.3 times** the $1,999 starting price, which is what Tom's Hardware meant by prices remaining in "3x MSRP+" territory.

Tom's Hardware also wrote that, about a month earlier, reports of **$10,000-plus** prices for the flagship card "seemed to become the norm" after rumors that the RTX 5090 and RTX 5080 were being withdrawn from retail. We are treating that as Tom's Hardware's account of earlier street prices, not as a current price check.

Those Newegg figures are from November 15. We have not independently confirmed what Newegg is charging today.

## Canadian retail compared with the listing

VideoCardz and EtekNix reported that Newegg Canada listed the ASUS TUF Gaming RTX 5090 OC for **CA$9,499.99**, matching the Marketplace listing's starting ask, with the card sold and shipped by the retailer. On November 23, Tom's Hardware said an RTX 5090 at Memory Express was around **CA$10,000**, and that the cheapest card at Newegg Canada was going for **CA$9,300** as part of a combo that included a motherboard, a case, and a power supply.

Those are reported retail prices from late November, not an offer from VetTV News. We are not linking to product pages.

## A sharper drop in Japan earlier in November

Separately, [WCCFTech](https://wccftech.com/geforce-rtx-5090-and-rtx-5080-witness-a-sudden-price-drop-of-up-to-22-in-japan-but-its-unlikely-due-to-jensens-resignation/) reported on November 14 that some RTX 5090 and RTX 5080 prices in Japan fell sharply. Citing Japanese retailer Dospara and the Kakaku.com price-comparison site, it said a Palit RTX 5090 GameRock 32 GB fell from **1,288,000 yen** (about **$8,062**) to **1,048,000 yen** (about **$6,560**) in a single day, an **18.6%** cut. ITmedia, as cited by WCCFTech, reported a **22.38%** reduction on the same GPU at Dospara's Akihabara store, to **999,800 yen**. A GeForce RTX 5080 that had peaked at **359,800 yen** a few days earlier was listed at **299,800 yen** (about **$1,876**), a **16.7%** cut.

WCCFTech said it found no direct link between those cuts and NVIDIA's then-recent leadership change, and cited Gazlog reporting that prices had already peaked at the end of October, with RTX 5090 supply returning around November 3.

**Our take (opinion):** Some listings are coming down from extreme highs, and a large resale stash hitting Marketplace at the same moment retailers are putting cards back on shelves is a sign the market may have lost some pricing power. That is not the same as prices returning to NVIDIA's $1,999 starting price. The mid-November Newegg air-cooled floor of $6,659 is still more than three times that figure, and we have not independently confirmed a broader secondary-market index, only the figures the outlets above reported.
