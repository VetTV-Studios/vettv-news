Xbox's ID@Xbox team published its November Indie Selects on November 4, a curated list of six games also collected in the Indie Select Hub in the Xbox Store and at xbox.com/IndieSelects. The team also says a first-ever December Selects is coming next month.

**About platforms, dates and Game Pass:** Xbox's post doesn't give platforms or release dates for these games. Its store cards show a price for each, and the only membership label on any of them is "Free Trial" on Graveyard Keeper 2. None is labeled as part of Game Pass. Prices below are as the post's store cards showed them on November 5 and may change.

- **Silver Pines** (Team17, $24.99): a survival horror game the post compares to Resident Evil and Twin Peaks. It's from Wych Elm, a studio of ex-Arrowhead developers, and uses rotoscoped characters. An in-game camera pins photos of clues to your map. The post puts it at about 15 hours.
- **Graveyard Keeper 2** (tinyBuild, $24.99, Free Trial label): a dark medieval sim RPG in which the town is overrun by zombies. You don't need to have played the first game, and the Donkey is back.
- **Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun 2** (Devolver Digital and Big Fan Games, $24.99): a retro-style FPS with two playable characters, Ultramarine Malum Caedo and Sister of Battle Nyra Veyrath, plus a branching campaign.
- **Colorbound** (Whitethorn Games, $14.99): a watercolor-style puzzle platformer about Anku, a young Aymara boy who reunites his late grandfather's band. It started as a 2025 Latin American Games Showcase game jam entry by Chilean developer Isaías Arrué.
- **Ratatan** (Game Source Entertainment, $34.99): a roguelike rhythm action game, described as the spiritual successor to Patapon.
- **Tenebris Somnia** (New Blood Interactive, $24.99): a 2D adventure mixed with survival horror and live-action cutscenes. You play an indie filmmaker named Julia.

*Opinion, as labeled by Xbox:* each pick comes with a short personal recommendation from a member of the ID@Xbox team. We've summarized the facts rather than repeating their verdicts.
