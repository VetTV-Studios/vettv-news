Apple is putting daily caps on its most demanding AI features. A new [Apple Support page](https://support.apple.com/en-us/127901), published November 16, says some Siri AI and Apple Intelligence features "rely on powerful server-side models and have daily usage limits to ensure a high-quality, reliable experience for everyone." To raise those limits, Apple says, "you can upgrade your iCloud+ plan."

Apple's wording on timing is specific. It says Siri AI and Apple Intelligence features "are available for free" starting with the 2027 software releases, and that some features "have limits where increased access is available with eligible iCloud+ plans, starting with the 27.3 release." [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/16/apple-icloud-paid-siri-ai-upgrade/) reported on November 16 that the iOS 27.3, iPadOS 27.3 and macOS 27.3 betas are where Apple is starting to test the limits. **Apple has not given a release date for 27.3**, and we are not guessing one.

## What counts toward a limit

Apple lists these feature categories and says the limited features "include, but aren't limited to" them:

- **Siri AI**
- **Images:** "Intelligent Photo Editing Tools including Clean Up, Extend, and Spatial Reframing, and Image Playground"
- **Workflows and automation:** "AFM Cloud and Cloud Pro models in Shortcuts"
- **Third-party apps** "using Apple Foundation Models in Private Cloud Compute"

The page says, "Limits may vary by feature category, request complexity, system demand, system policies, and other factors." Apple doesn't publish the actual number of requests in a standard daily limit.

## What happens when Siri hits the limit

Apple says, "Siri AI includes a generous daily usage limit." When you reach it, you will be notified, and you "can still make everyday Siri requests like 'Set a timer for 3 minutes' or 'Text Mark that Annika's party is at 123 Main Street.'"

Advanced requests stop. In Apple's words: "If you reach your limit, advanced requests are paused until your limit resets or you upgrade to an eligible iCloud+ plan." Apple gives two examples of an advanced request: "Text Mark the address for Annika's party, and tell him what time we need to leave from home to make it on time" and "Add a summary of my San Francisco trip note to this email draft."

Apple also says, "Each device has its own daily usage limit per feature category."

[9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/18/ios-27-3-adds-siri-ai-usage-limits-heres-what-counts-toward-your-limit/) reported on November 18 that a beta tester on X said complex Siri questions stopped working shortly after he hit the limit, while basic requests kept working. 9to5Mac also published a list of criteria it attributes to a leaker, describing how Siri supposedly decides whether a request is "advanced." Apple has not published or confirmed that list, so we aren't reporting it as fact.

## When limits reset

According to Apple, "All feature categories reset daily at the same time, and limits do not roll over to subsequent days." Your limit "resets around midnight in your current time zone," with three exceptions:

- If you're traveling internationally, it resets around midnight in the central time zone of the country or region of your Apple Account.
- If you share an iCloud+ plan through Family Sharing, it resets based on the time zone of the person who bought the plan.
- If you're not signed in to your Apple Account on the device, it resets about 24 hours after you started using that feature category.

## What iCloud+ adds

Apple says more access starts with an iCloud+ plan "with at least 200 GB of storage." After a device uses up its own daily limit, it moves to an increased limit that is "shared by all devices signed in to your Apple Account and across any Family members sharing your plan, on a first-come, first-served basis."

Apple's table for "Select Apple Intelligence Features and Siri AI":

- **Free and iCloud+ 50 GB:** Standard Device Limit
- **iCloud+ 200 GB:** Up to 2X Standard Device Limit
- **iCloud+ 2 TB:** Up to 5X Standard Device Limit
- **iCloud+ 6 TB:** Up to 10X Standard Device Limit
- **iCloud+ 12 TB:** Up to 20X Standard Device Limit

Shortcuts using the AFM Cloud Pro model have a separate row. The free tier and the 50 GB plan get no access. The 200 GB plan gets "Access to AFM Cloud Pro Model," and the 2 TB, 6 TB and 12 TB plans get up to 5X, 10X and 20X access. Apple also says some Apple Intelligence features in the Home app, such as video summaries, work with up to 1 camera on 2 TB, 2 cameras on 6 TB and 5 cameras on 12 TB.

For reference, Apple's [U.S. iCloud+ pricing](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108047) is $0.99 a month for 50 GB, $2.99 for 200 GB, $9.99 for 2 TB, $29.99 for 6 TB and $59.99 for 12 TB. MacRumors reported there is no change in plan pricing.

## Where the higher limits aren't offered

Apple says increased access to Siri AI with iCloud+ "is not available in Brazil, the EU, UK, or South Korea." Increased access to select Apple Intelligence features with iCloud+ "is not available in China, Brazil, the EU, UK, or South Korea." Apple adds that the standard amount of access and other plan features "may change over time."

## Opinion: a meter on Siri

*This section is opinion.* Usage caps are normal for chatbots like ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude, so it isn't shocking to see one here. It is a change for Siri, which people have always treated as a free part of the phone. Apple's choices soften the blow: everyday requests keep working, the limit is per device, and the upgrade is an iCloud plan many people already pay for. The weak spot is transparency. Apple calls the free limit "generous" without giving a number and only gives two examples of an advanced request. That makes it hard for anyone to know whether they need to pay more before they hit the wall.
