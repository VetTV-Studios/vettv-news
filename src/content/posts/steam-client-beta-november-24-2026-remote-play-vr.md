Valve posted [Steam Client Beta - November 24th](https://steamcommunity.com/groups/SteamClientBeta/announcements/detail/713418568234960323) at **7:27 p.m. CT on November 24**. The Steam Client Beta RSS feed lists that time as 1:27 a.m. UTC on November 25, and names the author as jmccaskey. This is a bug-fix follow-up to the larger [November 18 Steam Client Beta](/posts/steam-client-beta-november-18-2026-ui-mods/). It applies only to people who have opted into the Steam Client Beta. Valve has not posted a new stable Steam client with these changes.

## General

Valve says it:

- "Fixed a Steamworks SDK regression causing a crash for games calling IsP2PPacketAvailable with a null pointer"
- "Fixed issue where a Steam Workshop item would sometimes pick the wrong version if the user was on the default game branch."

Valve does not name the games affected by the Steamworks crash.

## Library

Two review-editing fixes landed in this build:

- "Editing a review for a game in your library will now properly clear the "Early Access" flag from the review"
- "The "Attach PC Specs to this review" checkbox now accurately reflects whether PC specs were already attached to an existing review when editing it"

## Remote Play

Under Remote Play, the notes have a single item: **"Fix several VR streaming crashes."** Valve does not list which headsets, games, or crash signatures were involved.

The November 24 notes do not mention Steam Input or CSS class-name changes. Those were part of the [November 18 beta](/posts/steam-client-beta-november-18-2026-ui-mods/).
