NVIDIA [said](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/rtx-spark-desktop-local-ai-available-now/) on November 18, 2026, that new RTX Spark compact desktops from ASUS, Dell and MSI "are available today," bringing "local AI agents, image and video generation, and model fine-tuning to a dedicated Windows PC." The [blog post](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/rtx-spark-desktop-local-ai-available-now/), by Justin Walker, says the systems join "the RTX Spark family of laptops available today."

"Mini PCs from Acer, GIGABYTE and HP are coming soon, along with the Microsoft Surface RTX Spark Dev Box."

NVIDIA describes the hardware this way: "RTX Spark desktops are equipped with a 20-core NVIDIA Grace CPU, an NVIDIA Blackwell RTX GPU with 6,144 CUDA cores and up to 128GB of unified memory, delivering up to 1 petaflop of AI performance. Ten-gigabit Ethernet connectivity enables fast transfers of large datasets, model files and creative assets between RTX Spark compact desktops and networked storage."

The post also says the systems "provide room for larger local models alongside RTX-accelerated creative and development tools," and it lists these capabilities as what "this advanced hardware unlocks":

- "Running massive 120-billion-parameter large language models locally"
- "Rendering 90GB+ 3D scenes in real time"
- "Smoothly editing 4:2:2 video in real time"

Those characterizations are NVIDIA's. The blog post does not list prices.

NVIDIA names two of the new machines: "RTX Spark compact desktops like the ASUS ProArt GR1X mini PC are built to run personal AI agents 24/7," and it includes a caption for "The MSI EdgeMesa N AI+ Series RTX Spark compact desktop." It does not name a Dell model.

## The ASUS ProArt GR1X

ASUS's [ProArt GR1X product page](https://www.asus.com/us/displays-desktops/mini-pcs/proart-mini-pc-series/proart-gr1x-mini-pc/) describes a compact 150 × 150 × 51 mm chassis, "about 1.1 liters," with a 20-core NVIDIA Grace CPU, a 6,144-core Blackwell RTX GPU, up to 128GB of LPDDR5X-9400 unified memory, a 64GB option, up to 1 petaflop of FP4 AI performance, 10GbE LAN, Wi-Fi 7, and cooling "designed to sustain up to 140W TDP." It says the system ships with Windows 11. The product page does not state a price.

ASUS also [announced](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261118973257/en/ASUS-Announces-Availability-of-ProArt-GR1X) the ProArt GR1X's availability on November 18. We were not able to load the full text of that release while reporting this story.

In its [November 17 fiscal third-quarter results](/posts/nvidia-q3-fy2027-revenue-115-8-billion/), NVIDIA said it had "reinvented Windows PCs with NVIDIA RTX Spark laptops, now shipping from ASUS, Dell, HP, Lenovo, Microsoft Surface and MSI." Today's announcement adds compact desktops from some of those partners.
