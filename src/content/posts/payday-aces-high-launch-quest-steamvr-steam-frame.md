PAYDAY: Aces High, the officially licensed VR co-op heist game set in Starbreeze's PAYDAY universe, launches today, November 5, on Meta Quest, SteamVR and Steam Frame. Developer Fast Travel Games announced the date and the $24.99 USD price in October.

**Launch status (checked 8 AM CT, Nov 5):** the Meta Quest store lists the game as "Coming soon," with release at 7 AM PST (9 AM CT). The Steam page still showed "Coming soon" with no price. We'll update this once the stores show it as available.

## What it is

From the publisher's announcement:

- **Crews of up to four** plan a job, build loadouts from weapons, gadgets and skills, and choose whether to stay quiet or go loud.
- **Story:** after business titan Warren Jupiter frames them, the players team up as The Aces to get revenge.
- **Four Aces:** the Mastermind (Hearts), the Ghost (Clubs), the Technician (Diamonds) and the Enforcer (Spades).
- **Gear:** assault rifles, SMGs, shotguns and pistols, plus gadgets such as a grapple and sentry guns, unlocked with cash and rep.
- **Playtest bonus:** players who joined the final playtest get an exclusive Kitty Mask in the full game.

Steam's store page lists the game as VR-only with online co-op and cross-platform multiplayer.

## Steam Frame

The PAYDAY team said in August that the Steam Frame version is the same version as on the SteamVR store page, so it's one Steam purchase for both.
