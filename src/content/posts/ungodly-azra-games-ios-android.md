An official [Azra Games Games Press release](https://www.gamespress.com/CONSOLE-GRADE-CARNAGE-IN-YOUR-POCKET-AZRA-GAMES-DARK-FANTAS-Azra-Games) dated **November 11, 2026**, is titled "CONSOLE-GRADE CARNAGE IN YOUR POCKET, AZRA GAMES' DARK FANTASY RPG UNGODLY NOW AVAILABLE." It says UnGodly, "its debut free-to-play dark-fantasy action RPG with character collection and team-building, is now available on the App Store and Google Play, and is coming to Galaxy Store and ONE Store in December."

The [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ungodly/id6740617332) (seller Azra Games Inc) also states, "UnGodly is live worldwide!" and, in version history, "The Divine War begins. UnGodly 1.0 is our biggest update ever, now live worldwide." The game is listed as free with in-app purchases.

A footnote on the official release says **the playable hero Conan the Barbarian will not be part of the November 11 release, but will be released in December 2026.**

## What the studio claims

Marketing language such as "console-grade" and Founder and CEO Mark Otero's statement that "You get the same depth, fidelity, and experience you'd expect on PC or console" are the studio's own claims. Otero also says the fight fills the whole screen on iPhone Duo, and that the studio "elevate[s] the experience across other platforms, using precision controls on Mac and Google Play Games for PC, or playing in a full spatial experience on Apple Vision Pro." Those platform descriptions are Azra's.

The release describes four-hero mid-fight swapping tied to ancient elements, open-world PvE campaigns, a branching story, "progression you earn, not buy," and Leaderboards and Challenges live from day one. Those are developer descriptions.

The official site is [ungodlygame.com](https://ungodlygame.com).

*Opinion:* the verifiable news is the store launch; the console-grade marketing is the studio's pitch and should be read as such.
