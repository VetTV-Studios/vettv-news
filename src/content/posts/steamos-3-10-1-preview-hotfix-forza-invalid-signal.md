Valve [posted SteamOS 3.10.1 Preview](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1675200/view/1849458702691760) on November 16 at 6:18 p.m. CT. The notes say the update "is for the Steam Deck Preview channel, and includes new features that are still being tested." Players can opt in under Settings > System > System Update Channel.

"This is an early Preview build of SteamOS 3.10, and may contain unexpected bugs or stability issues," Valve writes. If problems show up, it asks people to file a bug on the [SteamOS issue tracker](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/SteamOS/issues). The notes "are an overview, and may not include all changes since the previous SteamOS 3.9 Stable Release."

## What is new in 3.10.1

Under the heading "New in SteamOS 3.10.1," the General list has two items:

- "Fixed a 3.10 regression causing launch failures with titles such as the Forza Horizon series"
- "Fixed a 3.10 regression causing certain displays to show an 'invalid signal' error"

The notes do not name every affected game or display, and they do not describe a broader 3.10.1 feature set beyond those two regression fixes.

The same post also restates the SteamOS 3.10.0 Preview notes from November 13, including the Plasma 6.8.2 desktop update and the Linux 7.3 kernel rebase. Those items were already in the 3.10.0 Preview post; this article is about the 3.10.1 hotfix.
