## How to play

According to [Blizzard's starter guide](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315280/new-players-starter-guide-welcome-to-world-of-warcraft-forever), *Forever* runs on **Windows and Mac**. You need:

- **A Battle.net account.**
- **An active World of Warcraft subscription or Game Time.** *Forever* is included with either one, and there's no separate box to buy.
- **The Battle.net desktop app.** Install World of Warcraft, then choose *World of Warcraft: Forever* from the game version list.

The game is built for keyboard and mouse. Controller support is available in beta.

## What's in it

*Forever* is set after *Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom* and before Molten Core. The journey runs across the original continents to level 60. Blizzard says there are more than 1,000 new quests, plus new zones: Mount Hyjal, Shen'dralas, the Riverglades and Zephras Isle. The Riverglades alone adds more than 150 quests for the mid-30s to mid-40s.

The new **Skyborne** race starts on Zephras Isle, a levels 1–12 starting zone. You choose a faction when you create one. Horde-aligned Windshapers can be Shaman, and Alliance-aligned High-Order Skyborne can be Mages. New race and class combos include Orc Mages, Troll Warlocks, Undead Paladins, Human Hunters, Gnome Priests and Dwarf Shamans.

## Choices on your first login

- **Experience preset.** *Classic* keeps simpler visuals and fewer helpers turned on. *Enhanced* turns on HD models, quest markers, one-bag and transmog. Blizzard suggests Enhanced if you aren't sure, and you can change any of it later in Options.
- **Ruleset instead of realm.** You pick Normal, PvP, Roleplay or Roleplay PvP. Each one is a large shared player pool, not a single realm. A Hardcore ruleset comes sometime after launch.
- **Two-part names.** Characters get two names that together must be unique in your region.

Blizzard also lists a Camping system for shared campfire buffs, an account-wide **Legacy System** for horizontal progression, and refined gamepad support.

If you play on an NVIDIA GPU, [Game Ready Driver 621.14](/posts/nvidia-game-ready-driver-621-14-wow-forever-dlss-5/) is tuned for the launch.
