Dustin Rhodes said Wednesday that his grandson Rowan has died, and asked fans to keep the family in their prayers.

"Guys my grandson Rowan has passed. Please keep us all in your prayers during this time. I am here with @DakotaRunnels22 now," Rhodes [wrote on X](https://x.com/dustinrhodes/status/2125694628624290069) at 2:54 p.m. CT. About 13 minutes earlier, he had [asked for prayers](https://x.com/dustinrhodes/status/2125691462939815984) for his daughter Dakota Runnels and her husband Bleau.

Terri Runnels, Dakota's mother and Dustin's former wife, also confirmed the death in a Facebook post, according to [Cageside Seats](https://www.cagesideseats.com/aew/488557/dustin-rhodes-reveals-grandson-rowan-dead-three-years-old-terri-runnels-dakota-family-tragedy), [F4W/Wrestling Observer](https://www.f4wonline.com/news/aew/dustin-rhodes-announces-passing-of-his-grandson-rowan/), and [TMZ](https://www.tmz.com/2026/11/25/terri-runnels-dustin-rhodes-grandson-dead/). Speaking on behalf of Dakota, Bleau, and their families, she named the child Rowan Dane Oney and asked the public to respect their privacy, with no calls or visits. She said the family would share more details later and asked people to pray for them.

Rowan was 3 years old and was born in 2023. He was the older son of Dakota Runnels and her husband Bleau, [F4W reported](https://www.f4wonline.com/news/aew/dustin-rhodes-announces-passing-of-his-grandson-rowan/).

The family has not publicly described the circumstances of Rowan's death. We are not reporting speculation about a cause.

Rhodes, long known to wrestling fans as Goldust, wrestles for All Elite Wrestling.
