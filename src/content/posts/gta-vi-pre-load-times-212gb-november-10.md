Rockstar has posted pre-load details for *Grand Theft Auto VI*, which launches **November 19** on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Those are the only platforms Rockstar names.

## Pre-load times

- **Xbox Series X|S:** starts globally at 12:00 a.m. GMT on Tuesday, November 10. In Central Time that's **6:00 p.m. CT on Monday, November 9**.
- **PlayStation 5:** starts at midnight local time on Tuesday, November 10, wherever you are.
- **Code-in-box** copies for both consoles should reach retailers starting November 12.

## Make room

Rockstar says the pre-load needs **up to 212GB**, depending on your console. Clear space ahead of time. Rockstar's support site has step-by-step pre-load instructions for each console.

## Launch-day extras

On November 19, Rockstar will post a free printable *GTA VI* cover art poster and a Leonida map on the game's official site. A double-sided physical poster comes to the Rockstar Store shortly after launch. It costs $0.99 alone or comes free with any Rockstar Store purchase.
