Assassin's Creed Valhalla's free "The Most Requested Update" went live on November 5. Xbox Wire, in a post by Ubisoft, confirms it on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One. Secondary outlets publishing Ubisoft's patch notes call it title update 1.8.0 and say it's on all platforms. PlayStation.Blog confirms it on PS5 and PS5 Pro.

## What's in it (per Xbox Wire)

- **New Game+:** unlocks after the Holy Day quest. Each new run scales up in difficulty.
- **Mithril:** a new resource for upgrading divine gear. In New Game+ you get it when you receive gear or interact with a book of knowledge you already own. It's also sold in the Niflheim, Mastery and River Raid shops.
- **Gear upgrades:** the maximum level for each piece of gear goes from 13 to 27.
- **Platinum:** in New Game+, looting Carbon, Nickel or Tungsten from a Wealth Chest gives Platinum instead.
- **Extended Progression Mode:** when it's on, gear upgrades, runes and masteries count toward your overall power level.
- **Stash:** a new feature in Ravensthorpe for storing any gear and runes from your inventory.
- **Rune Forge:** runes can be upgraded for silver from the menu, up to a new Superior tier, and over 300 engravable runes are added.

Lead Game Designer David Larouche told Xbox Wire that the extended progression mode, the higher gear caps, the new rune tier and the new rune perks are "available without New Game+ as well."

## From Ubisoft's patch notes (secondary copies)

We couldn't find Ubisoft's own patch notes page. The details below come from Ubisoft's notes as published by MP1st and TheGamer:

- **Holy Day quest:** it's the last quest of the Hamtunscire region, which comes before the true ending.
- **Superior runes:** the step from greater to superior costs Mithril, not silver.
- **Engraving:** a perk can be engraved into a rune for 450 silver once you own the weapon or the full armor set.
- **New achievements:** Ivaldi's Heir (fully upgrade a Divine Gear) and Circle of Renewal (complete a New Game+ run).
- **Download sizes:**
  - Steam: 7.8 GB
  - Ubisoft Connect: 12.27 GB
  - Xbox Series X|S: 15.02 GB
  - Xbox One: 13.8 GB
  - PS5: 5.26 GB
  - PS4: 13.42 GB

Ubisoft's notes also mention PSSR 2 support on PS5 Pro, which PlayStation.Blog confirms.
