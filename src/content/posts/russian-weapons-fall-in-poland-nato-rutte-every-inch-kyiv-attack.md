Russian weapons came down at three locations in southeastern Poland early Friday, Nov. 20, while Russia carried out one of its largest air attacks on Ukraine since the full-scale invasion began, Polish officials said. Nobody in Poland was hurt, and officials reported no damage to critical infrastructure. Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk said the incident appears to be an "intentional provocation," and NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte traveled to Warsaw on Saturday, where he said the alliance would defend "every inch" of Polish territory, according to [Poland's PAP news agency](https://www.polskieradio.pl/395/7784/artykul/3755517,nato-vows-to-defend-every-inch-of-polish-territory-after-airspace-violation).

## What fell, and where

At a Friday news conference, Deputy Prime Minister and Defense Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz said objects had been secured at three places: Nowy Lipowiec in Biłgoraj County (Lublin province), Rudnik nad Sanem in Nisko County and Mołodycz in Jarosław County (both in Podkarpackie province), according to a [Ministry of National Defense release](https://www.gov.pl/web/obrona-narodowa/wicepremier-w-kosiniak-kamysz-o-naruszeniu-polskiej-przestrzeni-powietrznej). He said the object at Nowy Lipowiec was a Russian Kh-101 cruise missile, that a second missile likely fell near Rudnik nad Sanem and that the third was probably a jet-powered drone.

**That count has since been revised.** Podkarpackie Governor Teresa Kubas-Hul later said the debris near Rudnik nad Sanem came from a jet-powered drone, not a missile, meaning one missile and two jet drones, [UA.News reported](https://ua.news/en/world/do-polshchi-pid-chas-rosiiskoyi-ataki-na-ukrayinu-zaletila-odna-raketa-zamist-dvokh), citing Polish broadcaster RMF24. She said the exact drone types would only be known after a detailed investigation. On Saturday, [Lublin regional prosecutors said](https://www.gov.pl/web/po-lublin/aktualny-stan-czynnosci-procesowych-w-sprawie-ujawnienia-obiektow-latajacych-na-terenie-wojewodztwa-lubelskiego-i-podkarpackiego) military prosecutors and bomb-disposal teams were still working at all three sites, and that the precise nature, construction and origin of the objects can only be established after laboratory testing.

Some reports still describe two missiles and a drone, including [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/ukraine-war-russia-intentional-provocation-poland-missiles-nato/), [AP](https://apnews.com/article/ukraine-russia-war-kyiv-attacks-putin-crimea-433e18f00cbd37a2a9da94e60fce0e07) and [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/21/russian-missiles-hit-poland-what-happened-and-could-nato-respond). Distances also vary in early reporting: CBS, citing Polish defense officials, put the objects 30 to 60 miles inside Poland, while Al Jazeera put the Kh-101 about 31 miles (50 km) from the Ukrainian border and the drone about 20 miles (30 km) in.

Kosiniak-Kamysz said the first alerts went out at 1:40 a.m. local time, which is 6:40 p.m. CT Thursday, [Rzeczpospolita reported](https://www.rp.pl/konflikty-zbrojne/art45519451-kosiniak-kamysz-na-terytorium-polski-spadly-dwie-rakiety-i-dron). Residents near Nowy Lipowiec heard a loud blast just after 5:30 a.m. local time (about 10:30 p.m. CT Thursday), [BBC News Polska reported](https://www.bbc.com/polska/articles/c9gklm84kzemo). Polish F-16s and Swedish Gripen jets flew overnight, supported by a Dutch refueling tanker and a Polish airborne early-warning aircraft, the defense ministry said.

## Who called it a provocation

"We know for sure that what fell on Polish soil came from Russia," Tusk said, according to the [prime minister's office](https://www.gov.pl/web/premier/rosyjskie-obiekty-naruszyly-polska-przestrzen-powietrzna-kolejna-odslona-rosyjskiej-eskalacji), adding that, in his assessment and that of foreign partners, "it does not look like an accident." Tusk said "it seems we are dealing with an intentional provocation by Russia." One object fell not far from where troops from the Coalition of the Willing, a group of countries backing Ukraine, are holding exercises in Poland. The prime minister's office said a link between the incident and those drills "remains a hypothesis at this stage."

Kosiniak-Kamysz wrote that it is "hard to regard it as a coincidence" that the time and place matched the exercises, [CBS News reported](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/ukraine-war-russia-intentional-provocation-poland-missiles-nato/).

Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski struck a more cautious note on Saturday. "We will express our extreme dissatisfaction to the Russian side. We are awaiting confirmation of the indisputable facts," he said, according to [Ukrainian News](https://ukranews.com/en/news/1189110-poland-will-express-its-strongest-dissatisfaction-to-russia-over-the-airspace-violation-says-polish), citing TVN24. He said the incident would be discussed in Brussels at military and political levels.

## NATO's response, and Article 4

On Friday, Rutte posted on X that he had spoken with Tusk "about the grave violations of Polish airspace overnight," that NATO jets were scrambled, and that the incident was "clearly dangerous — a consequence of Russia's ongoing war against Ukraine," according to [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/ukraine-war-russia-intentional-provocation-poland-missiles-nato/) and [PAP](https://zachod.pl/1593738/szef-nato-po-naruszeniu-polskiej-przestrzeni-bedziemy-nadal-wzmacniac-zdolnosc-odstraszania-i-obrony/). In Warsaw on Saturday, Rutte said, "now we need to establish exactly what happened," and that NATO's top commander in Europe would look at possible vulnerabilities in the alliance's defenses, [PAP reported](https://www.polskieradio.pl/395/7784/artykul/3755517,nato-vows-to-defend-every-inch-of-polish-territory-after-airspace-violation). Asked whether it could happen again, he said he could not rule that out.

A spokesperson for that commander, U.S. Air Force Gen. Alexus Grynkewich, told CBS News he was in close contact with Poland's military chief and defense minister.

Kosiniak-Kamysz said Poland would ask NATO for more forces and equipment for Eastern Sentry, the alliance mission guarding its eastern flank, including early-warning aircraft and F-35 fighters, according to the [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/ukraine-war-latest-russia-strikes-3-kyiv-bridges-in-massive-aerial-assault-on-ukraine-at-least-9-dead/), citing Defence24. President Karol Nawrocki spoke with Rutte on Friday and remains in contact with him, the [president's chief of staff said](https://www.przyszlosc.pl/kancelaria/aktywnosc-ministrow/minister-szefernaker-rfm24,130471).

**Poland has not invoked Article 4**, the NATO clause that lets an ally request formal consultations, as of Saturday, according to [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/21/russian-missiles-hit-poland-what-happened-and-could-nato-respond). Germany's Bild reported Friday that Warsaw was considering it and that Berlin was advising against it, [Onet reported](https://wiadomosci.onet.pl/swiat/polska-skorzysta-z-artykulu-4-nato-nowe-doniesienia-po-upadku-rosyjskich-rakiet/jkpkjw0); VetTV News could not confirm that report. No Polish official has asked for Article 5, the alliance's mutual-defense clause. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/ukraine-russia-war-kyiv-attacks-putin-crimea-433e18f00cbd37a2a9da94e60fce0e07) notes Article 5 is not triggered automatically and requires a political decision by all members. Poland last used Article 4 in September 2025 after Russian drones entered its airspace.

## What Russia has said

There was no immediate comment from Moscow on the weapons that fell in Poland, according to [AP](https://apnews.com/article/ukraine-russia-war-kyiv-attacks-putin-crimea-433e18f00cbd37a2a9da94e60fce0e07) and [AFP](https://www.afp.com/en/warsaw-says-russian-missiles-hit-poland-denounces-intentional-provocation). Russian state news agency [RIA Novosti](https://ria.ru/20261121/polsha-2133130047.html) framed Sikorski's Saturday remarks as an admission that Poland has no proof the objects were Russian, and noted that Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov has previously called claims of Russian airspace violations "empty and groundless." Polish officials, including Tusk, say the objects came from Russia.

On Nov. 19, before the attack, President Donald Trump told Fox News that "Putin has been landing drones in Poland and a couple of little missiles, by mistake," the [Kyiv Independent reported](https://kyivindependent.com/ukraine-war-latest-russia-strikes-3-kyiv-bridges-in-massive-aerial-assault-on-ukraine-at-least-9-dead/). We did not find a new White House or State Department statement on Friday's incident as of Saturday morning.

## The attack on Ukraine

The overnight attack hit Kyiv and other regions, including far-western Lviv near the Polish border. Ukraine's Air Force said Russia launched 84 cruise missiles and 404 drones, and President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said 27 ballistic missiles were also fired, according to the [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/ukraine-war-latest-russia-strikes-3-kyiv-bridges-in-massive-aerial-assault-on-ukraine-at-least-9-dead/). The Air Force said 79 cruise missiles and 380 drones were shot down or suppressed.

Death tolls vary by time of reporting. Zelenskyy said at least eight people were killed, [CBS News reported](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/ukraine-war-russia-intentional-provocation-poland-missiles-nato/). The Kyiv Independent later put the toll at at least nine: seven in Kyiv, according to Mayor Vitali Klitschko, and two elsewhere, according to Prime Minister Serhii Koretskyi. Interior Minister Ivan Vyhivskyi said 30 people were wounded nationwide. Klitschko said three Dnipro River bridges in Kyiv were damaged, and officials reported damage to water, power and heating supplies.

Austria said its embassy in Kyiv was damaged by a projectile in this week's Russian strikes, with no staff hurt, and summoned Russia's ambassador, the [Kyiv Independent reported](https://kyivindependent.com/responsibility-lies-with-russian-aggressor-austria-pushes-back-against-moscows-claims-over-damage-to-embassy-in-kyiv/). Russia's embassy in Austria said Russia does not target diplomatic missions and blamed Vienna for any consequences; Austria rejected that. France called for a UN Security Council meeting, CBS News reported.

The attack came days after Ukraine damaged the Crimean Bridge, and Russian President Vladimir Putin promised retaliation, telling reporters on Thursday that Russia's response would be "far more painful," according to [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/ukraine-war-russia-intentional-provocation-poland-missiles-nato/).
