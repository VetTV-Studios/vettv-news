A postal employee has been charged after a shooting Thursday morning at the main Des Moines post office, Des Moines police said.

[Jennifer Christine Pledger](https://x.com/IowaPulse/status/2118460177133273108), 39, of West Des Moines, faces two counts of attempted murder, two counts of willful injury causing serious injury and one count of intimidation with a dangerous weapon, according to Des Moines police Sgt. Paul Parizek, as reported by Radio Iowa, We Are Iowa and the Associated Press. The [Polk County Jail](https://polkinmates.polkcountyiowa.gov/) inmate roster lists the same charges under booking number 260015181, with no bail.

## What police say happened

Witnesses told police Pledger went to the second floor of the post office at 1165 2nd Avenue around 9 a.m. and opened fire on fellow employees, We Are Iowa reported, citing Parizek. Investigators allege she shot one woman, causing her to fall, then stood over her and fired again, and also fired toward other employees as they fled, according to AP and Radio Iowa.

Two female employees, ages 45 and 53, were hit by gunfire. A 55-year-old male employee was injured in a fall while fleeing, Radio Iowa reported. Police have said the injuries are not believed to be life-threatening, and We Are Iowa reported all three were listed in stable condition. The second floor is not a publicly accessible part of the building, Parizek said.

## Arrest and investigation

911 calls reporting 10–12 gunshots began around 9:10 a.m., and officers arrived at 9:13 a.m., We Are Iowa reported. Pledger was taken into custody without incident east of the building at 9:21 a.m., near a bike trail under the University Avenue Des Moines River bridge. Police said a nine-millimeter handgun was recovered and that Pledger has not made any comments about a motive. The investigation remains ongoing.

*These are charges. Pledger has not been convicted of any crime.*
