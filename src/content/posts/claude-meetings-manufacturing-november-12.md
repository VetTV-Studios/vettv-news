Anthropic published two product updates on November 12: [Claude Meetings](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-meetings), now in beta, and [Claude for Manufacturing](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-for-manufacturing), a new plugin for plant teams.

## Claude Meetings

"Claude can now join your video calls as a visible participant, follow the conversation and start on work during the meeting," the Meetings article says. "Claude Meetings is now in beta." The post says a team can ask Claude questions "out loud or in chat," that "by default, Claude answers in writing," and that it can "work with you live to build docs, whiteboards, or other artifacts." It also "writes the notes and action items."

"Claude Meetings is starting to roll out in beta on Team and Enterprise plans," Anthropic says. "Support for other video conferencing platforms is coming soon." Under Getting started, it is more specific: "Support is currently available for Google Meet, with other platforms coming soon." On Team plans, "inviting Claude to your meetings is available by default, and an admin can turn it off." On Enterprise plans, "an admin turns it on for all or part of the organization." During the beta, "it isn't available to some organizations, including those with zero data retention (ZDR), HIPAA-ready configuration, or customer-managed encryption keys (CMEK)."

The company says notes and transcripts are free on Team and Enterprise plans during the beta, as are in-call answers unless they use a connected tool, which "counts toward your plan's usage." "The docs, whiteboards, and other artifacts Claude makes in a call are free through February 10, 2027."

On privacy, the article says Claude "saves a transcript, summary, and screen descriptions, not audio," appears as a participant, and posts a notice in the call's chat. "By default, Claude is set to stay out of calls when someone from outside your organization is invited, and to leave if someone from outside joins partway through," unless an admin allows guests.

## Claude for Manufacturing

The manufacturing article describes "a plugin with 17 skills and four connectors" that "helps manufacturers draft quotes, quality reports, and supplier requests from the systems they already run." The connectors listed are Autodesk Fusion, Infor, Microsoft 365, and Oracle NetSuite.

"The plugin's 17 skills cover sales, quality, operations, supply chain, design, and setup," Anthropic says. The post does not name all 17 skills. It does give example processes: quoting a new job, investigating a failed inspection, diagnosing a breakdown, sourcing from another supplier, comparing designs with Autodesk Fusion, and reporting on production.

"On Team or Enterprise plans, install the plugin and tell Claude, 'Set us up,'" the article says. "Claude for Manufacturing doesn't operate equipment." Anthropic says it is developing a separate Model Hardware Standard, described as a research preview, for programmable equipment.

A technical webinar is listed for November 17 at 11:00 a.m. ET (10:00 a.m. CT).
