*Updated Nov. 25, 2026, 6 p.m. CT: Added what Mayor Zohran Mamdani, NYPD Commissioner Jessica Tisch and Macy's parade executive producer Will Coss said at Wednesday evening's news conference, NBC's new broadcast plans for Thursday and Saturday, and the balloon schedule. The "What's still unclear" section has been rewritten.*

The Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade will not march on Thanksgiving this year. Macy's, New York City officials and the NYPD have decided to move the 100th parade to Saturday, November 28, because of a strong storm expected to hit the city on Thursday, [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/macys-thanksgiving-day-parade-postponed-sources/story?id=138452401) and [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/macys-thanksgiving-day-parade-weather-delay-rcna611355) reported Wednesday. The parade will now start at 8:30 a.m. ET Saturday, according to Macy's.

ABC News broke the story [on X](https://x.com/ABC/status/2125696242202099872) at about 3 p.m. CT Wednesday, citing "sources familiar with the decision." Macy's then confirmed the move on the record. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/macys-thanksgiving-parade-postponed-weather-cdc870899c8dc2d32a0ac3d86119b3ba) reported that Macy's announced the postponement because powerful winds are expected in New York.

## What Macy's said

"The safety of our participants and spectators is our top priority, and after close consultation with our City and agency partners, we have made the decision to move this year's celebration," Macy's said in a statement quoted by [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/macys-thanksgiving-day-parade-postponed-sources/story?id=138452401) and [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/macys-thanksgiving-day-parade-weather-delay-rcna611355).

The company also thanked "the thousands of volunteers, performers and partners who create the magic millions enjoy each year," adding, "We look forward to celebrating together on Saturday," [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/macys-thanksgiving-day-parade-weather-delay-rcna611355) reported.

At the time of writing, Macy's own [X account](https://x.com/Macys) had not posted about the new date.

## The storm behind the decision

On Wednesday, the National Weather Service upgraded its Thanksgiving forecast for the city to a high wind warning, [Gothamist reported](https://gothamist.com/news/thanksgiving-day-forecast-upgraded-to-a-high-wind-warning-ahead-of-parade). The warning takes effect at 10 a.m. Thursday, while the parade would normally still be on the route. A day earlier, [New York City Emergency Management](https://www.nyc.gov/site/em/about/press-releases/pr_20261124-NYCEM-Issues-Weather-Alert-For-Gusty-Winds-On-Thanksgiving.page) had told residents to plan for:

- **Wind:** gusts of 45 to 55 mph, strongest Thursday afternoon and evening, with gusts possibly topping 30 mph by mid-morning.
- **Rain:** 1 to 2 inches, with a few spots getting up to 3 inches.
- **Coastal flooding:** likely around high tides on Thursday morning and evening, and possibly Friday morning.

The giant balloons are the main concern. Under city rules adopted in 1998, the balloons cannot fly when sustained winds top 23 mph or gusts top 34 mph, and the NYPD has the final say on whether they go up, [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/25/macys-thanksgiving-parade-2026-balloons-weather) reported. Those rules came after strong winds pushed a Cat in the Hat balloon into a lamppost in 1997 and critically injured a spectator, according to [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/25/macys-thanksgiving-parade-2026-balloons-weather).

## A rare change for a 100-year tradition

This was supposed to be a milestone year. Macy's planned a record 100 balloons and inflatables for the 100th parade, according to [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/25/macys-thanksgiving-parade-2026-balloons-weather), along with performers from nine Broadway shows, [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/macys-thanksgiving-day-parade-weather-delay-rcna611355) reported.

- **Cancellations:** The parade has been called off only three times since it began in 1924, in 1942, 1943 and 1944, when the materials for the balloons were needed for World War II, according to [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/macys-thanksgiving-day-parade-weather-delay-rcna611355).
- **Grounded balloons:** All of the giant balloons were kept down only once before, in 1971, because of wind and rain, [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/25/macys-thanksgiving-parade-2026-balloons-weather) and [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/macys-thanksgiving-day-parade-weather-delay-rcna611355) reported.
- **First postponement:** [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/25/macys-thanksgiving-parade-2026-balloons-weather) called this the first time the parade has been postponed. We haven't independently verified that.

The route is unchanged. The parade runs from the Upper West Side to Macy's Herald Square flagship store and usually wraps up by noon, [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/macys-parade-2026-where-watch-time-thanksgiving-day-streaming-channel-rcna609932) reported.

## What officials said Wednesday night

Mamdani, Tisch and Macy's held a news conference Wednesday evening, [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/macys-thanksgiving-day-parade-weather-delay-rcna611355) reported.

- **The storm:** Mamdani said the city should expect sustained winds of 30 to 40 mph on Thursday, with gusts possibly reaching 65 mph, strong enough to bring down tree limbs and power lines, [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/macys-thanksgiving-day-parade-weather-delay-rcna611355) reported. "There is no destination worth risking your safety," he said, urging travelers to leave early and stay indoors if conditions turn severe.
- **The balloons:** Will Coss, the parade's executive producer, said the team was "heartbroken" but that safety "is, and always will be, our top priority," according to [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/macys-thanksgiving-day-parade-weather-delay-rcna611355). He said the balloons in New York will be taken down and trucked to a facility in New Jersey to be readied again, "a weeks-long process that's going to get condensed to about 24 hours."
- **Inflation:** Deflation was set to start at 7 p.m. ET Wednesday, and the balloons will be reinflated Friday afternoon, a city official told [ABC7 New York](https://abc7ny.com/story/will-macys-parade-balloons-fly-storm-wind-rain-puts-doubt/20078423/). The Friday reinflation will not be open to the public, [ABC7 New York reported](https://abc7ny.com/post/macys-parade-2026-balloon-inflation-how-attend-what-stormy-weather-could-mean/20074929/).
- **Security:** Tisch said the NYPD is working with Macy's and other officials on a full security plan, and that there are "no specific, credible threats to the parade at this time," [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/macys-thanksgiving-day-parade-weather-delay-rcna611355) reported.

## How to watch now

- **Thursday:** NBC will air the 2025 parade at 8:30 a.m. in all time zones instead of a live show, [NBCUniversal said](https://www.nbcuniversal.com/article/100th-macys-thanksgiving-day-parade-returns-live-nbc-and-peacock-nov-26-bringing-holiday-magic-life).
- **Saturday:** The 100th parade airs live on NBC and Peacock, and on Telemundo, [according to NBC](https://www.nbc.com/nbc-insider/2026-macys-thanksgiving-day-parade-everything-to-know). [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/entertainment/tv-streaming/2026/11/25/macys-thanksgiving-day-parade-2026-how-to-watch/92940287007/) reported that it starts at 8:30 a.m. ET, runs about 3.5 hours, airs live in all time zones, and gets an encore on NBC at 2 p.m. ET/PT.
- **Hosts:** Savannah Guthrie, Hoda Kotb and Al Roker are still set to host, [according to NBC](https://www.nbc.com/nbc-insider/2026-macys-thanksgiving-day-parade-everything-to-know).

## What's still unclear

Macy's said it is "working through the complex operational logistics" for Saturday and will share more details as they are finalized, [ABC7 New York](https://abc7ny.com/story/will-macys-parade-balloons-fly-storm-wind-rain-puts-doubt/20078423/) reported. As of Wednesday night, the reports we reviewed did not say whether every balloon, float and performer will make the Saturday date. This is a developing story, and we'll update it as Macy's shares more.
