Anthropic announced **Claude Fable 5.2** and **Claude Mythos 5.2** on **November 9, 2026**. The company says the two are the **same underlying model**. Fable includes cybersecurity and biology safeguards and is available to all users. Organizations pursuing approved work in cybersecurity or biomedical research can apply for **Mythos** through Anthropic's Life Sciences and Cyber Verification Programs.

## Pricing, speed, and context

The Claude Platform release notes list both models at **$8 per million input tokens** and **$40 per million output tokens**, down from **$10 / $50** on Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1. Both have a **1 million token** context window, **128,000** max output tokens, and always-on adaptive thinking. API IDs are `claude-fable-5-2` and `claude-mythos-5-2`.

Anthropic says the new models generate outputs **up to 30% faster** than the 5.1 versions. An optional **fast mode** is priced at **$16 / $80** per million input/output tokens and, the company says, speeds token generation by **up to 2.5 times**. Fast mode for Fable 5.2 is on the Messages API (`speed: "fast"`). Access to Mythos fast mode is limited to organizations in the verification programs.

Those speed figures, along with Anthropic's benchmark tables and research anecdotes, are **the company's own claims**. They are not independently verified here.

## Availability and limits

Anthropic says Fable 5.2 is available today on its platforms, including Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure. The release notes specify the Claude API, Claude Platform on AWS, Claude Models on Google Cloud (Anthropic-managed and Google-managed), Amazon Bedrock, and Microsoft Foundry.

Mythos 5.2 remains limited to verified organizations. Claude Enterprise customers can also use **Claude Security**, which Anthropic says is now powered in part by Mythos 5.2. Both models require **30-day data retention** and are not available under zero data retention unless Anthropic expressly authorizes it.

Anthropic is also running a launch promotion this week: eligible paid plans and seats that include Fable models get extra Fable usage, described as one to three Fable resets based on subscription length, with Fable included for up to **50%** of weekly usage limits for Pro plans and standard Team seats.
