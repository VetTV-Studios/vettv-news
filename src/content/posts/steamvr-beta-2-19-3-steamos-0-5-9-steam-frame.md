Valve [posted SteamVR Beta 2.19.3](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/250820/view/649241729050870282) on November 13 at 7:33 p.m. CT, and [SteamOS 0.5.9 Beta for Steam Frame](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4165890/view/687522235410285323) three minutes later, at 7:36 p.m. CT. The Frame notes say "SteamVR has been updated to version 2.19.3." Both updates are still in beta.

Anyone can opt into the SteamVR beta; Valve points to its [SteamVR beta instructions](https://help.steampowered.com/en/faqs/view/4F5E-AD22-7402-2EAD). On Steam Frame, 0.5.9 "is for the Steam Frame Beta channel," and players can opt in under Settings > System > System Update Channel.

## SteamVR 2.19.3 on Linux and Steam Link

Under Steam Link, Valve "Fixed low resolution in the mirror window and 3rd party mirror/recording apps. Now uses full resolution," and "Fixed an issue where manually setting the stream video size became the default on the next launch."

The Linux list is longer. Valve "Fixed Linux rendering issues with the improved streaming support introduced in version 2.19.1," "Fixed an issue causing the right eye to flicker," "Fixed the scene being distorted on HTC Vive and Valve Index when the SteamVR dashboard is up," "Improved streaming reliability," "Fixed scene brightness and visual corruption when running at high resolution," "Improved performance on Nvidia with wired headsets," and "Fixed a possible lockup at startup."

Elsewhere, the notes "Fixed Gyro to Joystick Camera in World and Player mode with Steam Input," and "Fixed an issue causing the VR View to not remember which eye was chosen or starting with the incorrect aspect ratio." For Valve Index, Valve "Added accessibility settings for prism correction (diplopia)," under Advanced Settings > General > Accessibility. That setting "needs to restart SteamVR to take effect."

## SteamOS 0.5.9 on Steam Frame

The Frame notes lead with Camera Pass Through / Room View: "PC Streaming now supports chroma key to allow Room View to be shown on VR content." Settings are on the PC, in the Camera section, "including a mode switch between Protect Content and Key Content." "Key Content will allow any game to simply draw the key color to control Room View transparency."

Valve adds, in bold, that "this feature requires also opting into the beta branch of SteamVR on PC."

The General list says "Foveated sharpening is now applied when streaming VR through Steam Link," and that prism-correction accessibility settings were added on the headset too, needing a headset restart. Fixes include "a visual glitch when opening/closing the dashboard in some games," "WebXR support in Chrome/Chromium when installed as user (instead of system-wide) from Discover," and "a system lock-up caused by certain apps installed from Discover (eg: VLC)."

Valve also "Linked the Show Perf Overlay in VR and Draw Performance Assessment toggles together. Turning off Show Perf Overlay in VR also disables the developer overlay."
