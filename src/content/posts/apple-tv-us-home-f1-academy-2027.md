Apple [announced](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-tv-becomes-the-new-us-home-of-f1-academy-the-all-female-racing-series/) on November 19, 2026, that F1 ACADEMY, the all-female racing series, will stream on Apple TV in the United States beginning in 2027. "Fans will be able to watch every qualifying session and race throughout the season," Apple's newsroom says. A footnote on that post adds, "Requires a U.S. Apple TV subscription."

Apple says the series is "entering its fifth season in 2027." The newsroom describes F1 ACADEMY as "the all-female racing series dedicated to developing the next generation of talent."

## How Apple describes its existing Formula 1 relationship

We are mentioning Apple's current U.S. Formula 1 arrangement only as the newsroom states it. Apple says the F1 ACADEMY announcement "builds on Apple's growing relationship with Formula 1, following the global success of Apple Original Films' blockbuster F1 and the launch of a five-year broadcast partnership that made Apple TV the exclusive U.S. broadcast partner starting in 2026."

The company also says fans in the U.S. "can catch all the F1 ACADEMY action exclusively on Apple TV beginning in 2027."

## What the people quoted in Apple's announcement said

"It's been incredible to see the excitement around Formula 1 on Apple TV, and we're thrilled to welcome F1 ACADEMY in 2027," said Jim DeLorenzo, Apple's global head of Sports.

Susie Wolff, F1 ACADEMY's managing director, called it a "landmark partnership with Apple TV in the U.S." and said, "Visibility remains fundamental to our mission. When young girls can switch on their screens and see women racing, competing, and succeeding, it changes what they believe is possible."

Ian Holmes, Formula 1's chief media rights and broadcasting officer, said bringing the series to Apple TV in the United States "marks another exciting step in its growth."

Apple's newsroom also says that since launching in 2022 as a Formula 4-level development series, F1 ACADEMY has become "the second-most-followed series after Formula 1, according to the 2025 Global F1 Fan Survey." That ranking is Apple's, citing that survey; we have not independently reviewed the survey.
