Meta introduced **Muse Ember** on **November 9, 2026**, calling it the company's smallest and most efficient model for repeated agent work such as tool calls, coding subtasks, routing, extraction, and long-document parsing. It is available today on Muse Code and the Meta Model API as **`muse-ember-1.0`**, and Meta also lists Muse Ember, Muse Spark, and Muse Wonder on Google Cloud and Oracle Cloud.

## Pricing and limits (from Meta)

Standard list pricing, for prompts up to **200,000** tokens, is **$0.10 per million input tokens**, **$0.004 per million cached input tokens**, and **$0.50 per million output tokens**. For longer prompts, Meta lists **$0.30 / $0.012 / $1.50**. The model has a **1 million token** context window and up to **512,000** output tokens, and supports the Responses API.

## Benchmarks and cost-per-task claims

Meta says Ember "delivers more intelligence per dollar than Claude Haiku 5.5 or GPT-6 Luna at any reasoning setting," matches Haiku's best score at about a third of the cost, and sits on the efficiency frontier of the Artificial Analysis leaderboard. It also cites about **$0.14** per Artificial Analysis task, versus **$2.39** for Muse Spark 1.3 at max effort, and a table of evaluation scores including AutomationBench-AA, Agents' Last Exam, and Terminal-Bench 4.0.

Those comparisons, dollar-per-task figures, and evaluation scores are **Meta's reported results** (some labeled as Artificial Analysis data as of November 9, others as Meta internal evaluations). They are not independently verified here. Meta's own table also shows Haiku 5.5 ahead of Ember on FrontierCode 2.0-AA at max effort (**34.9%** vs. **27.2%**).

Partner comments from Box, Cline, Oracle, Cloudflare, and You.com in the same post are those companies' testimonials, not independent audits.

## Muse on Apple Watch

Separately, the Apple App Store listing for **Muse from Meta**, published by Meta Platforms, Inc., now includes **Apple Watch** among supported devices and requires **watchOS 9.0 or later**. Version **15.0** release notes say: "Add your Muse to your wrist with the new Apple Watch Companion App!" plus bug fixes and improvements.

**MacRumors** reported on November 9 that the watch app lets users text, dictate to, or call Muse, and adds a complication for quick access. Those interface details come from MacRumors, not from the brief App Store notes.
