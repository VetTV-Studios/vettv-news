NVIDIA [reported](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-third-quarter-fiscal-2027) revenue of $115.8 billion for the third quarter of fiscal 2027, which ended October 25, 2026, up 20% from the previous quarter and up 103% from a year ago. The [November 17 results news release](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-third-quarter-fiscal-2027) lists GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins of 74.8% and 74.9%, respectively, and GAAP and non-GAAP earnings per diluted share of $2.70 and $2.68, respectively.

"Our momentum is real. NVIDIA is an exceptional technology leader, powered by the relentless innovation of our people," said Colette Kress, interim CEO and executive vice president and chief financial officer of NVIDIA. "Vera Rubin is off to an explosive start, our roadmap is on track and our ambition has never been greater."

## The quarter's results

From the release's GAAP summary table, with dollar figures in billions except earnings per share:

- Revenue: $115.8, compared with $96.2 in the second quarter and $57.0 a year earlier, up 20% quarter over quarter and up 103% year over year.
- Gross margin: 74.8%, down 0.2 points from 75.0% in the second quarter and up 1.4 points from 73.4% a year earlier.
- Operating expenses: $9.2, up 9% from $8.4 and up 57% from $5.8.
- Operating income: $77.5, up 22% from $63.7 and up 115% from $36.0.
- Net income: $65.3, up 9% from $59.7 and up 105% from $31.9.
- Diluted earnings per share: $2.70, up 10% from $2.46 and up 108% from $1.30.

The release notes that the quarter-over-quarter and year-over-year percentages were "calculated based on $ in millions."

On a non-GAAP basis, gross margin was 74.9%, compared with 75.0% in the second quarter and 73.5% a year earlier, down 0.1 points sequentially and up 1.4 points year over year. Non-GAAP operating expenses were $8.8 billion, non-GAAP operating income was $77.9 billion, and non-GAAP net income was $64.9 billion. Non-GAAP diluted earnings per share were $2.68, up 21% from $2.22 in the second quarter and up 111% from $1.27 a year earlier.

Kress's [CFO commentary](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000088/q3fy27cfocommentary.htm), posted with the results, says GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins "increased from a year ago on improved mix from Blackwell Ultra" and "decreased slightly sequentially, reflecting the initial Rubin ramp." It attributes the operating-expense increases to "higher compute infrastructure and employee compensation and benefits costs."

## Data Center and Edge Computing

The results news release does not break out Gaming, Professional Visualization, or Automotive as separate revenue figures. It reports two market platforms:

- Data Center revenue was $107.6 billion, up 21% from the previous quarter and up 110% from a year ago.
- Edge Computing revenue was $8.2 billion, up 14% from the previous quarter and up 42% from a year ago.

Those two figures add to the $115.8 billion total.

Kress's commentary provides a further split of Data Center revenue. Hyperscale was $60.1 billion, up 23% from $48.7 billion in the second quarter and up 71% from $35.1 billion a year earlier. AI Clouds, Industrial, & Enterprise was $47.5 billion, up 18% from $40.3 billion and up 195% from $16.1 billion. On NVIDIA's reportable segments, the commentary lists Compute & Networking revenue of $106.0 billion, up 20% from $88.3 billion and up 108% from $50.9 billion, and Graphics revenue of $9.8 billion, up 25% from $7.9 billion and up 62% from $6.1 billion.

The commentary calls third-quarter revenue and Data Center revenue records. It says the Hyperscale and AI Clouds, Industrial, & Enterprise platforms "both grew substantially, driven by the introduction of our Rubin infrastructure, our fastest architecture ramp ever, standalone Vera CPUs and the continued growth of Blackwell Ultra." It also states: "Shipments of Data Center Hopper products to China during the quarter were $2.4 billion."

On Edge Computing, the commentary says, "Strong Blackwell workstation sales and edge compute for Physical AI applications drove the increases, while higher memory and system prices continue to affect consumer PC demand."

## Vera Rubin and other items in the release

The release says NVIDIA "ramped production and began shipping NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 — the fastest product ramp in the company's history — with racks running at partners including CoreWeave, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure and Nebius, and coming online soon at SpaceXSI." A highlight bullet describes Vera Rubin NVL72 as "the most versatile and advanced computing platform ever built." Those characterizations are the company's.

The Data Center section also lists an "agreement to acquire Hugging Face." The release does not state a purchase price or a closing date.

Under Edge Computing, the release says NVIDIA "reinvented Windows PCs with NVIDIA RTX Spark laptops, now shipping from ASUS, Dell, HP, Lenovo, Microsoft Surface and MSI," and that it "launched NVIDIA DLSS 5 in NBA 2K27 and Hogwarts Legacy." It also says the company "expanded partnerships with Mercedes-Benz and Volvo Cars to advance safe automated driving."

## Capital returns and the balance sheet

In September 2026, NVIDIA's Board of Directors approved an increase of $150 billion to the company's share repurchase authorization, without expiration. During the third quarter, NVIDIA returned $28.1 billion to shareholders in the form of shares repurchased and cash dividends. As of the end of the quarter, the company had approximately $227.3 billion remaining under its share repurchase authorization. The CFO commentary says NVIDIA plans to use the remaining authorization "through fiscal 2028."

NVIDIA will pay its next quarterly cash dividend of $0.25 per share on December 23, 2026, to all shareholders of record on December 2, 2026.

The commentary lists cash, cash equivalents, and marketable debt securities of $53.5 billion at quarter-end, inventory of $39.7 billion, up from $31.6 billion in the second quarter "to support our Rubin product ramp," and cash flow from operating activities of $48.3 billion. It says supply commitments increased from $279 billion last quarter to $390 billion, "primarily related to the multi-year procurement of memory supply."

## Fourth-quarter outlook

NVIDIA's outlook for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2027, which includes an additional week, is as follows, according to the release:

- Revenue is expected to be $130.0 billion, plus or minus 3%. "NVIDIA is not assuming any Data Center compute revenue from China in its outlook."
- GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins are expected to be between 71.0% and 72.0%.
- GAAP and non-GAAP operating expenses are expected to be approximately $11.0 billion and $10.7 billion, respectively.

For the full year fiscal 2027, NVIDIA expects GAAP and non-GAAP tax rates to be between 16.0% and 18.0%, "excluding any discrete items and material changes to NVIDIA's tax environment."

The company said it would hold a conference call with analysts and investors at 2 p.m. Pacific time (4 p.m. CT) on November 17, with a webcast at [NVIDIA's investor relations website](https://investor.nvidia.com).
