The official Steam news post [SCUM - November Update | 1.3.6.0.177121](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/513710/view/694277269707817995) went up at **10:02 a.m. CT on November 11**. The same post includes a delay notice that the update, originally scheduled for November 9, was rescheduled to **November 11, 2026**.

"The November Update is live!" the developers write. "There's good news for our Linux, Steam Deck and Steam Machine players too, with support for joining multiplayer servers through Proton."

In the Linux section they add: "Some of you have already found ways to get SCUM multiplayer running through various workarounds. **Joining multiplayer servers through Proton is now officially supported on Linux devices, including Steam Deck and Steam Machine.**"

The post also says this first release of controller support "may still have some rough edges," and that combined with Proton multiplayer, controller support "gives our Steam Deck and Steam Machine prisoners more ways to play." The update does not include a wipe, and "no further official-server wipes are planned for 2026."

## Steam Deck store status

On the [SCUM Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/513710/SCUM/), Steam Deck compatibility is still listed as **Unsupported**. The page's compatibility data uses `resolved_category` 1 and the `SteamOSDoesNotSupport` token for both Steam Deck and SteamOS.

The official November Update post does not mention BattlEye or anti-cheat by name.

*Opinion:* official Proton multiplayer is the news that matters for Linux players; the store page still saying Unsupported is the caveat that should travel with the announcement.
