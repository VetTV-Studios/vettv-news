Capcom [announced on November 25](https://info.exoprimal.com/en/pages/important_261125_001/) that it will discontinue service for *Exoprimal* at **8:00 p.m. PDT on Wednesday, September 29, 2027** (10:00 p.m. CT). "Exoprimal will no longer be playable after the end of service," the company wrote. The same notice is on the [official Exoprimal site](https://www.exoprimal.com/en-us/).

The announcement is addressed to players on Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, Windows, PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, and Steam. Capcom said, "We cannot thank you enough for the love and support you have shown over the years, and we sincerely apologize for the gravity of this announcement."

## The schedule Capcom posted

Capcom listed three dates:

- **Survival Pass Premium Tiers** are no longer available on stores as of **5:00 p.m. PST on Tuesday, November 24, 2026** (7:00 p.m. CT). The notice names the Premium Tiers for Seasons 1 through 4.
- **The game, DLC, and all other products** will no longer be available on stores as of **6:00 p.m. PDT on Tuesday, March 30, 2027** (8:00 p.m. CT).
- **End of service** is **8:00 p.m. PDT on Wednesday, September 29, 2027** (10:00 p.m. CT).

Capcom also says it will still be possible to re-download the game after it leaves stores and before service ends. After the end of service, the game will not be playable. The company asked players to "continue to enjoy Exoprimal until service ends," and pointed questions to its [Exoprimal inquiry form](https://c.cid.capcom.com/inquiry/exoprimal.html).

*Exoprimal* is an online team-based shooter, so the end of service means the game as sold today will not keep working offline. Capcom did not announce an offline mode or a private-server option in this notice.
