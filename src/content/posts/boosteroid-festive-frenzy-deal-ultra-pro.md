Boosteroid is running a **Festive Frenzy Deal** from **November 12, 2026, through January 6, 2027**. On the company's [Festive Frenzy Deal page](https://boosteroid.com/festive-frenzy-deal/), it is offering **4 months of Ultra Pro** for **€44.89**, which is equivalent to **€11.22 a month**. That €11.22 figure is the 4-month bundle divided across four months, not a standalone monthly plan price.

The deal page lists a "Regular Ultra 4 Month price" of **€67.56** and a savings of **€22**. Boosteroid's promotional art for the campaign, including the Open Graph image on that page, shows **€16.89** struck through and presents the bundle as "from €11.22 per month." €16.89 is the monthly equivalent of that €67.56 regular 4-month price (€67.56 ÷ 4 = €16.89). A public Boosteroid pricing page was not available to confirm a separate month-to-month Ultra Pro rate, and the deal page does not publish a price in U.S. dollars.

A footnote on the deal page says: "Keep the price as long as you stay subscribed and the payment method is valid. Promotion valid through January 6, 2027."

## Subscribe and a chance to win a game

The same page ties a giveaway to the promotion. "All promotion participants automatically participate in our special Festive Frenzy Deal," it says. "**10 winners** will be able to choose one of the following titles." A second footnote adds: "Subscribe between November 12, 2026 and January 6, 2027 to participate. Our community managers will contact the winners and let them choose which game they want." The page also says winners "will be notified directly and may select their preferred prize from this list."

The prize list on the deal page has **11 titles**:

- Forza Horizon 6
- ARC Raiders
- Cyberpunk 2077
- Crimson Desert
- Clair Obscur: Expedition 33
- Gothic 1 Remake
- No Man's Sky
- Kingdom Come: Deliverance II
- Gears of War: E-Day
- Hades II
- Phantom Blade Zero

Boosteroid's official X account, [@Boosteroid_main](https://x.com/Boosteroid_main/status/2120821617609642391), posted about the deal at 4:10 a.m. CT on November 12 (10:10 a.m. UTC). The post matches those dates and says there is "a chance to win one of 11 games from our special holiday lineup," with 10 winners.

## What the page leaves unpublished

The deal page and that official X post do not publish detailed official rules, eligibility requirements (such as age or residency), regional availability, or pricing in U.S. dollars. Those details are left out here because they were not listed.

## What Ultra Pro includes, according to Boosteroid

The deal page describes Ultra Pro as offering up to 4K resolution and 120 FPS "on premium hardware for all supported games," a choice of server location, extra inactivity time of up to 3 minutes to rejoin games, private gameplay streams for up to 15 spectators, and early access to new releases and platform upgrades. It also says users can "Play over 2000 PC games in the cloud." Those are Boosteroid's claims on the campaign page.

The deal is listed as available on Boosteroid throughout the campaign period, through the [Festive Frenzy Deal](https://boosteroid.com/festive-frenzy-deal/) page.
