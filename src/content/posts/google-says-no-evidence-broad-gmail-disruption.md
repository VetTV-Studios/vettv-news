Google [said](https://x.com/NewsFromGoogle/status/2122773134453657666) Tuesday that it is not seeing evidence of a broad Gmail service disruption, and pointed users to the [Google Workspace Status Dashboard](https://www.google.com/appsstatus/dashboard/) for real-time status.

"We want to be clear that we aren't currently seeing evidence of a broad Gmail service disruption, and you can always check real-time status on our Status Dashboard," the company's News from Google account, @NewsFromGoogle, [posted](https://x.com/NewsFromGoogle/status/2122773134453657666) on X at 1:25 p.m. CT on November 17, 2026. Google [replied](https://x.com/NewsFromGoogle/status/2122774332581757324) with nearly the same wording to Downdetector five minutes later.

## User reports, then Google's denial

The statement followed a [Downdetector post](https://x.com/downdetector/status/2122720480742232235) at 9:56 a.m. CT that said, "User reports indicate problems with Gmail since 8:28 AM EST." Downdetector is a crowdsourced website where people report service problems; it is not an official confirmation that a service is down, and a rise in reports does not by itself establish the scope of any disruption.

[TechCrunch reported](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/google-joins-the-tech-outage-trend-by-announcing-its-not-having-one/) that claims of a Gmail outage circulated on X on Tuesday, and that some users said they were having problems while others said they were not. TechCrunch framed Google's post as the company announcing it was not having an outage during a recent wave of tech disruptions. That framing is TechCrunch's.

## What the dashboard showed

The Status Dashboard linked from Google's post listed "No incidents" when checked at 4:26 p.m. CT on November 17, 2026, with a last-updated time of 10:26 p.m. UTC. The dashboard's current-week chart, which runs from November 10 through November 17, did not list a Gmail incident. Google's posts did not offer a technical explanation for the user reports.

## Other outages the same day and week

Google's statement came on a day when [Apple's System Status page](https://www.apple.com/support/systemstatus/) listed several service problems that the company later marked resolved. Those included an App Store outage from 11:45 a.m. to 3 p.m. Eastern, an iCloud Mail performance issue from 11:45 a.m. to 1:25 p.m. Eastern, and issues affecting iCloud Web Apps, AppleCare on Device, Apple Business, Apple School Manager, Schoolwork, and the Volume Purchase Program. The page was last updated at 5:27 p.m. Eastern Standard Time on November 17.

Last week, the FAA [ordered a nationwide ground stop](/posts/faa-nationwide-ground-stop-november-13-legacy-network/) after a legacy network failure.

Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton, speaking Tuesday on Fox Business's "The Claman Countdown," said officials were not seeing signs of a coordinated cyberattack behind a broader wave of technology outages. In [remarks also posted](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2122800369075994738) by the White House Rapid Response account, Clayton said: "We do not see the indicia of a coordinated cyberattack — let me just be clear about that — and we do not see the indicia of state-sponsored activity here." In a [separate clip](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2122799793218969745), he said, "This is not a cyber attack and this is not a coordinated effort by a nation-state."

[Fox Business reported](https://www.foxbusiness.com/technology/jay-clayton-addresses-concerns-amid-widespread-tech-outages) that Clayton pointed instead to institutions installing security patches and upgrading systems. "Lots of institutions, because these vulnerabilities have been identified, are patching. They're taking their systems down. They're upgrading them. They're putting patches in place," he said. He also said, "Some systems have to hand off to other systems, and those handoffs are not coming as expected." Clayton was speaking about the wider outages, not about Gmail. Fox Business reported that he linked a Tuesday National Security Agency network outage to patching after a PHP vulnerability.
