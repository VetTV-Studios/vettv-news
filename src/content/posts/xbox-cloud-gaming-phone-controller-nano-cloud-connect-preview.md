Microsoft began rolling out two Xbox Cloud Gaming previews on November 4. Xbox says both are still being tested, and that availability and supported games or devices may be limited.

## Your phone as a controller on a Smart TV

- **What it does:** on a supported Smart TV, a supported Android or iOS phone can act as your controller. The controls open in the phone's browser, and some games get controls tailored to how they play.
- **How to turn it on:** enable Preview Features under Settings > Account in the latest supported Smart TV experience. When you launch a game with no physical controller connected, you'll get the option to use your phone for that session. You connect by scanning a QR code shown on the TV.
- **Where:** rolling out on supported TVs in countries where Xbox Cloud Gaming is supported.

*Unconfirmed:* Xbox's post links to a "supported Smart TV" page but doesn't name the TV brands or models, so we haven't listed any here.

## Cloud connect for the XBOX Nano Controller (US only)

- **Device name:** Xbox calls it the "XBOX Nano Controller."
- **What it does:** instead of connecting to your TV over Bluetooth, cloud connect uses your Wi-Fi to connect the controller directly to the cloud server running your game. Xbox's latency wording is exactly: "That can help reduce input latency, so gameplay may feel more responsive."
- **Where:** the preview is for players in the US. For now it works on supported Smart TVs and on the web, and Xbox says it "won't work with every cloud gaming experience yet."
- **Setup:** use the XBOX beta app on Android, or the Xbox Accessories app on PC. **Setup through the PC app requires Xbox Insider enrollment.** The app walks you through connecting the controller to your Wi-Fi. Then open a supported cloud gaming experience and sign in with the same Xbox account you used during setup.

Xbox has a cloud connect setup guide on its support site. Feedback for both previews can be sent from the preview experiences. For cloud connect, that's the Feedback icon in the Xbox Accessories app on PC or in the Xbox mobile app.
