Apple's online store lists the [GAMR Active Game Controller](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/hspk2zm/a/gamr-active-game-controller) at **$199.95**, marked **Online only**.

The product page describes GAMR as "an innovative, pressure-sensitive game controller play pad" with **nine pressure-sensitive stepping zones** mapped to directional and A, B, X, and Y controls. It "connects wirelessly through Bluetooth to iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Mac and Apple Vision Pro." The listing also mentions a growing selection of compatible Apple Arcade and App Store games, plus free original games in the GAMR app. Those compatibility claims are the product listing's.

## Specs from the listing

- Manufacturer part number: **GMR-PP-AA-01** (UPC/EAN 860014565038)
- System requirements: **iOS 26.5 or later**, **tvOS 26.5 or later**, and **macOS Tahoe 26.3.1 or later**
- Bluetooth 5.0; USB-C charging cable in the box, plus a carry strap
- Size: 39 in. long, 37 in. wide, 0.3 in. high; weight 64 oz. / 1814 g
- Rechargeable lithium-ion battery, with a listed charge time of 2 hours and stand-by time of 10 days
- Battery life is listed as **"up to 8-10 hours of use"** — that figure is the listing's claim

## Not an Apple-branded product

A warranty note on the same Apple store page says products sold through the site that do not bear the Apple brand name "are serviced and supported exclusively by their manufacturers," and that "Apple's Limited Warranty does not apply to products that are not Apple branded, even if packaged or sold with Apple products."

*Opinion:* $199.95 is a lot for a third-party floor pad Apple is only reselling; the useful facts are the price, the Mac requirement, and that Apple's own warranty does not cover it.
