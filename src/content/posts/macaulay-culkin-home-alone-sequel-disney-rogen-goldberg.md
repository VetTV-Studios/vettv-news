Kevin McCallister might finally be coming home. A new *Home Alone* movie with Macaulay Culkin back as Kevin is in development at Disney, Puck's Matthew Belloni [reported on November 20, 2026](https://puck.news/hollywood/2026/11/macaulay-culkin-is-in-for-another-home-alone). According to Puck, Seth Rogen and Evan Goldberg and their Point Grey company "just closed their deal and will work with Culkin to crack a script."

Several trades followed the same day with their own reporting. [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/11/home-alone-macaulay-culkin-seth-rogen-evan-goldberg-1237230258/) said it had "confirmed with sources" that a sequel starring Culkin, with Rogen and Goldberg writing and producing, "is in early works at the Disney owned 20th Century Studios." [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/home-alone-reboot-disney-macaulay-culkin-seth-rogen-1236954344/), [IndieWire](https://www.indiewire.com/news/breaking-news/home-alone-sequel-macaulay-culkin-seth-rogen-evan-goldberg-1235229735/) and [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/movies/home-alone-sequel-in-the-works-with-macaulay-culkin/) reported the same project. Disney declined to comment, according to Puck, Deadline, Variety and IndieWire, so this is reported, not officially announced.

The timing is very Kevin. The news broke 34 years to the day after *Home Alone 2: Lost in New York* opened in U.S. theaters on November 20, 1992, according to [Box Office Mojo](https://www.boxofficemojo.com/title/tt0104431/).

## What's been reported so far

- **How it started:** Puck says Culkin visited the Disney lot in July for a sit-down with studio head David Greenbaum. Now that Culkin has two young sons, Puck says, "he's been more receptive to returning to the franchise."
- **The idea:** Deadline says Culkin pitched Greenbaum a sequel told from the point of view of "an older dad." Deadline and IndieWire both say Rogen and Goldberg are working with Culkin on his idea.
- **Who's making it:** Rogen and Goldberg are producing through Point Grey, per Puck, Deadline, Variety and IndieWire. Deadline and TheWrap say they are also writing it; Variety says they are shaping the script with Culkin.
- **What comes first:** Puck and Variety both report that Rogen and Goldberg are first prepping an original horror comedy for Universal that they plan to direct.
- **What's not known:** Variety says plot details and casting "remain under wraps," and IndieWire says no director is attached. Chris Columbus directed the first two films, and Deadline says there is "no word if he's coming back yet." No release date or platform has been announced.

## The '90s movie that never really left

*Home Alone* opened in U.S. theaters on November 16, 1990, and made $477.4 million worldwide, according to [Box Office Mojo](https://www.boxofficemojo.com/title/tt0099785/). Chris Columbus directed from a John Hughes screenplay, Variety notes, with Joe Pesci and Daniel Stern as the bumbling burglars known as the Wet Bandits and Catherine O'Hara in the cast as Kevin's mother. TheWrap notes the film was nominated for two Oscars.

*Home Alone 2: Lost in New York* sent Kevin to the big city two years later and made $359 million worldwide, per [Box Office Mojo](https://www.boxofficemojo.com/title/tt0104431/). Deadline puts the two Columbus films at a combined $836.3 million.

That was Culkin's last run as Kevin in a movie. Puck calls it "nuts" that he "hasn't made a sequel since Home Alone 2: Lost in New York in 1992."

## Every Home Alone since

- ***Home Alone 3*** (December 12, 1997) recast the lead with Alex D. Linz, with Raja Gosnell directing and Hughes still writing, according to Variety. It made $79.1 million worldwide, per [Box Office Mojo](https://www.boxofficemojo.com/title/tt0119303/).
- **The fourth and fifth films** were made for TV, Variety says.
- ***Home Sweet Home Alone*** premiered on Disney+ on November 12, 2021, [according to Disney+](https://press.disneyplus.com/news/disney-plus-to-unleash-comedic-mayhem-holiday-season-home-sweet-home-alome). It starred Ellie Kemper, Rob Delaney and Archie Yates, was directed by Dan Mazer, and featured Devin Ratray from the original *Home Alone*. It is officially the sixth *Home Alone*, as Puck puts it. Puck's take on it was not kind.

Disney inherited the franchise from Fox, as Puck puts it, and Puck says a returning Culkin is "maybe the key to unlocking a theatrical franchise." That's Puck's analysis; Disney hasn't said whether the new movie is meant for theaters or Disney+.

## Why fans still care

The original keeps coming back every holiday season. *Home Alone* was No. 9 on Nielsen's top 10 streaming movies chart this week, with 177 million minutes viewed in the U.S. on Disney+, according to Puck and Deadline.

Culkin got his star on the [Hollywood Walk of Fame](https://walkoffame.com/macaulay-culkin/) on December 1, 2023, the 2,765th, in the Motion Pictures category. Catherine O'Hara, his on-screen mom, spoke at the ceremony. "'Home Alone' was, is, and always will be a beloved global sensation," she said, [as quoted by KTLA](https://ktla.com/entertainment/macaulay-culkin-receives-star-on-hollywood-walk-of-fame/). KTLA reports that Culkin ended his speech with the famous line, "Merry Christmas, ya filthy animals."

He has also played a grown-up Kevin again this month. [McDonald's announced](https://corporate.mcdonalds.com/corpmcd/our-stories/article/mcdonalds-home-alone-meal.html) on November 17 a Home Alone Meal "with Macaulay Culkin as Kevin McCallister," available at participating U.S. restaurants starting December 1. Puck says the McDonald's ad is Culkin's first as Kevin since 2018.

## Our take

*This section is opinion.* *Home Alone* is about a kid who gets left behind. A sequel about the grown-up Kevin, possibly as a dad, is a natural flip for an audience that watched the first two films on VHS and now watches them with their own kids. Rogen and Goldberg are known for comedy with a bit of edge, but this is a family franchise, so the tone will be the thing to watch. And it all depends on a script that, according to Puck, the three of them are only now starting to crack. Until Disney says something, treat this as an early-development project, not a release date.
