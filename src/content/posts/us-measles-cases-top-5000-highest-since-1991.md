The United States has recorded 5,182 confirmed measles cases so far in 2026, according to the [Centers for Disease Control and Prevention](https://www.cdc.gov/measles/data-research/index.html), which counted cases reported as of November 5 and updated its page on November 6. That is the highest annual total since 1991, [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/general-news/us-measles-cases-rise-to-5182-cdc-says-5008804) reported, and more than double the 2,289 cases the CDC counted for all of 2025.

The new count came at the end of what [CIDRAP](https://www.cidrap.umn.edu/measles/us-measles-total-2026-tops-5100-cases), the University of Minnesota's infectious disease research center, called the deadliest week for measles this year. Health officials in New York, Pennsylvania and Wisconsin each reported a measles-associated death between November 2 and November 5.

## Where the numbers stand

The CDC says the 2026 cases were reported by 47 jurisdictions, with another 18 among international visitors. It counts 43 new outbreaks this year, and 96% of cases (4,993 of 5,182) are tied to outbreaks, per the [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/measles/data-research/index.html). People who were unvaccinated or whose vaccination status was unknown make up 96% of cases, [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/general-news/us-measles-cases-rise-to-5182-cdc-says-5008804) and [CIDRAP](https://www.cidrap.umn.edu/measles/us-measles-total-2026-tops-5100-cases) reported from the CDC data.

The CDC data show 562 hospitalizations so far this year, about 11% of cases, according to [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/general-news/us-measles-cases-rise-to-5182-cdc-says-5008804). CIDRAP said 330 of the cases were added this week, the 13th straight week of triple-digit increases, and that 59% of infections are in children and teens.

The CDC also notes that kindergarten MMR (measles, mumps and rubella) vaccine coverage fell from 95.2% in the 2019-2020 school year to 92.4% in 2025-2026, below the 95% level it says protects most people in a community, leaving about 280,000 kindergartners at risk, per the [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/measles/data-research/index.html).

## Three deaths in one week

- **New York:** The state health department and Lewis County Public Health said on November 2 that a child in Lewis County had died, the first known measles-associated death in the state in more than 30 years, per the [New York State Department of Health](https://health.ny.gov/press/releases/2026/2026-11-02_measles.htm).
- **Pennsylvania:** On November 4 the state confirmed its sixth measles-associated death of 2026, an unvaccinated Jefferson County resident, per the [Pennsylvania Department of Health](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/health/newsroom/pa-department-of-health-confirms-sixth-measles-associated-death). Its six confirmed deaths were residents of Jefferson (2), Lancaster (3) and Mifflin (1) counties. The department said it is aware of a reported October death in Franklin County but had not confirmed it as measles-associated.
- **Wisconsin:** On November 5 the state reported the death of an infant tied to the outbreak in southwestern Wisconsin, its first such death since 1990, per the [Wisconsin Department of Health Services](https://www.dhs.wisconsin.gov/news/releases/110526.htm). "Any death of a child is a tragedy and even more so when it is related to a vaccine-preventable disease," said DHS Secretary Kirsten Johnson.

Those state reports add up to at least eight confirmed measles-associated deaths this year. The CDC lists four 2026 deaths and says that number is subject to change, per the [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/measles/data-research/index.html). Reuters reported the gap comes from the CDC switching from state health department figures to National Center for Health Statistics mortality data to track measles deaths. The CDC recorded three measles deaths in 2025, [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/general-news/us-measles-cases-rise-to-5182-cdc-says-5008804) reported.

## The hardest-hit states

Pennsylvania has the most cases. As of November 6 it had confirmed 1,487 cases across 45 counties, with 287 hospitalizations, per the [Pennsylvania Department of Health](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/health/newsroom/pa-department-of-health-provides-statewide-update-on-measles-out16). The department said providers gave more than 61,000 MMR doses in October, compared with about 25,000 in a typical month. In the CDC data, South Carolina (670) and Utah (536) had the next-highest counts, [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/general-news/us-measles-cases-rise-to-5182-cdc-says-5008804) reported.

Wisconsin had reported 431 cases in 13 counties as of November 3, its highest in more than 35 years, per the [Wisconsin DHS](https://www.dhs.wisconsin.gov/news/releases/110526.htm). By Friday the state's count had reached 477, above the CDC's figure of 419, [CIDRAP](https://www.cidrap.umn.edu/measles/us-measles-total-2026-tops-5100-cases) reported. State counts often run ahead of the CDC's because the agency updates its page once a week, on Thursdays at noon, per the [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/measles/data-research/index.html).

## What happens next

The Pan American Health Organization's Measles and Rubella Elimination Regional Monitoring and Re-Verification Commission meets November 10 to 13 in a closed session to review every country's elimination status, using data for 2025 and the first half of 2026, per [PAHO](https://www.paho.org/en/news/14-10-2026-measles-and-rubella-elimination-regional-monitoring-and-re-verification-commission). A country loses elimination status when one chain of transmission of the same viral strain runs for 12 months or longer. The commission's conclusions go to the PAHO Director, and a final report follows. The commission found last year that endemic measles had returned to Canada, which is currently the only country in the Americas classified as endemic, per [PAHO](https://www.paho.org/en/news/14-10-2026-measles-and-rubella-elimination-regional-monitoring-and-re-verification-commission).

The United States has held measles elimination status since 2000, per the [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/measles/data-research/index.html). CIDRAP reported the country is expected to lose it after this week's meeting. **Unconfirmed:** PAHO has not announced any decision, and that expectation comes from experts and unnamed sources who spoke to CIDRAP.

"I think we're going to see measles again next year, and the year after that, and the year after that, until we get on top of vaccinating our populace," Dr. Paul Offit of Children's Hospital of Philadelphia said during a Brown University Pandemic Center webinar, per [CIDRAP](https://www.cidrap.umn.edu/measles/us-measles-total-2026-tops-5100-cases).

Health officials in all three states said the MMR vaccine is the best protection against measles. Two doses are 97% effective at preventing it, per the [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/measles/data-research/index.html).
