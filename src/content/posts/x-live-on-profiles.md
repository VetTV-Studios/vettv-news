X's official Creators account, [@XCreators](https://x.com/XCreators/status/2125013157873938923), posted Monday, November 23, at 5:46 p.m. CT:

"Your streams now have a home on your profile."

"The new Live filter shows your live broadcast first, with every past stream right below, so new viewers can catch up on what they missed."

The post included an official promo graphic with the headline "Live on Profiles" and the line "Find live and past streams on any profile."

![Official X promo graphic for Live on Profiles, showing a SpaceX profile with the Live filter open](/images/x-live-on-profiles/image-1.jpg)
*Official X promo graphic for Live on Profiles. Image: X.*

The graphic shows a phone mockup of the SpaceX profile. A Live tab is selected, and an open menu lists Videos, Photos, and Livestreams, with Livestreams checked. Under that tab, a "Live now" section shows a current broadcast, and a "Past livestreams" heading appears below the player. The mockup is an iPhone. X did not say which platforms have the filter, or whether the layout matches the mockup on every device. SpaceX is the example profile in the graphic; the company did not announce the feature.

## What is still unconfirmed

The link in the @XCreators post points back to the photo on that post, not to a help article. As of Monday evening, November 23, a search did not turn up an official X help page or blog post that documents the new filter. @XCreators did not add a follow-up in the thread with more detail.

This article does not state a rollout schedule, region, or app requirement beyond what that post and graphic show.

**Our take (opinion):** putting a live broadcast and past streams in one profile filter should make it easier for someone who lands on an account to find the video, instead of scrolling the main timeline. How widely the tab is actually appearing, and on which apps, is still unconfirmed.
