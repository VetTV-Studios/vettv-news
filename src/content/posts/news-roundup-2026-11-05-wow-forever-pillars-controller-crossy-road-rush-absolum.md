## World of Warcraft: Forever is live

Blizzard says World of Warcraft: Forever is now live. It's a permanent new home alongside modern WoW and Classic, set after Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom and before Molten Core. Highlights from Blizzard's post:

- **New and reimagined zones** with more than 1,000 new quests across levels 1–60, including Mount Hyjal, Shen'dralas, the Riverglades and Zephras Isle.
- **The Skyborne,** a new playable race that can be Horde Shaman or Alliance Mages.
- **New race-class combinations** such as Orc Mages and Undead Paladins.
- **A Classic or Enhanced experience preset** when you first log in, and Rulesets in place of realm selection. Hardcore comes after launch.
- **A camping system, an account-wide Legacy progression system, and refined gamepad support.**

## Pillars of Eternity and Deadfire: controller support public test

Obsidian's PC Controller Support Public Test for Pillars of Eternity (patch 1.3.10.0) and Pillars of Eternity II: Deadfire (patch 5.1.0.0) is live on Xbox on PC, Steam and GOG. It's a public test: Obsidian says to expect bugs, and the test will keep running ahead of a full release.

- **Full controller support:** both games can be played start to finish with a controller, in Real-Time with Pause or Turn-Based Mode. You can switch to mouse and keyboard at any time.
- **Deadfire changes:** its Turn-Based Mode is reworked, so turns follow character speed and combat is deadlier. You can also switch TBM on or off mid-playthrough.
- **How to opt in on Steam:** Properties > Betas > `public_beta`.
- **Steam Deck:** Obsidian says to set the controller layout to the "Gamepad with Joystick Trackpad" template (or "Gamepad") before launching. Both games now support the on-screen keyboard for naming characters.

## Crossy Road Rush arrives on Apple Arcade

Hipster Whale and Atari launched Crossy Road Rush today as an Apple Arcade exclusive. It's an arcade-action platforming runner with more than 50 hand-curated levels, a Survival Mode with global leaderboards and daily challenges, and up to four-player local co-op plus online multiplayer. The App Store lists it for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Vision and Apple TV, with controller support.

## Absolum on iOS and Android

Playdigious said in September that Dotemu and Guard Crush Games' roguelite beat 'em up Absolum would release on iOS and Android on November 5 (via Gematsu, secondary). The mobile version has touch controls, controller support and cloud saves between iOS and Android. Cross-progression with PC through a Google Play Games account is listed for Android only. *We hadn't confirmed the store listings were live when we wrote this.*
