Anthropic, the company behind Claude, has set the price for its initial public offering at **$83 a share**. The company [announced it on X](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2123196808675405860) at 5:29 p.m. CT on November 18, writing that trading was "expected to begin on Nasdaq tomorrow," and published [a formal statement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering) the same day.

That price is above the $70 to $78 range the company had marketed, according to [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/18/nx-s1-6046212/anthropic-record-ipo-price) and [Bloomberg](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/mergers-and-acquisitions/anthropic-is-said-to-price-ipo-at-83-per-share). NPR reported that the prospectus had targeted 825 million shares in that range.

## The numbers, from the filing

Anthropic's [pricing term sheet on SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026074937/anthropicpbc-fwppricing.htm) lays out the deal:

- **Ticker:** ANT, on the Nasdaq Global Select Market.
- **Shares:** 980,000,000 Class A shares. Anthropic is selling 956,351,354, and selling stockholders are selling 23,648,646. Anthropic says it [will not receive any proceeds](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering) from the selling stockholders' shares.
- **Offering size:** $81,340,000,000, not counting the underwriters' option.
- **Underwriters' option:** up to 147,000,000 more Class A shares over 30 days, all sold by the company.
- **Concurrent private placement:** $26.0 billion of Class N common stock, or 313,253,007 shares, at the IPO price.
- **Dates:** priced November 18, trade date November 19, settlement November 20.
- **Lead bookrunners:** Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley.

By our math from those figures, the 956,351,354 shares Anthropic sold come to about $79.4 billion at $83, which matches [NPR's figure](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/18/nx-s1-6046212/anthropic-record-ipo-price) for what the company itself raised. [Bloomberg](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/mergers-and-acquisitions/anthropic-is-said-to-price-ipo-at-83-per-share) put the combined IPO and private placement at a record $107.3 billion. The filing says the registration statement was declared effective by the SEC on November 18.

[AP](https://apnews.com/article/anthropic-ipo-pricing-70b660ddf900d90bbf484ecdf1fb441c) reported that the price values Anthropic at $1.5 trillion and called it the biggest Wall Street debut of all time. That valuation is AP's figure; it does not appear in the term sheet we reviewed. NPR and Bloomberg both compared the offering with SpaceX's IPO earlier this year, which NPR put at $75 billion.

## When it trades

Anthropic says the shares are expected to begin trading on November 19, and the offering is expected to close on November 20, subject to customary closing conditions. As of 8:15 a.m. CT on November 19, before the regular Nasdaq session opened, ANT had not traded, so there is no first-trade price to report yet. We will add the opening price, with its time and source, once it is reported.
