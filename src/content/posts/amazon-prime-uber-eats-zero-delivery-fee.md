Amazon and Uber announced a new U.S. Prime benefit on November 5: restaurant orders placed through Amazon and powered by Uber Eats now carry a **$0 Delivery Fee** when the order is $25 or more.

The companies' [press release](https://press.aboutamazon.com/2026/11/amazon-launches-partnership-with-uber-eats-featuring-0-delivery-fee-for-prime-members) and Amazon's [aboutamazon.com story](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/prime-uber-eats-free-delivery) say members can start today on [Amazon.com](https://www.amazon.com/ubereats) or in the Amazon Shopping app by searching "Uber Eats." No new app, Uber account or separate credentials are required. Amazon News also [posted](https://x.com/amazonnews/status/2118298497728368847) the announcement on X.

## What's included

According to Amazon:

- **$0 Delivery Fee** on Uber Eats restaurant orders of **$25 or more** placed on Amazon from a qualifying restaurant
- Available to **all U.S. Prime members**, including half-price **Prime for Young Adults** and **Prime Access**
- Access to Uber Eats' restaurant network "across nearly ten thousand cities and towns," including national chains such as Wingstop, Chipotle and Sweetgreen plus local restaurants
- Launch promo: for the **first 14 days**, **$20 off each of the first two** Uber Eats orders of $25 or more
- **Prime Visa** cardmembers earn **5% back** on all Uber Eats orders through **December 5, 2026** (eligible Prime membership required)

Jamil Ghani, worldwide vice president of Amazon Prime, said in the release: "Whether it's game night, family night, or date night, Prime's new Uber Eats membership benefit makes getting dinner delivered even easier." Andrew Macdonald, Uber's president and COO, said the companies built an experience "designed to make restaurant delivery feel as natural, convenient, and affordable as ordering anything else on Uber Eats."

## How to order

Amazon's how-to:

1. Go to [amazon.com/ubereats](https://www.amazon.com/ubereats) or search "Uber Eats" in the Shopping app
2. Sign in with existing Amazon credentials, browse nearby restaurants and pay with existing Amazon payment methods
3. Amazon sends email and push delivery updates; Uber Eats sends a final order confirmation

Members can also share the waived delivery-fee benefit with one adult in the household through Amazon Family, the company says.

## Savings claims and caveats

Amazon says Prime-exclusive offers on Amazon could mean "potentially saving more than $175 per year"—more than the current annual Prime price of $139. That figure is Amazon's estimate. The footnote says it is based on a "typical number" of Uber Eats orders, annualized from **August 2026** order data, comparing Amazon-side discounts with ordering on Uber Eats directly **without Uber One**. Savings per order "will vary and are not guaranteed," and offers can change.

Prime itself remains **$14.99 per month** or **$139 per year**, with a free 30-day trial if eligible.

*Opinion:* folding restaurant delivery into the Amazon app is a clear convenience play. Treat the "$175 a year" number as Amazon's modeled estimate, not a promise for every household.
