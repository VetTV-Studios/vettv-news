[Professor Layton and the New World of Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3384600/Professor_Layton_and_the_New_World_of_Steam/) is scheduled to launch on December 9, 2026, according to its Steam store page. LEVEL-5 Inc. is listed as both developer and publisher. The game is available to pre-purchase on Steam for $64.99.

In a section headed "An Intuitive, Hands-On Puzzle-Solving Experience," the page says: "Steam Deck™ supports touchscreen controls." That is LEVEL-5's statement on the store listing. The store page's compatibility data does not assign a Verified, Playable, or Unsupported Steam Deck rating.

The listing describes "a new adventure in Professor Layton, the puzzle-filled fantasy adventure series that has sold over 20 million copies worldwide." It sets the story in Steam Bison, "a remarkable town of invention powered by steam engine technology," after the end of the Wild West era, with Professor Layton and Luke drawn into a mysterious case. Those sales and story details are from the store page.

A pre-order bonus, "Layton Series Soundtrack Selection," is described as 11 tracks from the series, available to players who pre-order by 11:59 p.m. JST on December 9, 2026. System requirements on the page currently list Windows 11 64-bit, with additional notes marked "TBD."
