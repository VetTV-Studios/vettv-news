NVIDIA's Board of Directors [announced on November 12](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-CEO-Transition/default.aspx) that Jensen Huang "has resigned as President, Chief Executive Officer, and a member of the Board, effective immediately." The same text appears on the [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition).

A [Form 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000082/nvda-20261111.htm) filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on November 12, with a date of earliest event of November 11, 2026, says that on November 11 the board "accepted the resignation of Jen-Hsun Huang, as NVIDIA's President, Chief Executive Officer and member of the Board, effective immediately."

## Interim CEO and board chair

The press release says Colette Kress, NVIDIA's executive vice president and chief financial officer, "has agreed to serve as Interim Chief Executive Officer." It says she will continue as executive vice president and chief financial officer "while serving as Interim Chief Executive Officer until a permanent successor has been named." The board "is initiating a search for a permanent Chief Executive Officer and will consider internal and external candidates."

The 8-K identifies her as Colette M. Kress, age 59, and says she has served as executive vice president and chief financial officer since 2013. The press release says she has held that role since joining NVIDIA in September 2013, and that she previously spent nearly 25 years in finance roles at companies including Cisco and Microsoft.

The board appointed Harvey C. Jones as "its first-ever Chair of the Board," the release says. The 8-K says that appointment was also made on November 11, that Jones is 73, that he has served on the board since 1993, and that he "has been the Managing Partner of Square Wave Ventures, a private family office, since 2004." The press release also notes prior service as chief executive officer and executive chairman of Synopsys.

Jones, quoted in the release as chair, said: "Colette has helped lead NVIDIA through a period of extraordinary growth and has a deep understanding of our business, customers, operations and financial position." He added, "The Board has full confidence in Colette and the leadership team, and we expect the company's operations, customer commitments and product roadmap to continue without interruption."

Kress, quoted as interim CEO and CFO, said: "NVIDIA's strength has always come from its people, and together with our leadership team, I am committed to building on the extraordinary foundation Jensen created over the last 33 years." She said the company's focus "remains on serving our customers, partners, developers and shareholders."

The release adds: "Jensen's contributions to NVIDIA are profound. NVIDIA is one of the most consequential technology companies in the world. The Board and the company thank Jensen for his immeasurable contributions to the company and the industry."

## What the company said, and what it left unsaid

The November 12 press release gives no reason for the resignation and includes no quote from Huang.

[Politico reported](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/11/12/nvidia-ousts-jensen-huang-amid-reports-of-affairs-with-two-women-01161630) on November 12 that the resignation followed reports of affairs with two women. Those reports are Politico's account, not a finding stated in NVIDIA's announcement.

Huang did address earlier reporting in a staff email dated November 8, which [NVIDIA Newsroom published](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/a-message-to-our-company) the same day under the headline "A Message to Our Company." In that email, Huang wrote: "I was unfaithful to my wife. My actions hurt her and our children. There is no excuse for what I did. I am deeply sorry." He also wrote that, "Regarding the national security concerns raised in news reports, NVIDIA's Board of Directors has initiated an independent investigation. I support the investigation and will cooperate fully." The November 12 resignation announcement does not mention those reports or that investigation, and it includes no further comment from Huang.

## Staff emails and the earnings date

[CNBC reported](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-kress-jones-employee-emails.html) on November 12 that it obtained staff emails from Kress and Jones. In the email CNBC published under Kress's name, she wrote: "You will hear more from leadership in the coming days about our future together, and we will host a company meeting on November 18 at 9am PT." That is 11 a.m. CT on November 18. The emails were reported by CNBC, not posted on NVIDIA's investor or Newsroom sites.

NVIDIA said it will report third-quarter fiscal 2027 financial results on Tuesday, November 17, at approximately 3:20 p.m. CT (1:20 p.m. PT), "as previously scheduled," followed by its earnings call at 4 p.m. CT (2 p.m. PT).
