Grand Theft Auto VI [launches](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI) Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, [Rockstar Games](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak3ak31a49a221/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-set-to-launch-november-19-2026) says. Official U.S. store listings go a step further on the clock: the [PlayStation Store](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/concept/10000730) product data for the U.S. locale, and the [Microsoft Store](https://www.xbox.com/en-us/games/store/grand-theft-auto-vi/9P3H4968GRSM/0017) catalog for the U.S. market, both timestamp the release to 2026-11-19T05:00:00Z, which is 11:00 p.m. CT on Wednesday, November 18, or 12:00 a.m. Eastern on Thursday, November 19.

That U.S. timestamp is a single nationwide store unlock, not a separate midnight in each U.S. time zone. The same listings on the [U.K. PlayStation Store](https://store.playstation.com/en-gb/product/EP1004-PPSA01547_00-GTAVISTANDARD001) and in the Microsoft Store's U.K. catalog timestamp to 2026-11-19T00:00:00Z, midnight Greenwich Mean Time, which is 6:00 p.m. CT on Wednesday. Those two official store clocks line up with PlayStation's description of a rolling launch by country.

## What official pages say about the unlock, in their own words

PlayStation Support's [U.S. Grand Theft Auto VI page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/games/gta-vi-support/) says: "Grand Theft Auto VI releases at midnight local time on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation®5 consoles."

The [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/), in a November 10 post by Tim Turi of Sony Interactive Entertainment, is more specific about how that midnight rolls: "We've also adopted a rolling midnight launch by country, beginning at 12:00AM NZDT in New Zealand and running across the globe as respective countries hit midnight on November 19."

Rockstar's own [Newswire announcement](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak3ak31a49a221/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-set-to-launch-november-19-2026) of the current date says, "Grand Theft Auto VI will now release on Thursday, November 19, 2026." The [official game site](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI) repeats "Coming November 19, 2026" for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Neither Rockstar page publishes a city-by-city clock.

Xbox Wire has not posted a native-console unlock hour for the United States. It has posted a time for cloud play. A [November 10 Xbox Wire post](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/) says Grand Theft Auto VI "will be playable on day one through Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S, starting at 1am ET." That is 12:00 a.m. CT on Thursday, November 19, one hour after the U.S. Microsoft Store timestamp. The same post says cloud streams use Xbox Series S graphics settings, and that the game must be purchased separately. Xbox Wire's [November 13 "Next Week on Xbox" post](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/13/next-week-on-xbox-new-games-for-november-16-to-20/) repeats the 1:00 a.m. ET cloud start and says pre-install is available so players are "ready to play at launch."

New Zealand is first. 12:00 a.m. NZDT on November 19 is 5:00 a.m. CT on Wednesday, November 18.

Players should still check the unlock time shown on their own store account. The official prose on PlayStation Support ("midnight local time") is broader than the U.S. store timestamps, which use a single Eastern midnight for the country.

## Platforms

At launch, official materials list only PlayStation 5, including PS5 Pro Enhanced, and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar's site also uses the line "Plays Best On PlayStation 5." The [PlayStation U.S. game page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/) says the game "will only be playable on PlayStation 5 consoles. It will not be available for PlayStation 4 consoles." It lists the title as a "single-player experience" and marks "Remote Play supported."

On Xbox, the [Microsoft Store product page](https://www.xbox.com/en-us/games/store/grand-theft-auto-vi/9P3H4968GRSM/0017) lists Xbox Series X|S. Xbox One is not a native platform. Xbox Wire says Xbox One players can stream the game through Xbox Cloud Gaming starting at 1:00 a.m. ET on November 19.

No official PC version or PC date appears on the Rockstar, PlayStation, Xbox, or Take-Two pages checked on November 17, 2026. Third-party rumors of a later PC release are unverified and are not treated as fact here.

## Editions and U.S. prices

Take-Two's [June 24, 2026, press release](https://taketwointeractivesoftwareinc.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi/) priced the Standard Edition at $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition at $99.99. Those figures were still on official U.S. store listings on the evening of November 17, 2026: $79.99 for the Standard Edition on the [PlayStation Store](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/concept/10000730) and the [Microsoft Store](https://www.xbox.com/en-us/games/store/grand-theft-auto-vi/9P3H4968GRSM/0017), and $99.99 for the [Ultimate Edition](https://www.xbox.com/en-us/games/store/grand-theft-auto-vi-ultimate-edition/9NNZSNHLR63L) on the Microsoft Store. The PlayStation Store U.S. listing also showed $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition. The Microsoft Store additionally lists a $20 Ultimate Edition Upgrade.

The Ultimate Edition, in Take-Two's words, "amplifies this experience with an exclusive collection of premium vehicles, weapons, apparel, and action threaded across all aspects of Jason and Lucia's story." A detailed contents list is on the [PlayStation U.S. game page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/).

PlayStation Support lists a Standard Edition on the PlayStation Store and as a physical code-in-box copy, and an Ultimate Edition on the PlayStation Store. Upgrades from Standard, including boxed Standard, are available only from the PlayStation Store, that page says.

Pre-order bonus wording is not identical across official pages, so it should not be flattened into one cutoff. Take-Two said all pre-orders and purchases before November 20, 2026, include the Vintage Vice City Pack, with a free month of GTA+ for digital pre-orders. The PlayStation U.S. FAQ uses "before November 20, 2026" for the GTA+ claim steps and says the month must be claimed by March 31, 2027. The legal footer on the same PlayStation page says "by November 19, 2026." Xbox Wire and the Microsoft Store product page say, "Purchase prior to 19 November 2026 23:59:59 to receive The Vintage Vice City Pack and one month of GTA+." Players should read the offer on their own store page.

## Pre-load, file size and physical copies

Pre-loading is already open. Rockstar's [November 10 Newswire post](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now) and [pre-load page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/pre-load-now) say pre-loading is available for everyone who has pre-ordered digitally, and that code-in-box owners can activate the code and begin as soon as it is received.

PlayStation Support says pre-load started at midnight local time on November 10, 2026. The [PlayStation U.S. game page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/) tells players to clear a minimum of 212 GB on a PS5. [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/games/1025271/gta-vi-storage-space-rockstar-games), quoting Rockstar on November 3, reported that the pre-load file requires up to 212 GB "depending on your device," with Xbox Series X|S pre-load starting at 12:00 a.m. GMT on November 10 (6:00 p.m. CT on November 9). Take-Two's June press release had said digital pre-loading would begin November 12; that earlier date was superseded.

The physical edition is a download code in a box, not a disc. PlayStation Support says: "Physical (Code-In-Box) versions will only contain a game download code inside the box to support pre-load. A disc will not be included in the box." VetTV News has already reported [buyer accounts of missing or already-used boxed codes](/posts/gta-6-boxed-download-code-missing-or-used/), [the official pre-load window and 212 GB figure](/posts/gta-vi-pre-load-times-212gb-november-10/), and [Spectrum's temporary gig-speed offer around the launch window](/posts/spectrum-gig-speed-lift-gta-vi-download-november-2026/). An earlier VetTV piece had treated a 200 GB PS5 figure as an [unconfirmed rumor](/posts/gta-6-ps5-install-size-rumor/); the later official wording is "up to 212 GB," depending on the device.

## What is not official yet

Rockstar has not, in the official pages checked on November 17, announced a review-embargo date or time. Reports that reviews may appear on November 18 remain unconfirmed and will not be treated as fact until Rockstar or the outlets themselves publish. A reviews roundup will follow only after scores and links can be taken from the published reviews.

Rockstar also had not posted official day-one patch notes or a final install size beyond the "up to 212 GB" pre-load figure, as of that check. Any patch or performance story will wait for an official note or a first-party platform post.

The [PlayStation U.S. FAQ](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi/) lists the game as rated M (Mature 17+) by the ESRB in North America and PEGI 18 in Europe. The [Microsoft Store product page](https://www.xbox.com/en-us/games/store/grand-theft-auto-vi/9P3H4968GRSM/0017) also lists Mature 17+ with the same content descriptors shown on Rockstar's site: Blood and Gore, Intense Violence, Mature Humor, Nudity, Strong Language, Strong Sexual Content, and Use of Drugs and Alcohol. Xbox's separate [marketing page](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/grand-theft-auto-vi) still showed "Rating Pending" when fetched on November 17. The store listing and PlayStation FAQ are the clearer current rating statements.
