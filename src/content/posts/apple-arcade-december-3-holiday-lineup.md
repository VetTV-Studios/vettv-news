Apple's Newsroom, in a **November 10, 2026** update, said **Apple Arcade** will add four games on **December 3**: **Plants vs. Zombies: Replanted**, **solitaire(ish)**, **Prince of Persia: The Lost Crown+**, and **LEGO DUPLO Playtime**.

Apple describes the December titles this way:

- **Plants vs. Zombies: Replanted**, from PopCap Studios, adds new levels and modes, local co-op and PvP on the same device, and an Arcade-exclusive Party mode.
- **solitaire(ish)**, from Playstack, is a roguelike twist on solitaire with perk cards and more than **150** special cards.
- **Prince of Persia: The Lost Crown+**, from Ubisoft, comes with all downloadable content, including the Mask of Darkness story expansion.
- **LEGO DUPLO Playtime**, from StoryToys, is described as an open-ended digital toy chest for mixing LEGO DUPLO bricks.

The same post says **Football Manager 27 Touch**, from Sports Interactive, is **available on Apple Arcade starting today** (November 10). Feature descriptions in the announcement are Apple's marketing copy.

Apple says Arcade Originals play across iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, and Apple Vision Pro, while App Store Greats are available on iPhone, iPad, and Apple Vision Pro. The November 10 post does not say which of the December 3 games fall into which category, so Mac availability for each title is not stated there. Apple lists Arcade at **$6.99** a month in the U.S., with a one-month free trial, and says one subscription covers a family of up to six.
