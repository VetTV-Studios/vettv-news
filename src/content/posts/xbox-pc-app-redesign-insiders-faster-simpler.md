Xbox Insiders can try an **updated Xbox PC app** starting **November 5**, Xbox Wire says in a post by Jason Beaumont, VP of Xbox Experiences. Microsoft frames the work as making Xbox **"faster, simpler, and more familiar"** on PC, web, and cloud.

## Xbox PC app (Insiders)

Per Xbox Wire:

- **Launch and load:** the app launches **up to 60% faster**; Home, Store, and Game Pass load **up to 50% faster**; in-app windows open **up to 65% faster**. Microsoft footnotes that figures come from its testing and may vary by device, connection, config, and usage.
- **Library:** simpler navigation, updated filters, and new ways to organize games.
- **Friends:** friends and social features move into **main navigation**—see what friends are playing, start a chat, or join a party.
- **Search:** search across more of Xbox from one place (games including installed titles and add-ons, plus people).
- **Look:** refreshed game cards, Home, Settings, and animations.

## Cloud web and other screens

Earlier this year, Insiders previewed a refreshed Xbox cloud gaming experience on the web. Over the **next few weeks**, Microsoft says it will bring that refresh to more players automatically; anyone who wants it sooner can go to [play.xbox.com](https://play.xbox.com). The update adds a Library tab for cloud-playable games (owned or Game Pass), a desktop mode for larger screens, and dark/light modes plus five high-contrast options.

Microsoft is also **beginning** to bring the refreshed look and navigation to Xbox cloud gaming apps on **supported TVs and VR headsets**.

## How to get in

Xbox Wire says to join the Xbox Insider Program by downloading the **Xbox Insider Hub** for Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, or Windows PC. The article does **not** spell out a Previews submenu path; treat Hub install plus Insider enrollment as what Microsoft published here. Feedback link: aka.ms/Xboxplayervoice.
