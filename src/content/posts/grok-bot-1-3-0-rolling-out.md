Grok Bot **1.3.0 is rolling out**. The official [Grok Bot changelog](https://x.ai/changelog/bot) lists that version under the heading "Smarter Slack Bots, restock alerts, org controls," dated **November 24, 2026**. The rollout was [announced on X](https://x.com/ericzakariasson/status/2125675144173556124) on November 25 at 1:37 p.m. CT, and [Elon Musk reposted the announcement](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2125877299849425282) on November 26 at 3:00 a.m. CT.

The announcement post says, "grok @bot 1.3.0 is rolling out!" and lists "smarter slack bots, restock alerts and mobile buy now, automatic teams setup with org controls, a sideways x drafts carousel, the ashby connector, and bcc on email drafts." An official graphic posted with that announcement is marked "1.3.0" and "Nov 24," and it counts "25 new / 34 improved / 43 fixed." We are reporting those totals as they appear on that graphic, not as an independent recount of the changelog.

This is the first numbered Grok Bot release we have covered since [1.0 / 1.0.1](/posts/grok-bot-1-0-out-of-beta/). xAI's news page has not posted a separate 1.3.0 article; the changelog and the X announcement are the primary sources.

## Slack, shopping, and org controls

On Slack, the changelog says that when a Bot hands work to another Bot, "the answer comes back in your Slack direct message, with a Connect button for anything that Bot still needs." "Saying a Bot's name in a Slack group direct message wakes that Bot, the same as an @mention does." In a one-to-one Slack direct message, "the Bot can answer in the main chat instead of always opening a thread." Under improvements, "Slack replies are one short plain paragraph, without bold labels, headings, or lists unless you asked for a breakdown," and "The Bot follows Slack channel etiquette, such as not answering a thank-you with another message."

On shopping, "A sold-out product card offers Notify me, which watches that seller and tells you when the item is back in stock." "A single-product card on iPhone and Android shows Buy now whenever the chat can buy it." "When your X Money balance is short, the purchase is refused before a card is issued and the Bot asks you to add funds or pay another way." A saved card "its bank refused shows Needs an update in your Vault."

The changelog says an organization "can limit its members to connecting accounts on the organization's own domains." "Team admins can require approval before someone who opens an invite link joins, and review those requests on a Requests tab on the Members page."

## Other additions in the 1.3.0 entry

The same changelog entry also lists an **Ashby** recruiting connector: "With Ashby connected, the Bot can answer recruiting questions about candidates, interviews, jobs, and offers." "An email draft can carry a Bcc, on the draft card and in Mac Mail." An editable Gmail draft that shows a signature "has a Remove signature control." Settings "lists the devices on your account and can remove a phone that is offline." "The Bot can name who was in a meeting it joined."

Some items in that same entry are marked as needing an older minimum Bot version, including Microsoft work-account and Outlook connection notes. Those version tags are xAI's; we have not independently tested which clients have 1.3.0 yet.

The announcement describes 1.3.0 as rolling out. It is not a claim that every account already has the build.
