Anthropic says you can now turn Claude artifacts into standalone desktop apps. In [Turn artifacts into Mac and Windows apps](https://claude.com/resources/articles/install-as-app), the company writes, "Starting today, you can use Claude artifacts to create, run, and share desktop apps. The "Install as app" feature is rolling out in beta in Claude for Mac and Windows."

[9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/24/anthropic-just-gave-claude-users-a-new-way-to-make-mac-apps/) reported the launch at 1:56 p.m. CT on November 24. [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1041882/anthropic-artifact-desktop-apps-mac-windows) followed at 5:04 p.m. CT the same day.

When an artifact is installed, Anthropic says, "it gets its own icon and opens in its own window instead of inside Claude." You can share it with others. Each app can use Claude and your connectors, and "it updates automatically when the underlying artifact is edited." Requests the app makes to Claude "count toward your plan's usage limits."

The [Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/18516354-install-an-artifact-as-an-app), which showed as updated November 25 when we checked, says the feature needs **Claude for Mac or Claude for Windows, version 6.28899.0 or later**. You have to be online and signed in to Claude to open an installed app.

## Permission tiers

Anthropic describes three levels of permissions:

- **Default:** "Installed apps can only do what they'd be able to do in a browser."
- **App permissions:** apps can ask for features Claude can tightly control, "like creating always-on-top windows, listening for global shortcuts, or sending notifications." The help center also lists menu bar items on macOS and system tray icons on Windows.
- **Full computer access:** "Apps can ask to run programs and read, change, or delete your files without asking each time."

Anthropic says your permission "only covers the current version of the app, so Claude asks again after every update, even your own edits." If part of an app comes from another website, "that part can change without Claude asking you again."

You can only give full access "to apps you made or, on Team and Enterprise, apps made by people in your organization." Anthropic "doesn't review apps before you install them, so only give full access to apps you trust."

## Who can use it, and what cannot be installed

Anthropic says Install as app is rolling out in beta "on all plans." The help center names **Free, Pro, Max, Ultra, Team, and Enterprise**. HIPAA-ready organizations and hybrid deployments cannot use it, the article says. On **Team**, the feature is on by default, with full access off until an admin enables it. On **Enterprise**, the entire feature is "off until an owner turns it on."

Some artifacts cannot be installed, including Claude Docs, Slides, and Sheets. The help center also says apps are not available on Linux, web, or mobile, and that legacy artifacts and live artifacts from Claude Cowork cannot be installed.

To try it, Anthropic says: update Claude for Mac or Windows, open an artifact, and choose "Install as app" from the Share menu, or from the artifact's title menu.
