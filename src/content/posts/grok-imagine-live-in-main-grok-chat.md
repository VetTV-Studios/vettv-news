The official Grok Imagine account, [@imagine](https://x.com/imagine/status/2121672946968318285), posted on November 14 at 12:33 p.m. CT (6:33 p.m. UTC): "Image and video creation now live in Grok. Just open Grok and start creating."

A minute later, the main [@grok](https://x.com/grok/status/2121673185540378797) account quoted that post and wrote: "We're bringing more @spacexai products together into one Grok experience, so there's one place to create, build and get work done." Neither announcement names other products in the merge, gives a timeline, or says what happens to any standalone Imagine screens.

SpaceX Super Intelligence's [Grok product page](https://x.ai/grok) now lists Imagine alongside Chat, Multi-agent, and Search, and says users can "generate images and video from text prompts or reference photos" and "restyle, edit, and iterate without leaving the conversation." The same page says the chat "generates images, video, and code in the same thread," with images "up to 2K resolution and 15-second videos," and it lists text-to-video "up to 15 seconds at 720p."

## The layoff report is still unconfirmed

The product posts came a day after a report about staffing on the Imagine team. On November 13 at 9:40 a.m. CT, The Information's [Aaron Tilley](https://x.com/aatilley/status/2121266902358229070) posted: "Scoop: SpaceX has axed the team (roughly 60 people) behind AI video generation tool Grok Imagine. It's still live for now, but the company is shifting resources elsewhere." He named Claudia Chong and Juro Osawa as co-authors.

As of November 15, a search of Elon Musk's and official SpaceX and SpaceX AI posts found no public confirmation or denial of that report. The official November 14 announcements do not mention staffing, layoffs, or the size of the Imagine team. This article does not treat the "roughly 60" figure as confirmed, and it does not assume the product change was caused by that report.
