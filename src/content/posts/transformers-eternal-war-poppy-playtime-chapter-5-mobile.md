Two mobile games highlighted in November 12 coverage are listed as available on iOS and Android: **Transformers: Eternal War** and **Poppy Playtime Chapter 5**. Official store dates do not match every "launches today" headline from that coverage, so the dates below come from the App Store and Google Play listings.

## Transformers: Eternal War

[Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/transformers-eternal-war/out-now-on-mobile/) wrote on November 12 that Transformers: Eternal War "has launched on iOS and Android." The [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/transformers-eternal-war/id6772922295) names the game Transformers: Eternal War, prices it as free, and gives a release date of November 11, 2026 (08:00 UTC; 2:00 a.m. CT), with version 1.2.1. The seller of record there is HOOLAI GAME LIMITED. The [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wanda.transformers.gg.am) uses the same title, lists Wanda Cinemas Games as the developer, and shows "Updated on Nov 12, 2026."

Pocket Gamer describes the game as an auto-battler, or idle strategy RPG, in which Autobots and Decepticons team up against Unicron's Chaos Legion, and it says Wanda Cinema Games is behind the release. The App Store description also frames it as a "Strategy Idle RPG" with "an original story by Simon Furman."

## Poppy Playtime Chapter 5: Broken Things

[Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/poppy-playtime-chapter-5/out-now/) also wrote on November 12 that "Poppy Playtime Chapter Five has just landed on iOS and Android." The official stores show the mobile edition was already listed before that date. The [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/poppy-playtime-chapter-5/id6788134172), from Mob Entertainment, Inc, is titled Poppy Playtime Chapter 5, is priced at $6.99, and gives a release date of November 6, 2026 (08:00 UTC; 2:00 a.m. CT), version 1.0. The [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mobentertainment.PoppyPlaytimeChapter5), also from Mob Entertainment, uses the same title, lists $6.99, and shows "Updated on Oct 27, 2026."

The App Store and Google Play descriptions present Chapter 5 as the official continuation of the series, sending players into "a realm ruled by The Prototype" while "Huggy Wuggy is hunting you once again." Pocket Gamer uses the Chapter Five / Broken Things naming and describes a new area controlled by The Prototype, with new GrabPack tools and environmental puzzles.

The store listings confirm both games are available on iOS and Android. They do not support treating November 12 as the first mobile ship date for Poppy Playtime Chapter 5.
