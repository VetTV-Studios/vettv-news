The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission declared effective Trump Media & Technology Group Corp.'s Form S-4 registration statement for its proposed all-stock merger with TAE Technologies, Inc. on **November 5, 2026**, the companies said in a [joint release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/05/3397116/0/en/trump-media-technology-group-and-tae-technologies-announce-effectiveness-of-form-s-4-registration-statement-for-proposed-merger.html) also posted on [TAE's site](https://tae.com/trump-media-technology-group-and-tae-technologies-announce-effectiveness-of-form-s-4-registration-statement-for-proposed-merger/).

TMTG (Nasdaq and NYSE Texas: **DJT**) said it has filed a definitive proxy statement/prospectus and consent solicitation statement. A virtual special meeting of TMTG shareholders is set for **December 14, 2026, at 9:15 a.m. ET**. Shareholders of record as of the close of business on **November 2, 2026**, may vote. TMTG expects to begin mailing the proxy materials on or about **November 6**. TAE plans to seek approval from its stockholders by written consent.

The companies say the transaction is expected to close by the end of 2026, **subject to** the required shareholder and stockholder approvals and satisfaction or waiver of other closing conditions. The merger has **not** closed.

## Deal background

TMTG and TAE signed an Agreement and Plan of Merger on **December 18, 2025**. Under the deal, a TMTG subsidiary would merge with TAE, and TAE would survive as a wholly owned subsidiary of TMTG, according to TMTG's Form S-4 filings on [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000143774926031534/djt20260922_s4.htm) (initial S-4, Sept. 30, 2026; File No. **333-299201**), later amended ([Amendment No. 1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000143774926033844/djt20261026_s4a.htm), Oct. 29; [Amendment No. 2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000143774926034438/djt20261029_s4a.htm), Nov. 2).

When the companies [first announced the S-4 filing](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/30/3371872/0/en/trump-media-technology-group-and-tae-technologies-file-form-s-4-registration-statement-for-proposed-merger.html) on Sept. 30, they said each side would own about **50%** of the combined company on a **fully diluted** basis after closing. Amendment No. 2 to the S-4 states that, assuming no conversions of TMTG convertible notes, pre-merger TMTG shareholders are expected to own about **49.5%** and pre-merger TAE stockholders about **49.5%** on a fully diluted basis (with different percentages of issued and outstanding shares). Treat those figures as company/SEC-document expectations, not a closed outcome.

## What the companies say TAE does

Company materials describe TAE, founded in 1998, as working across three units: **Fusion**; **Power Solutions** (including TAE PowerFabric for AI-related power management); and **Life Sciences** (boron neutron capture therapy, or BNCT). Forward-looking statements in the releases also refer to a **Da Vinci** prototype power plant. Those are **company descriptions and forward-looking statements**, not independent verification that commercial fusion power is operating.

Interim TMTG CEO **Kevin McGurn** said in the Nov. 5 release that with the S-4 effective, the company looks forward to a shareholder vote and "expect[s] to close by the end of the year." TAE CEO and CTO **Michl Binderbauer** said the merger "supports the next phase" of commercializing technologies from nearly three decades of fusion work.

## What still has to happen

- TMTG shareholders must approve merger-related proposals (including a charter amendment increasing authorized shares and the share issuance), per the S-4/A materials
- TAE stockholders must approve via written consent
- Other closing conditions must be met or waived
- The proxy warns the deal may not close on the expected timeline, or at all
