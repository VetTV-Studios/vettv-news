**Secondary report; primary confirmation outstanding.** OC3D reports that MSI is targeting **December 2026** for a graphics-driver update bringing **AMD FSR 4.1 Upscaling** to the **Claw A8 BZ2EM**, subject to stability testing and validation.

Mark Campbell's November 6 article names **MSI Forums** as its source. VetTV News has **not located and verified the original forum statement**. The schedule is therefore presented as OC3D's reporting—not as a directly authenticated MSI announcement.

## A target, not a released driver

OC3D describes the planned update as manufacturer-distributed and conditional on validation. A December target should not be read as a guaranteed release day, and the report does not establish that the driver is already available.

The draft makes no claim that support has shipped, that every game will benefit, or that a specific framerate or image-quality gain has been measured on this handheld.

## What still needs verification

Before this can be upgraded from an attributed report to a directly confirmed MSI story, the original statement needs to establish the speaker's role, exact wording, model scope, intended release window, and validation condition. A forum's MSI branding alone would not prove that a statement came from MSI staff rather than a community member.

No driver version or exact release date is confirmed here. Comparative claims about other handhelds, broad AMD driver-support rules, and promised performance gains are omitted because they are not necessary to report the attributed target.

MSI's original statement couldn't be located; the December target is OverClock3D's report and hasn't been independently confirmed.
