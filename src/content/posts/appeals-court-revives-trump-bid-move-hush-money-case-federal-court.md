A federal appeals court on Monday, November 23, gave President Donald Trump a new opening to move his New York hush money case into federal court, where he wants to argue that presidential immunity should wipe out his 2024 conviction.

In a [two-page order](https://www.courthousenews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/second-circuit-order-trump-hush-money.pdf), a panel of the 2nd U.S. Circuit Court of Appeals vacated an August 28 ruling by U.S. District Judge Alvin Hellerstein that had refused to let Trump file a second notice of removal. The panel sent the case back to the Southern District of New York "with instructions to GRANT" Trump's motion for leave to file that notice.

The order does not overturn the verdict and does not decide whether immunity applies. The panel said a full opinion explaining its reasoning "will follow in due course."

## What the order says

The panel, Chief Judge Raymond J. Lohier Jr. and Judges John M. Walker Jr. and José A. Cabranes, found two errors in Hellerstein's ruling, according to the [order](https://www.courthousenews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/second-circuit-order-trump-hush-money.pdf):

- **Removal after conviction:** Hellerstein "erred in holding that removal is categorically unavailable in a state criminal case after a federal officer defendant has been convicted."
- **Good cause:** He also erred in finding that Trump had not shown "good cause" for a late removal request, the standard the 2nd Circuit set out in its [November 2025 opinion](https://www.courtlistener.com/opinion/10731808/new-york-v-trump/) in the same case.

The order also directs the district court to notify the New York Supreme Court and its Appellate Division once Trump files his removal motion. "In order to preserve the status quo," the district court is to "respectfully request that no further proceedings occur in the state courts while the removal question is pending." The district court must then "promptly issue a decision," and if it allows removal, it "shall adopt the state court judgment."

[NBC News reported](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/donald-trump/appeals-court-gives-another-chance-move-hush-money-case-federal-court-rcna610988) that adopting the state judgment would let Trump ask the 2nd Circuit itself to throw out the conviction.

## How the case got here

- **May 2024:** A Manhattan jury convicted Trump on 34 felony counts of falsifying business records tied to a $130,000 payment to adult film actor Stormy Daniels before the 2016 election, [Courthouse News](https://www.courthousenews.com/trump-effort-to-toss-hush-money-conviction-gets-new-life/) and [Al Jazeera and the AP](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/appeals-court-renews-trump-bid-for-federal-jurisdiction-in-hush-money-case) reported. Trump has denied wrongdoing.
- **2024:** After the verdict, the U.S. Supreme Court ruled in *Trump v. United States* that presidents have immunity for official acts. Trump then asked to file a second, late notice of removal, which Hellerstein denied, according to the [2nd Circuit's 2025 opinion](https://www.courtlistener.com/opinion/10731808/new-york-v-trump/).
- **January 2025:** Trump was sentenced to an unconditional discharge, which leaves the conviction on his record with no further penalty, [NBC News reported](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/donald-trump/appeals-court-gives-another-chance-move-hush-money-case-federal-court-rcna610988).
- **November 6, 2025:** The 2nd Circuit vacated Hellerstein's denial and told him to take a closer look, including at whether evidence admitted at trial related to immunized official acts, according to the [opinion](https://www.courtlistener.com/opinion/10731808/new-york-v-trump/).
- **August 28, 2026:** Hellerstein denied Trump's request again, the ruling the panel has now vacated, according to the [order](https://www.courthousenews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/second-circuit-order-trump-hush-money.pdf).
- **October 28, 2026:** Hellerstein died at 92, the [Southern District of New York announced](https://www.nysd.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/2026-10/Press%20Advisory%20102826_1.pdf).

It is not yet clear which judge will take over. NBC News [reported](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/donald-trump/appeals-court-gives-another-chance-move-hush-money-case-federal-court-rcna610988) that the case had not been reassigned as of Monday. The Southern District docket on [CourtListener](https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/67326478/people-of-the-state-of-new-york-v-trump/), last updated early Tuesday, still listed Hellerstein as the assigned judge.

## What each side says

Trump's lawyers argue that the prosecution relied on evidence about official acts he took as president, and that the case belongs in federal court under the Supreme Court's immunity ruling. "This case involves important questions of presidential evidentiary immunity that arose out of the Supreme Court's landmark decision in Trump v. United States," Trump attorney Robert Giuffra said at last week's argument, [Courthouse News reported](https://www.courthousenews.com/trump-effort-to-toss-hush-money-conviction-gets-new-life/).

A spokesperson for Trump's legal team praised the panel's "prompt and correct ruling" in a statement [quoted by NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/donald-trump/appeals-court-gives-another-chance-move-hush-money-case-federal-court-rcna610988), adding: "President Trump will keep defeating the Liberal Hoaxes at every turn."

The Manhattan District Attorney's office, which brought the case, declined to comment on Monday's ruling, [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/donald-trump/appeals-court-gives-another-chance-move-hush-money-case-federal-court-rcna610988) and [Courthouse News](https://www.courthousenews.com/trump-effort-to-toss-hush-money-conviction-gets-new-life/) reported. At last week's argument, Steven Wu of the district attorney's office told the panel that Trump was prosecuted for falsifying business records, not for official presidential conduct, and that challenges to the conviction should go through the state courts, [according to NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/donald-trump/appeals-court-gives-another-chance-move-hush-money-case-federal-court-rcna610988).

## What happens next

Trump's separate appeal in New York's Appellate Division, which heard arguments earlier this month and has not ruled, is now effectively on hold, [Al Jazeera and the AP](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/appeals-court-renews-trump-bid-for-federal-jurisdiction-in-hush-money-case) and [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/donald-trump/appeals-court-gives-another-chance-move-hush-money-case-federal-court-rcna610988) reported. Once a new federal judge is assigned and Trump files his removal motion, that judge will decide whether the case moves to federal court. The [order](https://www.courthousenews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/second-circuit-order-trump-hush-money.pdf) sets no deadline beyond "promptly."

The 2nd Circuit's written opinion is still to come, and the time for either side to seek further review in the 2nd Circuit starts only once that opinion is issued, the order says. The [2nd Circuit docket](https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/74723882/people-of-the-state-of-new-york-v-trump/) showed no opinion as of Tuesday.
