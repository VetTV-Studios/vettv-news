The FBI is investigating an apparent breach of its National Crime Information Center (NCIC), the nationwide database that police and other criminal justice agencies use to check for warrants, wanted people and criminal records, according to reports from [CNN](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/world/2722874/the-fbi-was-hacked-again-this-time-the-breach-targeted-its-crime-database) and [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/justice-department/fbi-investigating-potential-breach-crime-database-rcna610536).

**What is confirmed so far is limited.** Both outlets cite unnamed sources: CNN cites two people briefed on the investigation, and NBC News cites two people briefed on the matter. The FBI has not publicly confirmed the incident. NBC News reported that the bureau had no comment as of Friday night, and we found no statement about it from the FBI or its director on X through Saturday morning. VetTV News has not independently confirmed the breach.

## What's been reported

- **The investigation:** the FBI is probing an apparent intrusion into NCIC by apparent hackers, CNN reported Friday evening. [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/justice-department/fbi-investigating-potential-breach-crime-database-rcna610536) reported the same later that night. Fox News [posted the news on X](https://x.com/FoxNews/status/2124152725722059007) at 8:47 a.m. CT Saturday and aired a [video report](https://www.foxnews.com/video/6408700392112) describing it as a reported cyberattack.
- **When it was found:** CNN reported that the intrusion was discovered "in recent days" and that bureau officials had learned of it by Thursday, November 19. It has not been reported when the intrusion actually happened.
- **What is unknown:** it has not been reported who was responsible, how they got in, or whether any records were viewed, copied or changed. [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/fbi-reportedly-hacked-again-this-time-targeting-nationwide-police-database/) notes that it is also unclear whether the incident affected NCIC's availability to police. No outage of the system has been reported.
- **The FBI's response:** the bureau did not respond to requests for comment from CNN, [Reuters](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/21/fbis-criminal-information-database-hacked-cnn-reports.html) or BleepingComputer, according to those outlets.
- **The second in months:** CNN, NBC News and Reuters describe it as the second reported intrusion involving an FBI system since September, when the extortion group ShinyHunters claimed to have taken FBI employee data through a third-party platform tied to the bureau's jobs website. No one has publicly linked the two incidents.

## Why NCIC matters

According to the [FBI](https://le.fbi.gov/informational-tools/ncic), NCIC is an information system holding records contributed by, and available to, criminal justice agencies nationwide. The FBI says it has 22 files with more than 18 million active records as of 2023, handles millions of transactions a day, and serves more than 100,000 criminal justice and law enforcement partners. Its best-known use, the FBI says, is by officers during routine traffic stops.

A former FBI official who led investigations into cybercriminal groups told CNN that the bigger worry isn't only whether hackers saw sensitive information, "but whether they could compromise the accuracy of the information that officers rely on."

## The timing

CNN reported that FBI Director Kash Patel appeared on Fox News on Thursday, after bureau officials had learned of the incident, to promote the FBI's cybersecurity work under Operation Winter Shield, an effort CNN says was completed months ago. "Other administrations could have made these updates, but they didn't," Patel said, according to CNN. "We are staying ahead of the curve and making up for lost time."

The reported breach also follows a week of high-profile tech problems. NBC News reported that the U.S. Department of Agriculture told employees on Thursday that a system it uses to pay farmers had been targeted in a cyberattack, and that a classified National Security Agency network suffered a multi-day outage that sources said showed no sign of a cyberattack. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/nov/21/usda-fbi-outages-cyber-attack) and NBC News both note that it is not clear whether any of these incidents are related.
