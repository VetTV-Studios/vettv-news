A Goofy Movie is reportedly set to air on Disney Channel on **Wednesday, November 25 at 6:30 PM ET (5:30 PM CT)**, according to the fan accounts [The Disney Beat (@DisneyBeat101)](https://x.com/DisneyBeat101/status/2118174255250804948) and [ToonHive](https://x.com/ToonHive/status/2118181863168647464) on X.

![Goofy and Max fishing in A Goofy Movie](/images/goofy-movie-disney-channel-nov-25-reportedly/image-1.png)
*Image: A Goofy Movie (1995), Disney*

## What's confirmed and what isn't

**Disney hasn't announced or confirmed the airing.** Pirates & Princesses (secondary) [reported](https://news.piratesandprincesses.net/p/a-goofy-movie-reportedly-returns) on November 5 that Disney hasn't announced it and that TV listings don't show it yet. Most guides only list about two weeks ahead, so a November 25 slot wouldn't appear yet anyway. We searched Disney's TV press site and Disney's official X accounts and found nothing.

The Disney Beat lists the time as 6:30 PM "ET/PT," so West Coast viewers may get it at 6:30 PM local time. Check your local guide closer to the date. The account also [listed](https://x.com/DisneyBeat101/status/2118190933225128141) A Goofy Movie among Disney Channel's November titles.

## The "first since 2010" claim

The Disney Beat says this would be the movie's "first airing" on the channel "in 16 years," with the last one on March 7, 2010. That's the fan account's claim, and we haven't been able to verify it independently. ToonHive's post repeats it and credits The Disney Beat.

## Watch it now

You don't have to wait for cable: [A Goofy Movie is streaming on Disney+](https://www.disneyplus.com/browse/entity-dc9c2716-f06a-498d-b689-66f6bf70480d) (1h 22m, per the Disney+ listing). Disney's [movie page](https://movies.disney.com/a-goofy-movie) lists the release date as April 7, 1995, with a G rating.

*Opinion:* A Goofy Movie is one of Disney's great cult favorites, and "I2I" on a Thanksgiving-eve broadcast would be a fun bit of timing. Until Disney Channel lists it, though, treat it as reported, not confirmed.
