Grand Theft Auto VI has a 99 from critics on Metacritic. Players have it at about a 5. That gap is worth unpacking.

Vet [posted the split on X](https://x.com/The_VetX/status/2123937470479786116) as @The_VetX at 6:32 p.m. CT on November 20, 2026, with four Metacritic screenshots. We went back to Metacritic to check the numbers and to read what players are actually saying.

## The numbers

Vet's screenshots, taken shortly before he posted, showed:

- **PS5:** a 99 Metascore, with "Showing 159 Critic Reviews," and a 5.4 user score ("Mixed or Average"), with "Showing 9,413 User Reviews."
- **Xbox Series X:** a 99 Metascore from 14 critic reviews, and a 5.0 user score ("Mixed or Average").

When we checked Metacritic's [PS5 critic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi/critic-reviews/?platform=playstation-5), [Xbox Series X critic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi/critic-reviews/?platform=xbox-series-x), [PS5 user](https://www.metacritic.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi/user-reviews/?platform=playstation-5) and [Xbox Series X user](https://www.metacritic.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi/user-reviews/?platform=xbox-series-x) pages at 6:46 p.m. CT on Friday, November 20, 2026, they listed:

- **PS5 critics:** 99, "Universal Acclaim," with 159 critic reviews listed. Metacritic's positive, mixed and negative breakdown counts 152 of them, all positive.
- **Xbox Series X critics:** 99, "Universal Acclaim," from 14 critic reviews, all positive.
- **PS5 users:** 5.4, "Mixed or Average," with 9,491 written user reviews. The ratings split 46% positive, 8% mixed and 46% negative. The [main game page](https://www.metacritic.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi/) says the user score is based on 22,422 user ratings; Metacritic counts score-only ratings separately from written reviews.
- **Xbox Series X users:** 4.9, now "Generally Unfavorable," with 935 written user reviews. The ratings break down as 558 positive (40%), 122 mixed (9%) and 729 negative (52%).

So the Xbox Series X user score has slipped from 5.0 to 4.9 since Vet's screenshot. These numbers are moving by the hour.

## Why players only got a say on Friday

The user scores are brand-new. Metacritic's own [support FAQ](https://metacritichelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/16147081687959-As-a-Metacritic-User-when-can-I-submit-a-rating-or-review-for-games-movies-tv-shows-or-albums) says that for games, users can post "starting the day following the game's release date - specifically, 36 hours following the official release of the game." Metacritic added that wait in 2020 "to ensure our gamers have time to play these games before writing their reviews," a spokesperson told [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/metacritic-will-now-make-users-wait-36-hours-before-pos-1844421321) at the time.

GTA 6 has already needed moderation. [VGC reported](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/metacritic-has-already-had-to-remove-fake-gta-6-user-reviews/) on November 17 that Metacritic removed fake GTA 6 user reviews posted before launch and blocked new ones. Once user scores opened on November 20, [TheGamer reported](https://www.thegamer.com/grand-theft-auto-6-gta-review-bombing-user-score/) a flood of negative reviews, "many of which are from players who admit they 'haven't even played the game' themselves," along with "politically charged reviews."

## Why the Xbox critic count is so small

It isn't a different release date. Rockstar [set the launch for November 19, 2026](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak3ak31a49a221/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-set-to-launch-november-19-2026), and the official U.S. PlayStation Store and Microsoft Store listings unlocked at the same moment, as [we reported on launch night](/posts/gta-6-out-now-ps5-xbox-series-launch/).

The likely reason is review access. Germany's [GameStar](https://www.gamestar.de/artikel/gta-6-test-review-spoilerfrei,3463175,wertung.html) wrote in its review that Rockstar provided it "genau einen Review-Key für Playstation 5" (exactly one review key for PlayStation 5) and that "Ein Review-Key für die Xbox Series X/S stand uns nicht zur Verfügung" (a review key for Xbox Series X/S was not available to us). Some outlets did review it on Xbox, including [Pure Xbox](https://www.purexbox.com/reviews/xbox-series-x/grand-theft-auto-6), which gave it a 10. Rockstar hasn't said publicly how it handed out review copies, so we're not repeating unconfirmed claims about why.

## What players are complaining about

We read through several hundred of the user reviews Metacritic showed on its PS5 and Xbox Series X pages on Friday evening. The same complaints come up again and again. Quotes are word for word from Metacritic.

**30fps and frame drops.** This is the biggest one. "It is capped at an unstable 30 FPS, with constant frame-rate drops in densely populated areas and an alarming number of bugs that break missions," wrote Randall_89, who scored it a 1 on PS5. It's a real trade-off. [Digital Foundry](https://www.digitalfoundry.net/features/gta-6-ps5-vs-ps5-pro-xbox-series-x-and-series-s-which-console-plays-best) found that every console version targets 30fps with no 40fps or 60fps mode. The base PS5 and Xbox Series X dip during intense moments, down into the mid-20s with heavy explosions, while the PS5 Pro held a consistent 30fps through most of its testing. As [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/gta-6-base-ps5-performance-suffers-minor-and-sustained-frame-rate-drops-but-its-unwavering-on-ps5-pro) summarized it, that's "far from unplayable, but it's certainly not ideal." Rockstar told Kotaku that no 60fps or 40fps mode is coming to the launch consoles, [Engadget reported](https://www.engadget.com/2321134/rockstar-just-settled-a-major-question-about-gta-6s-controversial-fps-decision/), because the CPU is "not powerful enough to run the game's complex simulation at higher frame rates."

**Bugs.** "The game is really buggy especially for a AAA game that was delayed, and cost about $100," wrote BlissfulAce77 on PS5. For context, Take-Two [priced](https://taketwointeractivesoftwareinc.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi/) the Standard Edition at $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition at $99.99, as covered in our [launch-week preview](/posts/gta-6-launch-week-preview-countdown/).

**Price.** Some reviewers outside the U.S. frame it in local terms: "In Brazil, GTA VI costs almost a third of the minimum monthly wage," wrote mafas on PS5.

**Story, characters and "not a GTA game."** Many negative reviews take aim at the writing, at leads Jason and Lucia, and at relationship and everyday-life mechanics that some players say don't feel like GTA. "Not worth buying this game. Terrible fps. The writing is bad," wrote xjamesmode, who scored it a 3.

**Review bombing and politics.** Some zero and one scores are culture-war complaints rather than reviews of how the game plays, which matches what TheGamer described. A few reviewers also claim, without evidence, that critics were paid. We've seen nothing to support that, and we're not repeating it as fact.

## The other side: players who love it

46% of PS5 ratings are positive, and many of those reviews push back hard. "The strongly negative reviews are very obviously review bombing. That being said the claims of perfection are similarly disingenuous," wrote frankpaulr, who gave it an 8 on Xbox Series X. Others shrug off the frame rate: "I wish it wasn't a 30fps lock, but it's not really bothered me any," wrote AlionaG3n, who gave it an 8 on PS5.

And some land in the middle. "Genuinely, this is not a bad game or a perfect game," wrote Ballistyx, who gave it a 6 on Xbox Series X.

## Our take

*This section is opinion.* A 99 and a 5 on the same game should make anyone stop and look closer, and that's the point of calling it out.

Here's how I'd read it. The 99 is real. Critics had the game for weeks before launch (GameStar says it got its PS5 key on October 26), and every one of the 152 scored PS5 reviews Metacritic counts is positive. Read our [critic review roundup](/posts/gta-6-reviews-roundup-metacritic-99/) and you'll see they also flagged the 30fps cap. But a 99 doesn't mean flawless, and buyers should know that going in, especially on a base PS5 or Xbox Series X.

The 5 is real too, but it's noisy. Some of it is honest frustration about performance, bugs, price and a story that isn't for everyone. Some of it is people who haven't played the game. On Metacritic, a written review from someone with 60 hours and a zero from someone with none count the same.

What should buyers do? Ignore both headline numbers and read the reviews in the middle, the 6s, 7s and 8s, from both critics and players. If 30fps is a dealbreaker, that's the deal: there's no performance mode, and Rockstar says one isn't coming on these consoles. If it isn't, there's a reason critics, as TheGamer put it, "repeatedly described" it as one of the best games ever made.

The gap is the story. Both scores need context, and neither one should be the only thing you read before spending $80 to $100.
