The official [Games Press release](https://www.gamespress.com/Winners-Announced-at-the-44th-Golden-Joystick-Awards-Presented-by-Pot-) dated **November 11, 2026**, announces the winners of the **44th Golden Joystick Awards**, "revealed tonight in a glittering ceremony in London" and live-streamed on YouTube, Twitch, Steam, TikTok, X, and more.

The full winners list includes: **Best Gaming Hardware - Steam Controller (public vote).**

The release says the event features **25 categories**, **22 of which are voted for by the global gaming public**, plus three critically chosen awards: Streamers' Choice, Critics' Choice, and a new Developers' Choice Award. It also describes the Golden Joystick Awards, first held in 1983, as "the world's longest-running publicly-voted gaming awards."

The Games Press primary does not state a vote percentage for the hardware category.

*Opinion:* a public-vote hardware award is a nice marker for the revived Steam Controller; it is still just a fan poll, not a lab test.
