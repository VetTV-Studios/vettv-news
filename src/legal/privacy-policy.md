# Privacy Policy

*Last updated November 18, 2026*

VetTV News ("we," "us") is published by VetTV Studios. This page explains what the news site at [news.vettvstudios.com](https://news.vettvstudios.com/) collects and how that information is used. For the company-wide policy that covers VetTV Studios' other sites and products, see [vettvstudios.com/privacy](https://www.vettvstudios.com/privacy).

## Third-party scripts on this site

Every page includes Google's Subscribe with Google publisher script, [news.google.com/swg/js/v1/publisher.js](https://news.google.com/swg/js/v1/publisher.js). It powers the "preferred source" button in the footer. When that button initializes, the page also loads:

- a Google frame from `news.google.com/swg/ui`
- Subscribe with Google interface code and styles from `www.gstatic.com`
- a request to `play.google.com/log`

Google may receive technical data when these scripts and requests run, such as your IP address and the page you are visiting. Google describes how it uses data from sites that partner with it in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and in [How Google uses cookies in advertising](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). You can review or change ad personalization in [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/) or [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/).

In a November 18, 2026 review of the home page, an article page and a section page, the site did not set cookies and did not write to first-party local storage or session storage. The review did not find Google AdSense (`adsbygoogle` / `googlesyndication`), Google Analytics, a Facebook/Meta pixel, Vercel Analytics, Vercel Speed Insights, or third-party webfonts loading. Pages include a Facebook domain-verification tag in the HTML; that tag did not load Facebook scripts or cookies in the review.

## Newsletter signups

Newsletter signups are not open on the live site yet (the form currently says signups open soon and does not send your address anywhere). When signups are turned on, your email address will go to our newsletter provider, [Kit](https://kit.com/privacy), which will store it and send the newsletter. We will use it only to send that newsletter. Every issue will include a one-click unsubscribe link, and you'll get a confirmation email first.

## Email you send us

If you write to us, we keep your message and email address only to reply and follow up.

## Hosting

The site is a static site hosted on Vercel. Vercel may log basic technical data, such as IP addresses, to run and secure its service, under [Vercel's privacy policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## Search, links and embeds

On-site search, when you use it, is provided by Pagefind files hosted on this site. Articles and pages also link to other websites. Those sites' privacy practices are their own.

## Your choices

- For Google's ad personalization, use [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/) or [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/).
- To unsubscribe from the newsletter once it is running, use the link in any issue.
- To ask us to delete an email or message you sent us, write to [vet@vettvstudios.com](mailto:vet@vettvstudios.com).

## Children

The site isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.
